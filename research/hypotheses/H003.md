# H003: Continuum rank-change obstruction

## Question addressed

research/QUESTION.md

## Statement

Under locality, Lorentz covariance, finite action, and a finite predictive set of transition data, exact open-set rank change between d=4 and d=5 cannot be described by either an intrinsic or resolution-based continuum first-order action without a fatal ambiguity, extra physical fiber data, constraint failure, ghost, or loss of hyperbolicity; a discrete/emergent or permanently fixed-dimensional ontology is required.

## Motivation

Changing the rank of the kinetic/soldering structure can change the number and type of constraints, while exact quotienting may erase canonical data. Hyperbolicity, a differentiable action, and deterministic transfer of data across such a locus need not survive. This hypothesis is the serious null alternative to H001 and H002: the initiating geometric picture may be kinematically definable but dynamically impossible under the stated continuum assumptions.

## Assumptions

- Dynamics is local in a finite jet of continuum fields and has finitely many field species and transition data.
- Lorentz/diffeomorphism covariance, finite action, and deterministic evolution (up to gauge) are required.
- Exact open-set dimension change removes rather than merely shrinks a spacelike direction.
- No external preferred foliation, nonlocal matching oracle, or permanent physical five-dimensional ambient bulk is allowed.
- The obstruction claim is restricted to this assumption set; it does not rule out discrete, nonlocal, emergent, or fixed-dimensional completions.

## Predictions

- Every candidate intrinsic action fails to define at least one of: differentiable variation, conserved symplectic evolution, finite predictive transition data, or a healthy hyperbolic reduced system.
- Every resolution action either retains observable fifth-direction information in the exterior or develops a singular/changing constraint structure at rank change.
- Apparent successful toy models evade the issue by using a fixed-dimensional ambient theory, imposing matching conditions by hand, analyzing only static energy, or allowing merely small rather than absent fibers.
- A viable theory, if any, must relax at least one assumption—most plausibly continuum locality or fundamental exact dimension—and use discrete/emergent dimensionality.

## What would support it

- A general constraint/principal-symbol obstruction covering the lowest-derivative first-order action class.
- The same fatal transition mode in independent intrinsic and resolution formulations after convention alignment.
- Proof that quotienting a canonical direction necessarily destroys or leaves observable symplectic data under the stated assumptions.

## What would falsify it

- One fully specified continuum model satisfying all assumptions that passes F0-A through F0-C: resolution control, complete variation, closed constraints, healthy kinetic signs, and a well-posed linearized transition problem.
- A rigorous theorem providing deterministic symplectic evolution across controlled `4/5` rank change with no hidden permanent dimension.

## Discriminating experiments/derivations

1. Derive the presymplectic form and constraint rank on both sides of the simplest circle-collapse transition; test whether the change can be accounted for entirely by regular first-class gauge generators.
2. Freeze coefficients near the transition and classify the principal symbol as the collapsing radius tends to zero. Loss of strong hyperbolicity that cannot be repaired by gauge or admissible interface data supports H003.
3. Compare quotient-identical resolutions for residual charges/holonomies. A nonremovable difference supports H003 against H002 but does not alone exclude H001.
4. Search the literature for theorem-level constructions or no-go results with the same notions of exact dimension and predictivity; neighboring degenerate-metric examples are not decisive.

## Current evidence

- Warning evidence, not a no-go: an explicit degenerate-tetrad sector is underdetermined before canonical reduction; a distinct signature-change model is ill posed despite self-adjoint interface matching; and a smooth circle pinch can mediate nonperturbative vacuum decay. H003 now splits into a supported restricted form (no canonical bare-quotient dynamics) and an unproven strong form (no admissible continuum theory at all). The decisive open test is whether the enriched edge sector is regular zero-charge first-class gauge or carries physical boundary degrees of freedom.
- `D006`/C016 supplied a serious interface warning but not a no-go. `W001-total-gauss` and C017 showed that the apparent interface Lorentz charge cancels the exterior Palatini flux on the matching shell, while the regular 5D core contributes no hidden flux.
