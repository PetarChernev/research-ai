# H002: Resolution-based gauge-equivalent dynamics

## Question addressed

research/QUESTION.md

## Statement

A smooth five-dimensional resolution with a rank-varying coframe can provide a consistent local covariant theory, and an explicit gauge/equivalence relation removes all exterior fiber data so that its physical observables reproduce an exactly four-dimensional quotient even if no autonomous intrinsic stratified action is available.

## Motivation

A smooth resolution provides standard differential forms, connections, and integration, while polynomial first-order gravity tolerates noninvertible coframes algebraically. The central extra requirement is a genuine gauge/equivalence relation that makes exterior fiber labels unphysical. Unlike H001, the resolution calculus may be indispensable; unlike a permanent Kaluza–Klein model, exterior fifth-direction observables must be removed exactly. H003 predicts that this removal or the dynamics will fail.

## Assumptions

- A smooth five-dimensional resolution with a controlled circle action exists for every configuration in the initial sector.
- Rank-five and rank-four coframe regions can coexist while connection and action variables remain well defined.
- The physical equivalence group can identify different resolutions and all fields along a collapsed exterior fiber without identifying distinct four-dimensional physics.
- The gauge generators remain regular and their algebra closes when the coframe rank changes.
- Boundary conditions remove, gauge, or constrain any exterior holonomy and connection components associated with the collapsed direction.
- Exactly one timelike direction and a timelike transition are retained.

## Predictions

- A polynomial five-dimensional resolution action plus explicit constraints/boundary terms has a differentiable variation without using an inverse coframe at rank change.
- Gauge-invariant observables are constant on every declared resolution-equivalence class.
- The reduced exterior phase space is isomorphic, in the tested sector, to that of the chosen four-dimensional theory and has no physical fifth-direction local mode.
- A Hamiltonian analysis identifies first-class generators implementing fiber collapse/resolution equivalence and gives a healthy finite reduced phase space on a simple transition background.
- An autonomous intrinsic action may fail to exist even though intrinsic quotient observables are reproducible after reduction.

## What would support it

- An explicit symplectic reduction from resolution data to quotient data with matching observables for two nontrivially different resolutions.
- A complete constraint algebra whose extra first-class generators remove all collapsed-fiber modes.
- A regular gauge-fixed principal symbol and positive reduced quadratic Hamiltonian across the rank transition.

## What would falsify it

- A gauge-invariant exterior observable (local mode, holonomy, charge, or symplectic flux) that depends on the nominally collapsed fiber or the chosen resolution.
- Rank change turns a required gauge generator singular or second class in a way that leaves an extra ghost or arbitrary function.
- Equivalent resolutions produce different transition equations, amplitudes, or conserved charges.
- No differentiable local action can impose exact exterior collapse without adding a physical fixed-dimensional bulk.

## Discriminating experiments/derivations

1. Construct the smallest resolved cylinder/polar-collapse model and perform presymplectic reduction on the rank-four open set. Residual resolution data falsify the strongest form of H002.
2. Compare two resolutions of one quotient and calculate action, charges, and transition equations. Exact reduced agreement supports H002; intrinsic agreement without resolution machinery favors H001.
3. Perform the full Dirac constraint count and a frozen-coefficient principal-symbol test at and near rank change. A common fatal mode supports H003.

## Current evidence

- Neighboring ingredients only: Hilbert–Palatini equations are polynomial at noninvertible tetrads, but an explicit degenerate sector leaves many connection/torsion components undetermined; a smooth five-dimensional KK circle cap exists, but remains fixed-dimensional and its Ricci-flat saddle action is entirely a surface contribution. Neither supplies H002's gauge-equivalent reduction.
- `D001` shows exactly in a rotor analogue that a Gauss constraint imposed before collapse can yield a benign reduced phase space, whereas accidental gauge enhancement only at zero radius is singular. The gravitational constraint algebra remains uncomputed.
- `D002` disfavors the naive ordinary-gauge version of H002: the jumping collapse relation is not generated by ordinary smooth vertical automorphisms, the two embedded smooth resolutions are canonically inequivalent, and independently retained exterior `F`/mixed curvature survives ordinary gauge. An explicit forgetful kernel removes it only by declaration; whether an action makes that kernel first-class remains open.
