# H002: Resolution-based gauge-equivalent dynamics

## Question addressed

research/QUESTION.md

## Statement

A smooth five-dimensional resolution with a rank-varying coframe can provide a consistent local covariant theory, and an explicit gauge/equivalence relation removes all exterior fiber data so that its physical observables reproduce an exactly four-dimensional quotient even if no autonomous intrinsic stratified action is available.

## Motivation

A smooth resolution provides standard differential forms, connections, and integration, while polynomial first-order gravity tolerates noninvertible coframes algebraically. The central extra requirement is a genuine gauge/equivalence relation that makes exterior fiber labels unphysical. Unlike H001, the resolution calculus may be indispensable; unlike a permanent Kaluza–Klein model, exterior fifth-direction observables must be removed exactly. H003 predicts that this removal or the dynamics will fail.

## Assumptions

- A smooth five-dimensional resolution with a controlled circle action exists for every configuration in the initial sector.
- Rank-five and rank-four coframe regions can coexist while connection and action variables remain well defined.
- The physical equivalence group can identify different resolutions and all fields along a collapsed exterior fiber without identifying distinct four-dimensional physics.
- The gauge generators remain regular and their algebra closes when the coframe rank changes.
- Boundary conditions remove, gauge, or constrain any exterior holonomy and connection components associated with the collapsed direction.
- Exactly one timelike direction and a timelike transition are retained.

## Predictions

- A polynomial five-dimensional resolution action plus explicit constraints/boundary terms has a differentiable variation without using an inverse coframe at rank change.
- Gauge-invariant observables are constant on every declared resolution-equivalence class.
- The reduced exterior phase space is isomorphic, in the tested sector, to that of the chosen four-dimensional theory and has no physical fifth-direction local mode.
- A Hamiltonian analysis identifies first-class generators implementing fiber collapse/resolution equivalence and gives a healthy finite reduced phase space on a simple transition background.
- An autonomous intrinsic action may fail to exist even though intrinsic quotient observables are reproducible after reduction.

## What would support it

- An explicit symplectic reduction from resolution data to quotient data with matching observables for two nontrivially different resolutions.
- A complete constraint algebra whose extra first-class generators remove all collapsed-fiber modes.
- A regular gauge-fixed principal symbol and positive reduced quadratic Hamiltonian across the rank transition.

## What would falsify it

- A gauge-invariant exterior observable (local mode, holonomy, charge, or symplectic flux) that depends on the nominally collapsed fiber or the chosen resolution.
- Rank change turns a required gauge generator singular or second class in a way that leaves an extra ghost or arbitrary function.
- Equivalent resolutions produce different transition equations, amplitudes, or conserved charges.
- No differentiable local action can impose exact exterior collapse without adding a physical fixed-dimensional bulk.

## Discriminating experiments/derivations

1. Construct the smallest resolved cylinder/polar-collapse model and perform presymplectic reduction on the rank-four open set. Residual resolution data falsify the strongest form of H002.
2. Compare two resolutions of one quotient and calculate action, charges, and transition equations. Exact reduced agreement supports H002; intrinsic agreement without resolution machinery favors H001.
3. Perform the full Dirac constraint count and a frozen-coefficient principal-symbol test at and near rank change. A common fatal mode supports H003.

## Current evidence

- Neighboring ingredients only: Hilbert–Palatini equations are polynomial at noninvertible tetrads, but an explicit degenerate sector leaves many connection/torsion components undetermined; a smooth five-dimensional KK circle cap exists, but remains fixed-dimensional and its Ricci-flat saddle action is entirely a surface contribution. Neither supplies H002's gauge-equivalent reduction.
- `D001` shows exactly in a rotor analogue that a Gauss constraint imposed before collapse can yield a benign reduced phase space, whereas accidental gauge enhancement only at zero radius is singular. The gravitational constraint algebra remains uncomputed.
- `D002` disfavors the naive ordinary-gauge version of H002: the jumping collapse relation is not generated by ordinary smooth vertical automorphisms, the two embedded smooth resolutions are canonically inequivalent, and independently retained exterior `F`/mixed curvature survives ordinary gauge. An explicit forgetful kernel removes it only by declaration; whether an action makes that kernel first-class remains open.
- `D004`/C013 falsifies the most economical reduction story: a pure multiplier equating the persistent coframes and connections forces the exterior Palatini momentum `P^-_{ab}` to vanish, which is incompatible with a nondegenerate induced triad.
- `D005`/C015 sharpens this into an exact failure. The fully gauge-covariant, reference-free two-connection action is field-space integrable and independently Lorentz invariant, yet forces `P^-_{ab}=0` and induced coframe rank at most one. A nondegenerate branch exists only after adding a fixed covariantly transforming reference connection, giving `2 pi ell_* / kappa_5 = 1 / kappa_4`. H002 is therefore not viable as pure gauge equivalence; it survives only as an enriched theory with extra boundary data whose charge status is undetermined.
- `D006`/C016 found a rank-ten Stueckelberg shell block and nonzero partial interface moment map but correctly left its total gauge status open. `W001-total-gauss`, C017, and passing O007 then showed that the regular core flux is zero and the exterior Palatini `-P^-` term cancels that interface moment map on the matching shell. This removes the partial charge as a standalone falsification of H002, but it does not support H002: the trace-aware primary stabilization, seed/corner gauge declaration, first-class closure, and quotient rank remain unproved.
- `CY001-trace-primary` and C018-C020 removed an autonomous additive rank-ten block at the defining-primary level in one conditional duplicated-trace presentation, but still required an actual boundary-compatible background before preservation. D018/C021 and passing O009 now show that the smallest source-free static **unwarped** background does not exist in the D002 smooth-flat class: the bulk equations force `Lambda_5=0`, `rho''=0`, while the zero-extension jet forces `rho=0`, even though all D005 shell/orbit equations are compatible with the limiting traces. This is negative evidence for the minimal H002 realization, not for all warped, sourceful, mixed, or alternative-action versions.
- `CY003-warped-background`, D019/C022, and passing O010 exclude the least-radical warped extension as well. For the complete diagonal proper-normal ansatz with one common `2+1` warp, the equations retain both branches of `w'/rho=C` but the smooth-flat endpoint forces `Lambda_5=0`, `w=1`, and then `rho=0`. The D005 shell remains compatible. H002 is therefore unsupported even in this enlarged symmetric source-free benchmark; sourceful, anisotropic, off-diagonal, alternative-action, weaker-regularity, and moving-interface versions remain open.
- `CY004-source-regularity`, D020/C023, its same-model critique, and passing O011 now exclude the ordinary regular-source escape for the two exact D002 profiles within that same common-warp geometry. For every allowed smooth-flat common warp, `rho''/rho` diverges, the required orthonormal stress is unbounded, and both independent normal/circle NEC contractions are eventually negative, although every absolute stress component is locally five-volume integrable. This is further negative evidence for the minimal H002 realization, not a proof against other profiles, torsion/spin, anisotropic or off-diagonal geometry, alternative actions, weaker regularity/topology, or moving interfaces.
- `CY005-profile-independent-endpoint`, D021/C024, and its revised same-model critique close the remaining profile-rate escape for bounded curvature in the torsion-free diagonal proper-normal sector. Every positive `C^1` endpoint/`C^2` punctured radius with `rho(0)=rho'_+(0)=0` has `limsup rho''/rho=+infinity`, so its orthonormal radial-circle curvature is unbounded on every endpoint neighborhood. An explicit smooth-flat oscillatory profile shows that no actual limit or eventual sign follows. This is profile-independent negative evidence for the smooth-flat diagonal realization of H002, but it does not extend C023's stress, NEC, or `L1` conclusions and leaves different regularity/topology, connection dynamics, and genuinely non-diagonal geometry open.
- `CY006-finite-regularity-attachment`, D022/C025, its same-model critique, and passing O012 test the selected nonzero-slope escape without changing the diagonal bulk or D004/D005 action. The punctured `rho=cs` core is flat and all persistent D005 shell equations hold, but a free finite `delta omega^{43}=delta chi^{43}dtheta` trace contributes `+(2pi/kappa_5)delta chi^{43}o_Sigma`; the unchanged persistent shell has no conjugate term, so stationarity kills the intended triad volume. Fixing both slope and mixed holonomy leaves a fixed-boundary candidate but no equation for that datum. This is negative evidence against the unchanged-action finite-slope realization of H002, not against a covariant mixed completion or a different admissible tangent category.
- `CY007-mixed-polarization-completion`, D023/C026, its same-model critique, and passing O013 test one standard full `SO(1,4)` tensorial-difference primitive rather than another geometry. The primitive exactly cancels the C025 `43` row, but its complete variation only changes polarization: a fixed reference anchors `c` externally, an unrestricted reference or seed forces boundary rank at most two, and a fixed-seed orbit fixes the holonomy conjugacy class while adding an unclassified edge/corner sector. This is negative evidence against this one enriched H002 repair, not against every mixed action. It also shows that honest covariance of the total interface problem requires an explicit normal/reduction field or full mixed extension beyond frozen D005.

## Open problems

- Define the enlarged equivalence/gauge group and its action on the connection and transition locus.
- Decide whether the exterior is a quotient phase space, a boundary condition, or a separate stratum.
- Determine whether collapsed-fiber holonomy or torsion survives as physical data.
- Define the minimal covariant boundary normal/reduction field relating core `SO(1,4)` to exterior `SO(1,3)`, and decide whether the finite normal-plane `43` holonomy/tangent is gauge, a fixed superselection datum, or a physical edge variable before proposing another mixed action.
- Define enough of the lower-regularity quotient/trace category to decide whether the free finite mixed tangent used by C025 is physically mandatory, gauge, or excluded by a principled regularity condition rather than by an external boundary choice.
- If a new background survives that test, require an actual covariant source action and source dynamics rather than inferring them from an effective conserved tensor before resuming functional trace preservation.
