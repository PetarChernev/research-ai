# H004: Dynamically selected Hopf fermion

## Question addressed

research/QUESTION.md

## Statement

Conditional on a consistent dimensional theory with a stable Q=1 defect, the inclusion of its admissible Hopf-framed sector preserves the nontrivial Z2 rotation/exchange loop, and reduction at odd opened-fiber momentum induces the same nontrivial quantum-line-bundle holonomy, yielding a lowest j=1/2 multiplet and fermionic exchange without separately choosing an FR character.

## Motivation

Standard Hopf-map sectors have a nontrivial loop topology, and fixed odd right-`U(1)` weight on `SU(2)` gives spinorial transformation and a lowest half-integer representation. The proposed mechanism becomes substantive only if these two facts are tied by the microscopic action on the full defect configuration space. H005 is the direct alternative: topology may merely allow a fermionic choice, not select it.

## Assumptions

- Gates F0 and F1 have passed and define a stable localized `Q=1` defect and admissible one-/two-defect configuration spaces.
- Hopf framing is genuine geometric collapse data and cannot be removed by an allowed gauge transformation.
- Allowed core overlap and rank-changing histories preserve the relevant topological sector.
- A globally meaningful collective right-`U(1)` coordinate or associated principal bundle exists, with integer momentum `k`.
- Integrating out microscopic fields is valid below the first noncollective excitation and includes all Berry/topological terms.

## Predictions

- The inclusion from the charge-one Hopf sector to the full one-defect configuration space maps the Hopf `\mathbb Z_2` loop injectively.
- In the full two-unit-defect space, exchange and a `2\pi` rotation of one defect represent the same nontrivial loop.
- Microscopic reduction at odd `k` constructs a line bundle whose holonomy on that loop is `-1`; no independent FR sign remains to be chosen in the odd sector.
- The lowest collective multiplet has `j=|k|/2`; for `|k|=1` it is a two-state `j=1/2` multiplet, subject to any additional constraints from the full theory.

## What would support it

- A computation of the full configuration spaces and inclusion-induced fundamental-group maps with overlap and all admissible rank events included.
- A microscopic collective-coordinate reduction deriving the Berry connection/transition functions and matching its `-1` holonomy to both loop representatives.
- An independent reconstruction of the topology and quantum bundle using an alternate presentation.

## What would falsify it

- Any admissible path in the full theory contracts the purported nontrivial rotation or exchange loop.
- Rotation and exchange are distinct classes in the relevant physical configuration space.
- Odd fiber momentum gives a spinorial rotor but leaves the FR character as an independent choice.
- The purported fiber coordinate is gauge, ill-defined globally, or lacks a conserved quantized momentum.

## Discriminating experiments/derivations

1. First audit the standard based Hopf-map statements, lift ambiguities, charge-sector dependence, and rotation/exchange claims without using the initiating note as authority.
2. Once admissible generalized geometries are defined, search explicitly for a homotopy through merged cores or additional rank strata that unwinds the loop. Survival favors H004; one contraction supports H005.
3. Derive the collective quantum line bundle from the microscopic symplectic potential at fixed `k`; check whether its holonomy is forced or can be tensored with either flat `\mathbb Z_2` bundle. The latter supports H005.
