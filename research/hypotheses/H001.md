# H001: Intrinsic stratified continuum dynamics

## Question addressed

research/QUESTION.md

## Statement

A genuinely variable-dimensional Lorentzian differential/stratified space admits an intrinsic local first-order variational principle for d in {4,5}, with resolution-independent observables and a provisionally well-posed linearized initial-value problem at controlled circle-collapse transitions.

## Motivation

The proposed ontology says that physical dimension belongs to the generalized space itself, so an intrinsic formulation is the cleanest realization. Differential and stratified spaces suggest that smooth functions, derivations, and incidence data can exist without a fixed-rank tangent bundle. Unlike H002, no five-dimensional resolution may retain physical exterior data. Unlike H003, controlled singular strata are assumed to admit local predictive dynamics.

## Assumptions

- A category can be chosen in which the regular strata, transition control data, smooth functions/forms, integration, and causal curves are simultaneously defined.
- The transition is controlled (for example, locally a resolved spacelike circle collapse) rather than an arbitrary dimension jump.
- Locality can be formulated using stratum-local densities plus finite incidence/transition data.
- Exactly one timelike direction persists and the transition world-volume is timelike in the first model.
- A finite set of admissible variations can vary both geometry and the transition without introducing an unbounded set of matching data.
- The long-wavelength `d=4` phase is permitted to have the Einstein–Cartan/GR action.

## Predictions

- Local dimension and all physical observables can be defined on `X` without selecting a preferred resolution.
- A single differentiable action produces both stratum equations and transition equations; no externally imposed Israel-like matching rule is needed.
- In the exactly `d=4` open region there is no local fifth-direction metric, connection, momentum, or Wilson-line degree of freedom.
- Around at least one regular circle-collapse background, gauge fixing leaves a finite strongly hyperbolic linearized system with no negative-kinetic physical mode.
- On nondegenerate open strata the equations reduce to the declared four- and five-dimensional first-order gravity theories.

## What would support it

- An intrinsic local model with a Stokes/variation calculus and an explicit equivalence to more than one resolution.
- A complete first variation with conserved presymplectic current and stress-energy balance across the transition.
- A Dirac–Bergmann and principal-symbol calculation passing the F0-C criteria on a planar or polar collapse background.

## What would falsify it

- A proof that the needed differential, causal, or integration structures cannot coexist for the allowed `4/5` local models.
- Demonstration that intrinsic observables necessarily require unbounded or resolution-specific transition data.
- An unavoidable nonclosing constraint, ghost, or loss of hyperbolicity for every local intrinsic action satisfying the assumptions.
- Failure to recover ordinary `d=4` and `d=5` gravity away from the transition.

## Discriminating experiments/derivations

1. Define one compact local quotient model and compute its derivation tangent dimensions, differential forms, integration/Stokes data, and admissible morphisms. Compare two inequivalent smooth resolutions. Resolution-independent agreement favors H001; agreement only after adding resolution gauge data favors H002.
2. Vary the lowest-derivative intrinsic first-order action on a planar/polar circle-collapse local model, including transition displacement. A finite closed set of intrinsic equations favors H001; irreducible reliance on resolution variables favors H002 or H003.
3. Compute the linearized principal symbol and constraint algebra. A healthy intrinsic system disfavors H003; a fatal failure common to all candidate intrinsic interface terms supports H003.

## Current evidence

- Neighboring ingredients only: conically smooth stratified spaces provide intrinsic cone-local models, enriched morphisms, tangential classifiers, and functorial unzipping; quotient diffeology provides plotwise forms and a restricted foliation-quotient descent theorem. Neither source supplies the combined Lorentzian, integration, action, or resolution-invariance package, so H001 remains unsupported.
- `D001` derives a necessary canonical diagnostic: collapse is benign only if disappearing directions are regular zero-charge gauge directions and reduced evolution remains deterministic. It does not analyze an intrinsic field theory.
- `D002`/C007–C009 provide partial kinematical support: the bare quotient has an explicit conical local model, a profile-independent final diffeology, and an actual quotient-diffeological form complex. However, the physical dimension notion, integration/Stokes, causality, and action remain absent, so H001 is not promoted.
- `D003`/C010–C011 remove the strongest form of H001: the bare final diffeology supplies **no** canonical measure, stratum weights, or nonzero collapsed-fiber incidence trace, and every regular descended four-form has zero core-boundary trace. An autonomous intrinsic action cannot be read off the quotient calculus alone.
- `D004`/C012 and `D005`/C014–C015 make the consequence concrete: the bare five-dimensional Palatini boundary potential pushes to zero while the four-dimensional one does not, and the natural repair requires a marked circle, an edge isomorphism, and reference data that are not quotient-intrinsic. H001 survives only in a weakened "intrinsic state space plus declared incidence data" form.
