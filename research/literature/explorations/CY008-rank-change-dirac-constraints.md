# Literature exploration: rank-changing Dirac constraints

> **NON-EVIDENTIARY EXPLORATION.** Everything below is a candidate lead based on metadata, abstracts, and structural comparison. It does not establish H002, H003, a constraint count, or any source-supported project claim.

## 1. Bounded question and distinct lens

The bounded question is how to organize a future complete bulk-plus-interface analysis when the primary surface or presymplectic kernel is not constant-rank near exact coframe-rank change. Priority is given to machinery that can expose: constraint bifurcation, irregular constraints, arbitrary multipliers/functions, gauge/second-class conversion, tangency to singular strata, and possible failure of deterministic continuation.

This branch is distinct from searches for a particular reduction-field action, edge-mode quantization, or general degenerate-spacetime phenomenology. It asks what **constraint algorithm and stratification language** should be used once an `SO(1,4)->SO(1,3)` reduction field and complete action exist. Moving interfaces, principal symbols, and degree counting are intentionally outside this first pass.

Project anchors were: six surviving Palatini primary null combinations; `Phi_u`; six `p_g` nulls; the finite mixed `43` tangent/holonomy; a future reduction field; the clean-shell assumption; the absent functional trace adjoint; and the need to preserve every surviving primary without an inverse coframe. The structural seed is the equation `i_X omega = dH` (or its field-theory analogue) when `rank(omega)`, the Legendre image, or the Jacobian of constraints changes.

## 2. Vocabulary/history map and search trail

Vocabulary branches:

- **Dirac side:** constraint bifurcation; field activation; nonlinear constraint effects; irregular/functionally dependent constraints; ineffective constraints; linearization instability; rank of the primary Poisson matrix; variable number of degrees of freedom.
- **Geometric side:** Gotay-Nester-Hinds (GNH) algorithm; final constraint submanifold; tangency/consistency algorithm; presymplectic Lagrangian systems; almost-regular/semiregular Legendre map; constant-rank image; second-order equation problem.
- **Covariant field side:** pre-multisymplectic constraint algorithm; integrable multivector fields/distributions; holonomy constraints; multimomentum Legendre map; Einstein-Palatini multisymplectic formulation.
- **Singular-reduction side:** orbit-type strata; stratified symplectic space; singular momentum level; seams; Poisson algebra of strata; stratum-preserving Hamiltonian flow.
- **Gravity-nearby side:** degenerate/noninvertible tetrads; invertible/noninvertible phases; torsion in degenerate sectors; Poincare-gauge constraint bifurcation.
- **Interface side:** BV-BFV cutting/gluing; (derived) fiber product of field spaces; boundary phase space; matching as a homotopy fiber product.

Search surfaces were Crossref and arXiv metadata/abstract APIs. First pass queries included `Presymplectic manifolds Dirac-Bergmann`, `constraint bifurcation Poincare gauge Nester Yo`, `irregular constrained systems`, `degenerate tetrad first order gravity Hamiltonian`, `multisymplectic constraint algorithm singular field theories`, `Palatini constraint degenerate tetrads`, and `Hamiltonian field theory boundary gluing constraints`. Pivots were from “variable rank” (mostly noisy) to the historically productive terms **irregular**, **constraint bifurcation**, **field activation**, **almost regular**, and **singular reduction**. Second pass used exact-phrase arXiv queries for those terms plus `BV-BFV gluing`. The stopping condition was reached at ten source families after the second pass added no category beyond the six above.

## 5. Decisive warnings and candidate no-go mechanisms

1. **Regularity failure before counting.** If shell constraints are functionally dependent, naive Poisson-rank counting can create false first-class generators or hide multiplier equations. This is a candidate diagnostic, not a verdict on the present shell.
2. **Almost-regular frameworks may exclude the target by definition.** A nonconstant-rank Legendre map can destroy the global primary submanifold/fiber-bundle assumptions. The proper response may be a stratumwise algorithm plus compatibility data, not forced “regularization.”
3. **Constraint bifurcation can invalidate linearized counts.** A gauge variable on the special background can become dynamical off it, or conversely a full-theory mode can disappear on a singular locus. Therefore the future background matrix must be compared with nearby full nonlinear minors.
4. **Cross-stratum determinism is an extra condition.** Solvability of `i_X omega=dH` on each stratum does not ensure a unique vector field tangent to, leaving, or entering the rank-change locus. A candidate obstruction is nonunique continuation through a locus where additional kernel directions appear.
5. **Singular reduction may preserve strata.** In standard invariant Hamiltonian systems, reduced dynamics is commonly stratum-preserving. If the reduction field places `43` reopening in a transverse orbit-type direction, ordinary Hamiltonian gauge reduction might not generate the desired transition. This warning applies only if project rank strata really coincide with orbit-type strata.
6. **Degenerate-tetrad solutions are not canonical well-posedness results.** Finite curvature and continuous fields across a phase boundary do not rule out hidden arbitrary functions, missed interface variations, or a changing physical degree count.
7. **Gluing can be derived rather than additive.** BV-BFV fiber products support D017’s warning against adding isolated boundary ranks, but only after the boundary complexes and trace maps exist. Failure of transversality may itself signal extra derived/reducible interface data.

## 6. Negative searches and uncertainties

- Literal `rank-changing presymplectic form interface gravity` and `variable-rank primary surface Palatini` searches produced no clear primary family in two passes; “rank changing” was dominated by unrelated matrix/statistics material.
- `degenerate tetrad first order` found solution-building papers, but no abstract explicitly advertising a complete bulk-plus-interface Dirac-Bergmann analysis across the degenerate boundary.
- `almost-regular` material appears designed for a single constant-rank Legendre image; no inspected abstract supplied a ready-made theorem for a stratified Legendre map.
- `BV-BFV gluing` supplied ordinary cutting/gluing technology, not mixed-dimensional or rank-changing boundary conditions.
- No inspected material established that gauge and second-class sectors can convert smoothly while preserving deterministic evolution. Primary texts remain unread, so even the exact hypotheses behind the warnings are uncertain.
