# Exploratory Literature Leads

This directory holds bounded discovery reports from `literature-explorer`.
Reports may contain alternate terminology, historical trails, cross-domain
analogies, proposed structural mappings, and transferable methods. They are
candidate leads, not source evidence, and must never be linked to a claim as a
canonical literature note.

Promising source statements are handed to `literature` for primary-source and
regime validation under `research/literature/notes/`. Promising mathematical or
physical correspondences are handed to `theorist` for an auditable `DNNN`
derivation. The director incorporates only the resulting evaluated artifacts.
