# Literature Evidence

Keep canonical BibTeX metadata in `bibliography.bib` and source-specific evidence packets in `notes/`. A note records what the source explicitly supports or contradicts, the exact evidence location, assumptions, regime, and extraction uncertainty.

Open-ended discovery reports live in `explorations/`. They deliberately range
across terminology, history, fields, and mathematical structures, but remain
candidate leads. Promote source-dependent leads through a focused note and
mapping-dependent leads through a `DNNN` derivation before incorporating them.

Prefer primary sources and stable identifiers. A paper discussing a related phenomenon does not establish a project claim. Search for contradictory evidence and trace important citations backward to their original source.

External discovery and library integrations are optional; see `docs/INTEGRATIONS.md`.
