# Literature note: Hamiltonian and Noether Charges in First Order Gravity

## Question investigated

What boundary dimension and variational structure occur in a standard four-dimensional first-order gravitational action, and does that construction supply a model for a direct five-dimensional-to-three-dimensional singular interface term?

## Search strategy

On 2026-08-19, arXiv and the Springer DOI record were queried for `first order gravity Palatini boundary term variation`. The full arXiv text was read through the generic covariant variation in §II and the complete first-order action in §III. The exact action and boundary decomposition, rather than the abstract, were used as evidence.

## Source identity

- Alejandro Corichi, Iraís Rubalcava-García, and Tatjana Vukašinac, “Hamiltonian and Noether Charges in First Order Gravity,” *General Relativity and Gravitation* **46** (2014), no. 11, article 1813.
- DOI: [`10.1007/s10714-014-1813-0`](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10714-014-1813-0).
- arXiv: [`1312.7828`](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.7828).

## Claims supported

- **Literature-supported generic result:** on a four-dimensional spacetime manifold `M`, the variation of a four-form Lagrangian gives Euler-Lagrange terms plus `d theta`; ordinary Stokes converts this into an integral of a three-form over `partial M`.
- **Literature-supported gravitational example:** the paper's variables are a cotetrad and `SO(3,1)` connection on a four-dimensional spacetime, possibly with boundary.
- **Literature-supported action:** Eq. (37) writes the well-posed Palatini action as a four-dimensional bulk integral plus an ordinary boundary integral over `partial M`.
- **Literature-supported boundary structure:** `partial M=M_1 union M_2 union Delta union I`; the weakly isolated horizon `Delta` is a three-dimensional null hypersurface. Thus the relevant action boundary is codimension one.
- **Project interpretation:** this is a clean `4 -> 3` first-order-gravity benchmark. It gives no `5 -> 3` codimension-two incidence and does not analyze a rank-jumping quotient.

## Claims contradicted

- The source contradicts the broad claim that surface terms can be ignored whenever the bulk equations are first order: differentiability and finiteness require boundary terms under the stated asymptotic conditions.
- It does not directly contradict a proposed new singular-interface action; rather, it does not support extrapolating the ordinary Palatini boundary term to D002's direct `5 -> 3` incidence.

## Exact evidence location

- §II.1, Eqs. (2)--(4): four-form Lagrangian on four-dimensional `M`, variation, and Stokes integral over `partial M`.
- §II.1, Eqs. (5)--(8): addition and variation of an ordinary boundary term.
- §II.1, Eq. (15): decomposition of the ordinary spacetime boundary into `M_1`, `M_2`, `Delta`, and `I`.
- §III opening paragraphs: cotetrad and Lorentz connection on four-dimensional spacetime.
- §III.1, Eqs. (36)--(37): complete action and explicit Palatini bulk/boundary action.
- §III.2.2, opening definition: `Delta` is a three-dimensional null surface; Eqs. (53)--(59) state its isolated-horizon geometry.

## Assumptions / regime

- Ordinary four-dimensional Lorentzian spacetime with cotetrad and `SO(3,1)` connection.
- Asymptotically flat outer boundary and, when present, a weakly isolated-horizon inner boundary with specified field falloffs/conditions.
- Standard manifold boundary and Stokes theorem; no non-pure-dimensional topology, circle collapse, changing coframe rank, or resolution equivalence.
- The detailed charge conclusions depend on the authors' covariant-Hamiltonian convention and boundary conditions; those charge conclusions are not used to infer the benchmark incidence result.

## Confidence

- **Source identity:** high; DOI, journal article number, authors/title, and arXiv record agree.
- **Evidence extraction:** high for the displayed action and boundary dimensions.
- **Project applicability:** high as a codimension-one control case; low as evidence for the singular `5 -> 3` problem.

## Unresolved issues

- How to formulate a Palatini/Chern--Simons-type action on a non-pure-dimensional quotient rather than an ordinary manifold with boundary.
- Whether additional corner or defect degrees of freedom would be required at the three-dimensional interface.
- Whether any such term is invariant under the competing smooth structures exhibited in D002.
