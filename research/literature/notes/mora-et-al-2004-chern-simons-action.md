# Literature note: Finite Action Principle for Chern--Simons AdS Gravity

## Question investigated

What boundary form and variational term arise in explicit five-dimensional first-order Chern--Simons AdS gravity, and do they produce a direct three-dimensional interface contribution when a circle collapses?

## Search strategy

On 2026-08-19, arXiv and DOI/Crossref records were queried for `five dimensional Chern-Simons gravity first order boundary term variation`. The complete arXiv text was read through the five-dimensional construction and its odd-dimensional generalization. Equations (6)--(22), not the abstract alone, were used as evidence.

## Source identity

- P. Mora, R. Olea, R. Troncoso, and J. Zanelli, “Finite Action Principle for Chern--Simons AdS Gravity,” *Journal of High Energy Physics* **2004** (06), 036.
- DOI: [`10.1088/1126-6708/2004/06/036`](https://doi.org/10.1088/1126-6708/2004/06/036).
- arXiv: [`hep-th/0405267`](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0405267).

## Claims supported

- **Literature-supported action:** Eq. (6) gives the five-dimensional first-order Chern--Simons AdS action as a five-form bulk integral plus `dB_4`; `B_4` is an ordinary four-form boundary term.
- **Literature-supported variation:** Eq. (7) gives bulk equations plus `int_{partial M} Theta`; Eqs. (10)--(12) display the four-form surface variation.
- **Literature-supported boundary condition/result:** Eqs. (13)--(16) determine a local `B_4` and show that the on-shell boundary variation vanishes under the stated extrinsic-curvature/second-fundamental-form condition.
- **Literature-supported generalization:** Eqs. (17)--(22) retain the ordinary pattern `d=2n+1` bulk to `2n`-dimensional boundary.
- **Project interpretation:** this source provides the exact ordinary `5 -> 4` first-order Chern--Simons control case. It contains no mechanism that regrades a collapsed four-face into an intrinsic three-dimensional term.

## Claims contradicted

- The source contradicts the broad assertion that the five-dimensional Chern--Simons bulk action is variationally complete without a boundary term under the paper's asymptotically AdS boundary conditions.
- It does not directly rule out a new three-dimensional defect/corner action. It only shows that the standard action and Stokes variation terminate first on the ordinary four-dimensional boundary.

## Exact evidence location

- §2, Eqs. (1)--(5): five-dimensional manifold, `partial M`, boundary condition, and component form of `B_4`.
- §2, Eq. (6): first-order five-dimensional Chern--Simons AdS action.
- §2, Eqs. (7)--(12): bulk equations and explicit four-form surface term.
- §2.1, Eqs. (13)--(16): boundary condition, integrated `B_4`, and on-shell variation.
- §3, Eqs. (17)--(22): general odd-dimensional bulk and even-dimensional ordinary boundary term.

## Assumptions / regime

- Ordinary five-dimensional manifold with boundary, negative cosmological constant, and the special Lovelock combination that is Chern--Simons for the AdS group.
- Independent vielbein and spin connection in first-order notation; the paper's boundary geometry uses intrinsic curvature and second fundamental form.
- Boundary conditions fix the relevant extrinsic-curvature data and are designed for asymptotically AdS configurations.
- No collapsed circle, non-pure stratification, rank-changing coframe, quotient equalizer, or resolution-independence condition is present.

## Confidence

- **Source identity:** high; title/authors, JHEP locator, DOI, and arXiv record agree.
- **Evidence extraction:** high; the explicit action, variation, boundary form, and assumptions were read in the full text.
- **Project applicability:** high as a standard `5 -> 4` dimensional control; low for direct support of the singular interface.

## Unresolved issues

- Whether varying an auxiliary resolved four-face and then integrating its circle fiber can produce a consistent three-dimensional corner term.
- Whether such a term can be written solely in D002's descended quotient fields.
- Whether it is invariant under changing the resolution profile and smooth structure.
