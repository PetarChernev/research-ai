## Synthesis by candidate claim

- **C001:** No source establishes the full state-space package. Conically smooth spaces supply controlled intrinsic local topology; diffeology supplies quotient smoothness and a restricted forms-descent theorem. Their compatibility, Lorentzian enhancement, integration, causal structure, and physical resolution equivalence remain unproved.
- **C002:** First-order polynomiality at degeneracy is supported only as an ingredient. Existing evidence instead makes the missing complete variation and connection-data count more urgent. The smooth-pinch example demonstrates that omitting boundary/surface terms can erase the entire saddle action.
- **C003:** No direct evidence was found. Degenerate-tetrad underdetermination and signature-change ill-posedness are distinct neighboring warnings, not a shared no-go argument. The required `4<->5` constraint and hyperbolicity calculation remains open.

Accordingly, none of C001–C003 should be promoted to `literature-supported` from this packet.

## Dependence and mismatch warnings

1. Conically smooth and diffeological frameworks solve different parts of C001; combining them is a new construction, not triangulation of one established result.
2. Kaul–Sengupta analyze constant rank three in four-dimensional first-order gravity, whereas this program needs a five-to-four rank transition and physical quotient. Their underdetermined functions cannot yet be counted as ghosts or physical modes.
3. Witten's bolt/bubble remains a smooth fixed-dimensional manifold. It is evidence for circle-pinching regularity, not for exact open-set dimension loss.
4. Alty–Fewster change signature and introduce an elliptic/negative-spectrum sector. Their instability mechanism may not survive a spacelike direction becoming gauge.
5. These five sources are mutually useful but do not constitute independent confirmation of the program's combined claims because no source studies the combined system.

## Focused literature gaps

2. Lorentzian/causal and hyperbolic PDE structures on conically smooth or comparable stratified spaces.
3. Covariant phase-space and boundary/transgression terms for first-order gravity with changing coframe rank.
4. A complete Hamiltonian analysis of spatial-rank-degenerate tetrads/coframes, preferably including a rank-changing interface.
5. Transmission/energy estimates for a constrained hyperbolic system whose spacelike fiber mode becomes first-class gauge, rather than changes signature.
6. A theorem relating quotient diffeology/conical smoothness to equivalence classes of circle-collapse resolutions.
