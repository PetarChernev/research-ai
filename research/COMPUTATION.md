## Evidence standard for the new portfolio

Strong evidence for the next bounded model would be:
1. explicit physical states, all retained exterior degrees of freedom, and a
   definition of dimension with a declared probe and energy/length window;
2. an autonomous action or transition generator whose region follows from
   initial data, not a prescribed trajectory;
3. a finite-domain observable and complete energy accounting over a time when
   that observable can actually be measured;
4. relevant controlled limits, residuals, negative controls and, if numerical,
   convergence in domain, discretization, time step and precision;
5. a distinct accounting of motion, formation and stability. None substitutes
   for another; particle stability is not required for a transient toy success.

Literal intrinsic change additionally needs a field/observable algebra and
causal transfer across incidence changes. A spectrum or kinetic rank cannot
prove deletion of physical points/data. Emergent geometry needs locality and a
controlled resolution window; equal spectral exponents do not prove common
probe geometry. D034 and its critique supply scoped rejection tests, not a
universal impossibility result.

## Current machine-check obligations

the sketches are machine verified. No stack, custom kernel, environment, or
Engineer task is selected.

- D027/D029 residuals, D030's four-site polynomial, and D031's two-state
  generator are cheap consistency checks, but the explicit hand witnesses and
  bounded attacks already settle their role in the route comparison. They
  would not discriminate the missing junction, finite-region or matter bridge.
- D028's snapshot spectral test is the most promising later executable
  assertion. Its initial smoothing and parameters are not yet fixed, and
  finite-size binding alone cannot decide a finite-lifetime experiment. First
  specify a monotone profile, an interior source (circle momentum is conserved),
  cutoff/gradient scales and a useful observation time. A predeclared binding
  or response assertion can then warrant an obligation. Do not manufacture
  arbitrary parameter choices merely to obtain a passing eigenvalue.
- D030 is the lowest-cost alternative for a finite-region response contract.
  First define a finite-energy source/detector and useful signal threshold;
  the next check should measure it, not just re-diagonalize a four-cycle.
- D031 requires theory of matter on created interiors before a computation can
  decide the main physical gap. A shared-edge count is a useful future control,
  not evidence that a probe experiences the new direction.
- Reject D032's unspecific Hessian-as-locality check. Its first critique remains
  revision-required; no implementation is authorized. D033's determinant-one

## Computational representations and methods

Scientific Computation must assess the mathematical domain, encoded assumptions,
operations, equality/error semantics, domain boundaries, negative controls,
and trusted implementation surface before any conclusion-critical code.

Methods follow the chosen assertion, not the archived toolkit:
- Finite site/incidence algebra may admit a tiny exact matrix representation;
  a generic CAS or new reusable library is not justified in advance.
- Radial probe questions may suit analytic bounds or converged numerical
  operators, but weighted measure, self-adjoint domain, infinity treatment and
  time dependence must be frozen first. No PDE package is selected now.
- A field-transfer existence theorem is not replaced by finite sampling;
  formal infrastructure is a separate cost decision, not mandatory ceremony.

## Numerical and formal evidence standards

No W002 numerical or formal evidence is claimed. For a future exact obligation,
every declared identity, sign, rank and negative control must agree in the
chosen explicit representation. Numerical evidence requires a predeclared norm,
tolerance, refinement ladder, infinity/boundary controls, solver and precision
checks, and a result confined to the sampled regime. Formal evidence proves
only its encoded proposition and assumptions. Sampling is not a universal proof;
no criterion may be weakened after execution to clear a failure. A materially
changed strategy must preserve and explicitly supersede, not delete, old checks.

## Phase-transition triggers

- A chosen finite-domain observable and measurement window justify moving from
  route mapping to focused computation. Predeclare exact question and criterion.
- A surviving intrinsic transfer or cell-matter model may require new functional
  or incidence machinery; assess it then, not before model survival.
- Stability, universal probes, gravity, and quantization each change the evidence
  standard; none is inferred from a toy spectrum or energy minimum.
