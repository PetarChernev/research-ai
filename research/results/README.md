# Consolidated Results

Store validated cross-artifact summaries here only after linking their underlying derivations, experiments, literature notes, and claim IDs. This directory is not a place to promote preliminary output into polished conclusions.

User-approved Opus claim attacks live in `verification/`. Fast same-model GPT attacks live in `research/critiques/`. Pre-cutover reports marked `legacy: true` remain under `verification/` as nonqualifying historical records. A failed or inconclusive verification remains visible and may require a claim-ledger and `STATE.md` update.
