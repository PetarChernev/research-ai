## Three different meanings, not interchangeable answers

| Meaning | What changes? | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Intrinsic local dimension | Physical neighborhoods/function algebra or cell incidence; exterior fiber points absent | Physical space, integration, causal matter dynamics and complete transition data |
| Emergent geometry | Microscopic state produces additional local propagation geometry over a scale window | Relational locality, spectra **and** transport, controlled errors and compatible probes |
| Probe-relative accessibility | A given probe gains an excitable or propagating channel at certain energies | Source/detector definition, state preparation, spectrum/response and observation time |

Operational success can be a useful baseline without being evidence for literal
disappearance. Initial data may specify a region: autonomy means its subsequent
trajectory follows from equations. Formation and stable persistence are separate
stronger gates. An energy formula alone also need not distinguish dimensional
physics from an ordinary phase bubble.

## Model sketches and what their calculations actually establish

### A. Moving intrinsic domain: energy before a complete geometry

D027 assigns a fixed-radius circle only over a varied interior, with separately
postulated stratum and wall measures. Its spherical restriction gives

`E = 4 pi sigma R^2 / sqrt(1-Rdot^2) + (4 pi/3) Delta-epsilon R^3`,

`Rddot = -2(1-Rdot^2)/R - (Delta-epsilon/sigma)(1-Rdot^2)^(3/2)`,

where `Delta-epsilon=2 pi a rho5-rho4`. These are derived effective-model
relations, not ADM formulas. The finite-radius metric does not simply descend
where all fiber points are identified. All radial predictions are mimicked by
an ordinary phase wall with the same pressure and tension. Next needs a scalar
junction variational domain with energy and information transfer, not more
wall-force algebra or another Palatini counterterm.

### B. Positive-radius phases: nearest continuum operational comparator

D028 varies a canonical scalar on a fixed base with
`V(phi)=lambda(phi^2-v^2)^2/4` and `R_circle=R0 exp(phi/f)>0`.
It derives wall tension `sigma=2 sqrt(2 lambda)v^3/3`, finite spherical initial
energy `4 pi sigma L^2[1+O(delta^2/L^2)]` for large domains, and exact initial
contraction `Lddot(0)=-2/L` for locally kink-matched data. A minimally coupled
circle probe has homogeneous masses `m_n=|n|/R`.

The critic isolates three gates: finite spatial extent must really support the
channel; it must be prepared and observed before contraction; and large radius
contrast must obey gradient/EFT bounds. Conserved circle momentum forbids an
incoming exterior zero mode from automatically exciting `n=1`. Homogeneous
thresholds are not a finite-lived probe experiment. A scaling argument also
excludes a nontrivial static finite-energy bubble in this positive canonical
scalar model. Stable particles, spontaneous formation and literal R=0 are absent.

### C. Localization: a clean but differently directed effect

D029's autonomous five-dimensional kink traps one probe state at
`mu0^2=m0^2+a^2`, below a continuum beginning at `m0^2+2a^2`. A boost gives
exact inertial wall motion. This reduces low-energy transverse access on an
infinite layer, rather than creating a finite bubble with another direction
inside. Tails, sufficiently energetic probes and a decoupled species retain
bulk access. More exact algebra on this spectrum does not bridge the mismatch.

### D. Dynamical links: transparent operational workbench

D030 has N real probe fields on a fixed cycle over ordinary 3+1 spacetime and
a varied common link field q. The potential coupling
`g^2 q^2 sum_j(phi_(j+1)-phi_j)^2/2` gives

`omega_n^2 = k^2+m^2+4g^2 q0^2 sin^2(pi n/N)`.

Compact finite-energy selector data evolve autonomously. Site-local preparation
and readout are necessary to distinguish internal transport from flavor masses.
A neighbor acquires initial acceleration proportional to `g^2 q^2` times the
source-site field. The critic extends this early-time statement to localized
finite-energy data in a plateau. A quantitatively useful signal/time window is
still needed. Large N offers a limited continuum-like momentum window, not an
exact fifth open stratum.

At q=0 **N physical exterior probes remain**. Strong links, not vanishing links,
make relative modes heavy at fixed N. Calling link removal state removal would
invert the spectral interpretation. The cost is explicit and finite, unlike
the infinite-sheet limit of G. No stable finite domain is demonstrated.

### E. Incidence-changing space: literal but modest

D031 takes physical space to be the realization of a simplicial complex. Adding
a triangle to an existing cycle creates its open interior, rather than adding
a dimension label. The triangle-plus-tail witness has two- and one-dimensional
open spatial neighborhoods when filled. The claim concerns new interiors and
previously bare edges: not every toggle changes every adjacent point's dimension.

For face area A, edge length a, area coefficient mu, boundary tension tau and
q already occupied neighboring edges, `Delta E=mu A+tau a(3-2q)`.
Uniform autonomous heat-bath rates obey detailed balance. For the isolated
two-state witness,

`p_occupied(t)=[1-exp(-nu t)]/[1+exp(beta epsilon)]`,

`epsilon=mu A+3 tau a`, and mean geometry energy is `epsilon p_occupied(t)`.
This is **occupation probability**, not the cumulative probability of ever
forming a face. Reservoir heat balances geometry energy; the model is not an
isolated Hamiltonian system. Existing cycles supply eligible sites but not their
occupation trajectories. The unchanged vertex Laplacian cannot detect the face.
Next needs interior-resolving matter and a creation/removal transfer rule, not a
large occupancy simulation. No macroscopic 3+1/4+1 or causal quantum claim follows.

### F/G. Warnings rather than immediate defaults

D032 has a positive matrix Hamiltonian, global noncommutative equilibrium and
adjoint spectrum proportional to `l(l+1)` with multiplicity `2l+1`. Its spatial
spectral dimension is near two only in a large-N intermediate-resolution window.
There is no local patch or extra-single-direction region. A Hessian's physical
zero modes do not classify communication: massive fluctuations can couple
systems without zero modes. That proposed check is **not adopted**; the critique's
stronger representation-rigidity discussion is not promoted as separate evidence.

D033 turns a transverse gradient coefficient `q^2/f^2` on/off without losing
temporal kinetic rank. Fixed modes cross q=0 regularly with nonnegative energy,
but infinitely many massless exterior base fields remain. Modewise ODE uniqueness
and determinant-one transfer do not imply full-interval uniform nonlinear PDE
control. A norm/cutoff/derivative-loss question must precede computation if revived.
