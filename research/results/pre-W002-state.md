## Current question

Can an exact local `d=4 <-> d=5` Lorentzian theory be defined without a
physical ambient bulk, from one covariant first-order variational principle,
and shown provisionally consistent? The unwarped and common-warp source-free
backgrounds fail, and C023 now shows that the two exact D002 profiles in the
same zero-spin-current geometry require unbounded, NEC-violating effective
stress despite componentwise local volume integrability. C024 now proves that
every positive classical proper-normal radius with zero endpoint value and
slope has unbounded radial-circle sectional curvature. C025 tests the selected
nonzero-slope escape: its punctured bulks are flat, but a free finite mixed `43`
trace activates an uncancelled D004 boundary equation. C026 tests the first
minimal covariant action repair. Its full tensorial-difference primitive cancels
that row but only changes polarization: fixed reference data anchor the slope,
unrestricted reference variation degenerates the triad, and a fixed-seed orbit
fixes holonomy while adding unclassified edge/corner structure. C027 now tests
the smallest explicit covariant reduction field. Its induced stabilizer
connection is well defined and cancels the coset `43` row, but the compulsory
free-coframe equation forces `c=0`; no positive rank-three finite-slope
background survives. C028 now exhausts the complete purely algebraic
exterior-degree-four class generated by `V,E,K=D_omega V,eta,epsilon` with at
most one `K`: its only two invariants cannot simultaneously cancel the mixed row
and retain free rank-three `c>0` stationarity. C029 now makes one tangent
category explicit. For ordinary boundary-trace connection descent along
`p:Sigma x S1->Sigma`, generic polar holonomy is excluded; at an admitted
identity-holonomy lattice value the constant nonzero mixed tangent fails the
linearized descent condition and is not an infinitesimal periodic presentation
mode. This avoids rather than cancels C025's row and does not define a full
connection on the singular collar or descend `(A,V)`. The next F0 discriminator
is therefore which complete field object/category must descend, not another
unbounded counterterm search. Functional constraint preservation remains
deferred until a complete action admits a genuine background.
