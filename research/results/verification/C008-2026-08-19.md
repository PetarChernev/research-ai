## Reconstruction

### 1. Dimensions, flatness, and asymptotic order

Write `r=L/s` for `s>0`. Since both `L` and `s` have length dimension, `r`, `r^2`, and `r^4` are dimensionless, while `rho_i` has the length dimension of `ell`. For any positive integer `p`, derivatives of `exp[-(L/s)^p]` are that exponential times finite sums of powers of `s^{-1}`. The exponential dominates every such power as `s downarrow 0`. Both zero extensions are therefore smooth and have zero jet at the interface.

The ratio relevant to the canonical comparison is

\[
k(s)=\frac{\rho_2(s)}{\rho_1(s)}
=\exp[-(L/s)^4+(L/s)^2].
\]

Near zero, the exponent is at most `-(L/s)^4/2`, after shrinking the neighborhood. Every derivative of the zero extension of `k` is therefore zero at the interface. Thus `k` is smooth and flat. Conversely,

\[
\rho_1/\rho_2=\exp[(L/s)^4-(L/s)^2]\longrightarrow\infty.
\]

This ordering is asymptotic, not necessarily global on all of `(0,epsilon)`: if `s>L`, then `(L/s)^4<(L/s)^2` and `rho_2>rho_1`. No global inequality is needed for the germ or strict-inclusion arguments.

### 2. Smooth forward canonical map

On the first horn the canonical map is the restriction of

\[
\widetilde h_{12}(u,s,x,y)
=(u,s,k(s)x,k(s)y)
\]

with `k=0` for `s<=0`. This is an ordinary smooth ambient map. On a positive circle its output radius is `k rho_1=rho_2`; on the collapsed branch both radii are zero. Therefore its restriction is the canonical homeomorphism `h_12:H_1->H_2`, and pullback of every locally ambient-smooth function on `H_2` is locally ambient-smooth on `H_1`.

### 3. `C1` obstruction for the inverse

Transport the ambient coordinate `x` on `H_1` to `H_2` by the inverse. Its value on a positive circle is

\[
f_1(u,s,\theta)=\rho_1(s)\cos\theta.
\]

If this function had even a `C1` ambient extension `Phi` near the tip of `H_2`, then at fixed `u` and small positive `s`,

\[
\Phi(u,s,+\rho_2,0)=+\rho_1,
\qquad
\Phi(u,s,-\rho_2,0)=-\rho_1.
\]

The one-variable mean-value theorem on the intervening ambient segment yields a point `xi_s` with

\[
\partial_x\Phi(u,s,\xi_s,0)=\frac{\rho_1(s)}{\rho_2(s)}.
\]

The right side diverges. The points `(u,s,xi_s,0)` converge to the tip, while a continuous first derivative is bounded on a sufficiently small closed ambient box. This contradiction excludes a `C1` extension, not merely a `C-infinity` extension. Hence the inverse is not smooth for the locally ambient-extendible differential structures.

### 4. Both strict algebra inclusions

Every ambient restriction on `H_i` pulls back along the smooth resolution map `F_i` to a smooth function on `M` constant on the quotient fibers. Therefore

\[
C_i^\infty\subset C_q^\infty.
\]

The ambient extension of `h_12` above gives

\[
C_2^\infty\subset C_1^\infty.
\]

The first inclusion is strict because `f_1=rho_1 cos(theta)` is the restriction of ambient `x` on `H_1`, while the preceding derivative contradiction shows `f_1 notin C_2^infty`.

For strictness of `C_1^infty subset C_q^infty`, define the dimensionally valid slower profile

\[
\rho_0(s)=\ell e^{-L/s}\quad(s>0),
\qquad \rho_0=0\quad(s\leq0).
\]

Its zero extension is smooth and flat, so `f_0=rho_0 cos(theta)` is quotient-smooth. If it had a `C1` ambient extension on `H_1`, the same antipodal mean-value argument would require

\[
\partial_x\Phi=\frac{\rho_0}{\rho_1}
=\exp[(L/s)^2-L/s]\longrightarrow\infty.
\]

It does not. Thus

\[
C_2^\infty\subsetneq C_1^\infty\subsetneq C_q^\infty.
\]

### 5. Separate ambient-subdiffeology test

The function-sheaf obstruction does not automatically prove failure for every possible diffeology, so I checked the literal sub-diffeologies independently. Give a plot parameter `t` the same length coordinate convention as `s`, set `s(t)=t`, and for `t>0` set

\[
(x(t),y(t))=\rho_2(t)(\cos\Theta(t),\sin\Theta(t)),
\qquad \Theta(t)=e^{(L/t)^3},
\]

with `(x,y)=(0,0)` for `t<=0`. Every `n`th derivative of the angular factor grows by at most a power of `t^{-1}` times `exp[n(L/t)^3]`. Multiplication by `rho_2=ell exp[-(L/t)^4]` makes every derivative tend to zero. Hence this is a smooth ambient curve in `H_2`.

Its canonical inverse has radius `rho_1` and the same angle. Orthogonality of radial and angular derivatives gives

\[
\left|\frac{d}{dt}(x_1,y_1)\right|^2
=(\rho_1')^2+(\rho_1\Theta')^2,
\]

and

\[
\rho_1|\Theta'|
=3\ell L^3 t^{-4}
\exp[-(L/t)^2+(L/t)^3]\longrightarrow\infty.
\]

The image curve is not `C1`. Thus the inverse also fails the plot criterion for the ambient sub-diffeologies. This is logically separate from the locally extendible-function argument.

### 6. Smooth base-diffeomorphism attack

Let a sign-preserving smooth normal-coordinate diffeomorphism satisfy `phi(0)=0` and `phi'(0)=a>0`. Then

\[
\phi(s)=as+O(s^2).
\]

An equivariant label-preserving horn map covering `phi` has radial multiplier

\[
\frac{\rho_2(\phi(s))}{\rho_1(s)}
=\exp\left[-\frac{L^4}{\phi(s)^4}+\frac{L^2}{s^2}\right],
\]

which is still flat at zero, while the reciprocal multiplier for the inverse still diverges. Linear or higher smooth corrections to `phi` cannot exchange a fourth-order pole in the exponent with a second-order pole.

Exact circumference matching makes the obstruction especially transparent:

\[
\rho_2(\phi(s))=\rho_1(s)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\phi(s)=\sqrt{Ls}
\]

on the positive side. This has an infinite derivative at zero and is not an allowed smooth base diffeomorphism. The opposite matching map has positive-side behavior `s^2/L`, whose inverse is not smooth and which cannot be extended across zero as a sign-preserving local diffeomorphism. Thus no allowed smooth base diffeomorphism of this canonical equivariant kind invalidates the stated restricted claim.

## Falsification attempts

1. **Dimensional-exponent attack.** All exponential arguments `(L/s)^2`, `(L/s)^4`, `L/s`, and `(L/t)^3` are dimensionless. The exact-matching coordinate `sqrt(Ls)` also has length dimension. No hidden dimensional scale is missing.
2. **Flatness attack.** Differentiating the ratio `k`, rather than only the profiles, could have spoiled the forward ambient extension. Quartic decay dominates the positive quadratic term and every derivative polynomial, so it does not.
3. **Global-ordering attack.** `rho_2<rho_1` is false far enough from the tip if the slab extends past `s=L`. The claim only needs `rho_2/rho_1->0`; all proofs were restricted to a sufficiently small positive neighborhood.
4. **Inverse-extension attack.** A nonlinear ambient extension off `H_2` cannot evade the antipodal mean-value theorem, because its derivative is forced on an actual line segment, not estimated from a guessed radial formula.
5. **Algebra-equality attack.** The explicit witnesses `rho_1 cos(theta)` and `rho_0 cos(theta)` separate the two inclusions. Both witnesses are checked to be members of the larger algebra before being used to exclude membership in the smaller one.
6. **Function-sheaf versus diffeology attack.** The rapidly rotating curve is an ambient-subspace plot but has no smooth lift through the final quotient at zero. Its inverse image is not `C1`, confirming that the one-way result is not just an artifact of testing extendible scalar functions.
7. **Base-coordinate attack.** Every genuine smooth base diffeomorphism has a nonzero linear normal term and preserves the order of the pole in the exponential. Singular maps such as `sqrt(Ls)` are homeomorphisms on a half-line but are not allowed smooth resolution equivalences.
8. **Overclaim attack.** The argument does not rule out an exotic abstract diffeomorphism that mixes the base, angular, and ambient tangent directions in a way not induced by the declared canonical resolution map. C008 explicitly excludes that classification.

## Checks

- **Dimensions and units:** `[s]=[L]=[ell]=[rho_i]=L`, while `theta`, all exponential arguments, and all profile ratios are dimensionless. In the curve test, `Theta'` has dimension `L^-1`, so `rho_1 Theta'` is dimensionless as a derivative of one length coordinate by another.
- **Signs and normalization:** The negative quartic term makes `rho_2` asymptotically smaller. Reversing the map direction reverses the multiplier and is exactly why only one direction is smooth. No `2pi` factor enters the radial comparison.
- **Symmetries and conservation laws:** The canonical maps preserve `u`, `s`, and the `S^1` label and are equivariant for circle rotations. No conservation law or physical gauge equivalence is claimed or inferred.
- **Limiting or exactly soluble cases:** At any fixed `s>0`, both horns are ordinary circles and the canonical map is a smooth radial rescaling. The failure occurs only in the interface limit. Exact radius matching produces the nonsmooth half-line map `sqrt(Ls)`.
- **Numerical reproducibility and convergence:** Not applicable; the witnesses and limiting contradictions are analytic. No numerical implementation, tolerance, or random input was used.
- **Hidden parameter dependence:** The local result is independent of the positive values of `ell` and `L` after shrinking near zero, but it depends on the exponent orders `2<4`, infinite flatness, preservation of the normal coordinate/label in the canonical comparison, and the ambient-restriction or ambient-subdiffeology definitions.

## Findings

1. **Restriction that must remain explicit:** the profile ordering is asymptotic near `s=0`, not a global inequality on an unrestricted slab.
2. **Meaning of “not C1”:** it is proved in two concrete senses: the inverse pullback of ambient `x` has no local ambient `C1` extension, and a smooth ambient-subspace plot is sent to a curve that is not `C1`. D002 does not define an unrelated abstract `C1` differential-space category.
3. **Allowed smooth base reparametrizations do not repair the order mismatch:** a local diffeomorphism has a nonzero linear normal jet and leaves quadratic versus quartic pole order unchanged.
4. **The broader classification remains open:** the result is canonical/equivariant, not a theorem that the underlying differential spaces admit no abstract diffeomorphism whatsoever.
5. **Within those restrictions, all claimed directions and strictness signs are correct:** the faster profile gives the smaller ambient-restriction algebra, the forward map `H_1->H_2` is smooth, and the inverse fails already at `C1`.
