## Reconstruction

Compact alternate hand derivation, from `Q_ab=(b/4) eps_abcd e^c ^ e^d`, `eps_0123=+1`, cut pullback `e^0=e^3=0, e^1=dx1, e^2=dx2`.

*Background.* A nonzero `eps_abcd` needs `{c,d}` drawn from the surviving legs `{1,2}`, forcing `{a,b}={0,3}`. Both orderings contribute, `eps_0312=+1`, so `Q_03=(b/4)(e^1^e^2-e^2^e^1)=(b/2) dx1^dx2` and all other `Q_ab=0`. This is (22) including the factor 1/2.

*Derivative.* `dQ_ab = (b/2) eps_abcd (de^c ^ e^d)`. Taking the `dx1^dx2` coefficient with `de^c = de_1^c dx1 + de_2^c dx2` gives
`dQ_ab = (b/2)[ eps_{ab c 2} de_1^c - eps_{ab c 1} de_2^c ]`.
Evaluating the six canonical pairs with `eps_0132=-1`, `eps_0231=+1`, `eps_0321=-1`, `eps_0312=+1`, `eps_1302=-1`, `eps_2301=+1`:

- `dQ_01 = -(b/2) de_1^3`
- `dQ_02 = -(b/2) de_2^3`
- `dQ_03 = +(b/2)(de_1^1 + de_2^2)`
- `dQ_12 = 0` (index 1 or 2 always repeats)
- `dQ_13 = -(b/2) de_1^0`
- `dQ_23 = -(b/2) de_2^0`

Five independent image directions `{01,02,03,13,23}`, `Q_12` absent, so **rank = 5** and nullity `8-5=3` with kernel `{de_1^2, de_2^1, de_1^1-de_2^2}`. (Cross-check on D006 (24): pre-Hodge, varying `e^1^e^2` reaches internal pairs `{02,12,32,10,13}` and misses `03`; internal Hodge exchanges `03` and `12`, giving the same conclusion.)

*Block rank.* For `Omega_{(v,Q(e))} = [[A_Q, -D],[D^T, 0]]` with `D=G dQ` and `(A_Q)_{IJ}=<Q,[T_I,T_J]>`: a null vector `(w,y)` needs `D^T w=0` and `A_Q w = D y`. `ker D^T` is one-dimensional, spanned by the `12` direction (dual to the missing image direction; `G` is diagonal). Since `[T_12,T_cd]` always produces a generator carrying index 1 or 2, its `03` component vanishes identically, so `A_Q e_12 = 0` for the purely-`03` momentum `Q`. Hence `w = c e_12` and then `D y = 0`, i.e. `y in ker(dQ)`. Nullity `= 1+3 = 4`, **rank `= 14-4 = 10`**, i.e. five pairs. The `(g,lambda)` block `[[A_lambda,-G],[G,0]]` has invertible off-diagonal `G`, hence rank 12 unconditionally, and off shell `12+10=22`.

*Shell pullback, done in honest coordinates.* On the shell `Pi=Q+lambda=0`, `lambda` is no longer independent: `lambda=-Q(e)`. Substituting into `Theta^B = <lambda,xi> + <Q,delta v v^-1>` gives `Theta^B|_shell = <Q(e), chi_v - xi>`, whose exterior derivative on the 20-dimensional `(chi_v, xi, y=de)` tangent space is `Omega_{(v,Q)} - Omega_{(g,Q)}` sharing one `e` sector — it is *not* a direct sum, and it does have `xi`-`y` cross terms. Null conditions: `D y + A_Q chi_v = 0`, `D y + A_Q xi = 0`, `D^T(chi_v - xi) = 0`. Setting `s=chi_v-xi` forces `A_Q s=0` and `D^T s=0`, so `s in span{e_12}` (1 dim); `xi` is then *free* (6 dims) because `(A_Q xi)_{12} = <Q,[T_12,xi]> = 0` always puts `A_Q xi` in `image(D)`; `y` is fixed up to `ker(dQ)` (3 dims). Nullity `=1+6+3=10`, so **shell rank `= 20-10 = 10`**, confirming (29) even though the `g` directions are null only after the shift `chi_v -> chi_v - xi`.

*Moment maps.* With `delta_alpha g g^-1 = alpha_+ - Ad_g alpha_-` and `zeta=alpha_-`, `Z=Ad_g alpha_-`:
`Theta^B` contracts to `<Pi,alpha_+> - <Pi-Q, Ad_g alpha_-> = <Pi,alpha_+> - <Ad_(g^-1) lambda, alpha_->`, i.e. `J_+^B=Pi`, `J_-^B=-Ad_(g^-1)lambda`, and on shell `J_-^B=+Ad_(g^-1)Q`, nonzero. ## Falsification attempts

**Attack 1 — shell cancellation is illegitimate.** D006 (29) and O005 assert that `lambda=-Q` "cancels only the `g` block". Because `lambda` becomes a function of `e` on shell, I expected new `g`-`e` couplings that could raise the shell rank above 10. The reconstruction above computes the shell form in the full 20-dimensional `(chi_v,xi,de)` space and finds nullity exactly 10, rank exactly 10. The cross terms exist but are absorbed by the shift `s=chi_v-xi`; the `xi` directions are null precisely because `(A_Q xi)_{12}=0`, which is the same `T_12` degeneracy that gives `rank(dQ)=5`. **Attack fails; claim survives, and survives a stronger test than the one O005 encodes.**

**Attack 2 — sign/factor/pairing error in the corner moment map.** I recomputed `eps_0312`, the doubling of ordered `(c,d)` terms, the contraction `(32)`, and the indefinite pairing `diag(eta_aa eta_bb)`. All reproduce `Q_03=+b/2`, `J_-^B=-Ad_(g^-1)lambda`, shell `+Ad_(g^-1)Q`, witness `+b/2`. The overall sign is convention-dependent through `Omega=delta Theta`, which D006 §Conventions (11) states; C016 only asserts proportionality to `Ad_(g^-1)Q` and generic non-vanishing, which is convention-robust. **Attack fails.**

**Attack 3 — hidden upgrade from a nonzero cut form/charge to physical edge modes, or D006/O005 overreach.** I looked for any step where non-vanishing rank is silently read as physical content. D006 §5 states the opposite (primary second-class *defining* constraints of a first-order parameterisation are not an obstruction), §7 states that fixing `u` at the corner kills (39) by boundary condition, and §8 records outcome (iii). I also checked the self-consistency argument that blocks a shortcut classification: varying `omega_+` and `varpi` in `S_I` alone gives `Pi=0` and `g^-1 lambda g=0`, hence `Q=0`, contradicting (22) — so the interface action genuinely has no rank-three stationary branch on its own and cannot supply the total Gauss generators. That argument is sound and makes the negative clause of C016 correctly scoped rather than a hedge. **Attack fails.** One residual interpretive point, not a defect: A and B differ by a field redefinition, so the rank-10 block is a property of the Stueckelberg *reference choice*; C016 says exactly this and defers classification.
