## Frozen target

D009 claims, for the smooth equalizer, fixed timelike interface, basic
persistent `SO(1,3)` sector, and adapted coframe `E^4=rho eta`, that the regular
five-dimensional Lorentz boundary momentum necessarily contains the collapsing
leg and hence has zero ordinary and normalized-Haar pushforward at `rho=0`.
A finite divided trace requires a selected profile, domain, and length scale;
the D005 current `Q=(b/4) epsilon e_+ e_+` instead comes from independently
retained marked-circle incidence data. The no-cancellation conclusion is
restricted to the regular equalizer, not asserted universally.

## Load-bearing inference

The decisive bridge is equations (14)--(18): ordered antisymmetric-pair
normalization must yield

\[
P^{(5)}_{ab}={1\over4\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}
E^c\wedge E^d\wedge E^4,
\]

and its actual boundary pullback must vanish before either circle pushforward
is applied. If a coefficient surviving without `E^4`, or a second boundary
channel for the same `alpha^{ab}`, existed, the core-cancellation conclusion
would fail.

## Reconstruction

For fixed `a,b`, the definition
`P_ab=(12 kappa_5)^-1 epsilon_abCDE E^C E^D E^E` contains all six permutations
of the remaining two persistent indices and `4`. Epsilon antisymmetry cancels
the wedge permutation sign, so the six contributions agree. Writing the
result with `c,d` still summed over ordered values counts each complementary
persistent pair twice; therefore `6/12` in the explicit sum is exactly
`2/4`, establishing (14).

On a collar at `s>0`,

\[
j_s^*P_{ab}^{(5)}={\rho\over4\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}
e_+^c\wedge e_+^d\wedge(d\theta+A).
\]

Fiber integration selects only the vertical `dtheta` part, giving (15) and,
after division by `2pi`, (16). At the actual boundary, however,
`j_+^*E^4=0` in the smooth equalizer, so the entire three-form—not merely its
vertical coefficient—vanishes. Thus both pushforwards vanish and the boundary
term `-int alpha^{ab}P_ab` is zero. This is exact pullback followed by
pushforward, not an interchange of a singular limit with integration.

Dimensions are consistent: `E^3/kappa_5` and `rho e^2/kappa_5` are
dimensionless, while `(1/rho)p_!P` has dimension `L^-1` and requires `ell_*`.
At fixed `rho=R>0`, `kappa_4=kappa_5/(2pi R)` converts (15) exactly to
`P^-=(4 kappa_4)^-1 epsilon e e`, confirming the normalization.

## Falsification attempts

1. **Ordered-pair factor.** Explicit permutation counting reproduces
`1/(4 kappa_5)`. The planar `03` component receives the ordered `(1,2)` and
`(2,1)` terms, yielding `p_!P_03=pi rho/kappa_5 dx^1 wedge dx^2`, so no hidden
factor of two was found. Attack fails.

2. **Mixed channel feeding the same `SO(1,3)` generator.** A basic parameter
with `alpha^{4a}=0` can still induce mixed connection variations through the
full covariant derivative when mixed background connections are present.
Nevertheless, the invariant integration-by-parts identity packages those
terms into the bulk component `alpha^{ab}DP_ab`; its boundary polarization is
only `alpha^{ab}P_ab`. A separate `P_4a` boundary term would require a nonzero
mixed parameter. Thus mixed fields may affect the bulk Gauss equation but do
not add a second boundary momentum for the declared persistent generator.
Attack fails within the stated subgroup and boundary conditions.

3. **Overbroad exclusion of core cancellation.** The conclusion is repeatedly
limited to the smooth regular equalizer and explicitly exempts divided,
finite-regularity, edge, distributional, and retained-circle data. D005's `Q`
contains unscaled `eta`, no `rho`, and a separate coefficient `b`; it is thus
not an omitted trace of the regular `E^4=rho eta` momentum. The exclusion is
correctly conditional, not universal. Attack fails. Boundary counterterms or
changed polarization could alter the total generator, but they are enriched
theories rather than counterexamples to the bare regular-core statement.

## Findings

- **Blocking errors:** none found in equations (14)--(18) or their claimed
  regular-equalizer consequence.
- **Minor revision point:** the sentence before (15) says “four horizontal
  forms vanish” on the three-dimensional interface. For `P_ab` the `A` term is
  three horizontal forms and need not vanish; rather, it is annihilated by
  fiber pushforward. This does not affect (15), and at collapse its coefficient
  `rho` makes the full pullback zero, but the explanation should be corrected
  if D009 is later revised.
- **Residual risk:** the result does not establish the complete total
  exterior-plus-interface generator, as D009 itself states.
- **Controls passed analytically:** dimensions, exact-collapse pullback,
  normalized versus ordinary `2pi`, planar coefficient, and fixed-radius
  Kaluza--Klein normalization.
