# Internal critique

## Scope and independence

**Prominent limitation:** this is a fresh-session but same-model critique of a
derivation produced by `openai/gpt-5.6-sol`. It is procedurally separate, not
model-independent, and agreement among these GPT branches is not independent
confirmation.

I reviewed only frozen D016 and the user-authorized minimum dependencies D002,
D004--D006, D009--D010, and the prior internal critique of D010. The scope is
the exact smooth equalizer, fixed interface, duplicated bulk-trace/interface-
copy presentation, persistent planar rank-three branch, and ordinary or Haar
circle pushforward. No divided, distributional, nonbasic, moving-interface, or
full-interface-`SO(1,4)` claim is assessed.

## Frozen target

D016 claims that every five-dimensional Palatini connection-momentum row and
its derivative has zero regular pullback to `\widetilde C=C\times S^1`, hence
zero circle pushforward, at exact collapse; consequently the pre-gluing image
overlap with rank-five `dQ_C` is zero. It further claims that coupling first
enters through explicit gluing incidence/preservation, while mixed and normal
Palatini equations prevent a truncation verdict.

## Load-bearing inference

The load-bearing bridge is

\[
j_C^*dP_{AB}=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{no pre-gluing bulk/copy primary overlap},
\]

followed by the assertion that the first canonical coupling is represented by
the gluing preservation equations (27)--(31). The first implication is sound
in the declared duplicated phase space; the second is not yet established by
the displayed canonical analysis.

## Reconstruction

With the ordered-pair pairing used by D004,

\[
P_{ab}=\frac1{4\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}E^cE^dE^4,
\qquad
P_{4a}=\frac1{12\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}E^bE^cE^d.
\]

The factors arise respectively from three placements of internal `4` and from
the antisymmetric ordered sums. Differentiation gives D016 (13)--(14). At the
cut, `E^4=\delta E^4=0`; therefore the `ab` rows vanish. For `4a/a4`, pullback
leaves three circle-horizontal one-forms on the two-dimensional base `C`, while
an admissible circle-vertical coefficient vanishes by the equalizer. Thus the
regular three-form pullback, and hence either circle pushforward, is zero for
all rows and admissible tangents. Since `epsilon_+` is an independent copy
before gluing, `dP` and `dQ` act on different factors, and the claimed zero
pre-gluing overlap follows.

## Falsification attempts

1. **Ordered-pair coefficients and signs.** Direct epsilon counting supports
   `1/(4\kappa_5)` in `P_ab`, `1/(12\kappa_5)` in `P_4a`, and
   `1/(2\kappa_5)`, `1/(4\kappa_5)` in their derivatives. It also supports the
   minus sign in `C_a4` and the two mixed-torsion placements in `C_ab`.
   Equation (24)'s signs follow from `{q,p}=I`, and the `+s,+r` signs in
   (29)--(30) follow from the chosen gluing action. **No sign or factor attack
   invalidates the zero/rank result.** However, D016 should say explicitly that
   its canonical pairing itself sums ordered antisymmetric labels; if one
   instead uses only ten independent pair coordinates with no compensating
   `1/2`, the displayed canonical momenta are doubled. This is convention
   control, not a change of the conclusion.

2. **Mixed rows, tangents, and the three boundary operations.** Neither an
   admissible circle-vertical tangent nor a circle-horizontal tangent evades
   `j_C^*dP=0`. A normal-covector component is also killed by pullback. But a
   one-sided normal contraction is different:
   `j_C^*\iota_{n_s}P_{4a}` and
   `j_C^*\iota_{n_s}dP_{4a}` can be nonzero two-forms because contraction can
   remove the normal leg before restriction. Circle pushforward cannot do
   this, and zero pullback does not imply zero normal contraction. D016 mostly
   observes this distinction for the four-form Euler equations, but its broad
   wording “every row ... has zero boundary value” should be restricted to
   tangential pullback/pushforward. The mixed/normal data therefore do not
   defeat the pre-gluing zero; they do remain relevant to preservation.

3. **Canonical preservation versus covariant variation.** Equations
   (27)--(31) are plausible weak Euler equations from (5), and separation into
   smooth bulk and boundary-supported functionals gives `r=s=0` if the regular
   trace map is sufficiently surjective. They are not, however, a demonstrated
   Dirac first-preservation calculation. D016 does not split the gluing
   multiplier components, list their zero-momentum primaries, form the total
   Hamiltonian, or show which primary preservation yields each temporal and
   normal component. Nor does it derive the post-gluing Dirac bracket. Thus the
   canonical claim that coupling “first enters through gluing preservation” is
   presently supported at the action/weak-variation level and by incidence
   blocks, not by the promised canonical chain. Likewise (32)--(36) justify
   stopping because horizontal mixed connection and normal jets are
   unspecified; they do not yet prove that a persistent truncation is
   inconsistent, only that its consistency is undecided.

## Findings

- **Sound within exact scope:** all tangentially pulled Palatini momentum rows
  and derivatives vanish; ordinary and Haar pushforwards remain zero; mixed
  `a4` rows do not evade the horizontal-degree argument.
- **Revision required:** distinguish throughout (i) pullback to
  `C\times S^1`, (ii) circle pushforward to `C`, and (iii) contraction with the
  one-sided spatial normal before restriction. Only the first two are proved
  zero.
- **Revision required:** relabel (27)--(31) as weak Euler/gluing equations
  unless a genuine total-Hamiltonian preservation chain is supplied. State
  trace-surjectivity as an assumption behind `r=s=0`.
- **Revision required:** phrase the stopping result as “truncation verdict
  undecidable without mixed/normal data,” not as a demonstrated truncation
  failure.
- **D010 scope:** D010's literal shared-`p_e` core-row claim is falsified in
  D016's unreduced duplicated-trace presentation: the copy is independent and
  the regular tangential `dP` block is zero. It is **not presentation-
  independently falsified**. After imposing gluing, a reduced/distributional
  trace presentation may induce shared brackets; D016 explicitly has not
  computed that Dirac reduction. The robust correction to D010 is therefore
  presentation-dependent, while its separate exterior Gauss tangency blocker
  remains untouched.

   admissible nonzero `j_C^*\iota_{n_s}dP_{4a}` witness. This would certify the

   component Hamiltonian split of (5), including multiplier primaries and
   trace incidence. Acceptance: identify exactly which first-preservation
   equations reproduce (21)--(30), and whether eliminating the gluing pair
   creates a nonzero reduced bulk/copy bracket despite tangential `dP=0`. This
   is the discriminating claim-to-computation bridge; a pass would still not
   decide the full mixed/normal truncation or physical degree count.

## Outcome

`revision-required`

The central zero-pullback and zero-pre-gluing-overlap result survives all three
attacks in its exact duplicated regular regime. Revision is required because
the report overstates a covariant weak-variation argument as canonical first
preservation and because normal contraction and post-gluing reduction remain
distinct, unresolved operations.
