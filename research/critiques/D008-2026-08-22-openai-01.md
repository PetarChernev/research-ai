## Frozen target

D008 claims, in the fixed-interface, basic, persistent small-`SO(1,3)` sector
and the unshifted D004/D005 polarization, that the total generator is (21). Its
exterior cut moment map is

\[
J_-^{\rm tot}=-(P^-+\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\lambda),
\]

so the interface contribution `Ad_(g^-1)Q` cancels exterior `-P^-` only after

`integral_C<Q,Z>` is left unclassified pending a seed/right action and corner
polarization. No ADM split, inverse coframe, or torsionless assumption is used.

## Load-bearing inference

The decisive bridge is from the Palatini contraction (13) and the interface
contraction (19) to the exterior component of (21). If either contraction or
the Stokes orientation has the opposite sign, the asserted cancellation (24)
becomes addition and the branch's main result fails.

## Reconstruction

With D008's convention
`omega(delta_1,delta_2)=delta_1 Theta(delta_2)-delta_2 Theta(delta_1)`, direct
substitution of `delta_alpha omega=-D alpha` and
`delta_alpha P=[alpha,P]` gives

\[
\omega(\delta,\delta_\alpha)
=-\langle D\alpha,\delta P\rangle
-\langle\delta\omega,[\alpha,P]\rangle
=-d\langle\alpha,\delta P\rangle
+\langle\alpha,\delta(DP)\rangle .
\]

The last equality uses invariance of the pairing and retains
`[delta omega,P]`. Integrating over `B_-`, whose outward boundary at `s=0` has
the declared `C` orientation, yields the boundary variation
`-delta integral_C<alpha_-,P^->`. Thus the exterior cut moment is `-P^-`.

For the interface potential, inserting
`xi_alpha=alpha_+-Ad_g alpha_-` and `Z_alpha=Ad_g alpha_-` gives

\[
\Theta_C^I(\delta_\alpha)
=\int_C\bigl(\langle\Pi,\alpha_+\rangle
-\langle\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\lambda,\alpha_-\rangle\bigr),
\]

because `Pi=Q+lambda`. Adding the exterior terms produces
`-(P^-+Ad_(g^-1)lambda)`. On the matching surface,
`lambda=-Q` and `Q=g·P^-`, this is zero. Therefore (24) is not an off-shell
identity; it is the zero-level matching equation for the displayed total
Lorentz moment map. This reconstruction supports the central cancellation.

## Falsification attempts

1. **Field-space sign/order.** I recomputed (13) directly rather than relying
   on the brief invariance argument. With the stated ordering of `omega`, (13)
   and the relation `Omega(delta,delta_alpha)=delta G` are consistent. The
   potentially dangerous connection term has the correct sign. Attack did not
   invalidate the result.

2. **Orientation and dressing.** D004 has
   `o_4=o_Sigma wedge ds` for `M_-` with `s<=0`; on a future spatial cut the
   outward face at `s=0` induces the stated positive `C` orientation. Stokes
   therefore supplies `-P^-`. D005 (22) independently has the exterior
   incident sign `-delta varpi wedge P^-`. The dressing also closes:
   `Q=g·P^-` implies `Ad_(g^-1)Q=P^-`. The core cut has the opposite induced
   incidence, but its persistent pushed trace vanishes in the frozen regular
   class, so it cannot upset (24). Attack did not find a sign or frame mismatch.

3. **Flux and claimed sufficiency of remaining data.** Equation (26) does not
   conceal its main restriction: D008 explicitly limits it to complete bulk
   and interface equations and tangent linearized solutions, with outer/lateral
   flux separately controlled. It therefore cannot be used off shell or as a
   constraint-closure result. The stronger statement in D008 (483)--(489),
   however, that the *only* missing data are the seed/right-action moment map
   and corner polarization is not established by (13)--(26). D006 (30)--(31)
   exhibits Palatini/interface primary constraints and itself says a Dirac
   verdict requires secondary stabilization and the total constraint system.
   D008 supplies the Lorentz Gauss component, but it does not test stabilization
   of the connection/coframe primaries, brackets with the coframe equations or
   `H(K)=0`, or whether bulk/interface secondary conditions pair with the
   residual rank-ten block. Those effects can make the block second class even
   after a seed symmetry and corner choice are declared. This materially
   narrows the claimed blocker and requires revision.

## Findings

- **Blocking error:** none found in the central on-matching-surface
  cancellation (21)--(24).
- **Required revision:** replace the claim that only seed/right and corner data
  remain with a conditional statement. Those data are necessary to define the
  candidate symmetry and charges, but a final first-class/physical/second-class
  classification also requires the relevant Palatini/interface primary
  stabilization and mixed secondary-bracket matrix. Also distinguish the
  interface matching shell (1) from the complete bulk shell: vanishing of the
  cut moment maps uses (1), while vanishing of all of (21) additionally uses
  the bulk Gauss equations `DP=0`.
- **Checks that passed:** field-space sign/order, exterior Stokes sign, adjoint
  dressing, normalization of the planar cancellation, and the classification
  of (24) as constraint-surface rather than off-shell cancellation.
- **Residual risk:** an allowed genuine corner symplectic potential can shift
  the Hamiltonian representative, and special stabilizer sectors can change
  rank; D008 already records both limitations.
