## Reconstruction

Varying the normalized action gives
`Dq=−q⟨phi_y²⟩/f²` and `Dphi=(q²/f²)phi_yy`. The Legendre transform leaves unit temporal Hessians and adds the nonnegative spatial term `⟨q² phi_y²/(2f²)⟩` to the energy. For smooth solutions with the stated boundary conditions, the selector's energy exchange is `−∫q qdot⟨phi_y²⟩/f²`, precisely canceled by the probe's. This is conservation conditional on solution regularity, not an existence theorem. The normalized measure consistently gives both fields mass dimension one.

On `phi=0`, constant `q0` gives the symbol `−ω²+|k|²+(q0/f)²p_n²`; its spatial rank drops from four to three at zero, but the time coefficient remains nonzero. The exact autonomous background `q=vt` gives `ü_n+[|k|²+(vp_n/f)²t²]u_n=0`. A fixed mode therefore has a regular linear ODE and unique continuation through zero. The exact sector `phi=0` also permits moving finite-support selector profiles through the base wave equation. These statements establish a background-driven linear response and autonomous prepared regions, not finite-amplitude continuum crossing.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Retained sheets: does rank loss actually reduce the state space?

**Attack:** At `q=qdot=0`, choose any smooth, label-dependent base-wave data for `phi`. The selector equation remains satisfied, while each circle Fourier mode obeys a massless base wave equation. All conjugate momenta survive. There is neither a constraint nor a gauge identification to remove the tower.

**Result:** This defeats intrinsic reduction and any single-field interpretation of the exterior, but not D033's expressly operational claim. It reinforces the disclosed cost rather than discovering a contradiction. At nonzero selector, a finite-energy probe sees transverse transport; at zero it does not. That is a legitimate probe-specific operational distinction even with infinitely many retained states. Shared selector backreaction also means this is not unrestricted local five-dimensional matter dynamics, as already acknowledged.

### 2. Modewise continuation versus uniform PDE predictivity

**Attack:** Invertible temporal Hessians and smooth coefficients for every fixed `n` do not imply estimates uniform in `n`. Energy at zero controls neither `phi_y` nor the source coefficient `⟨phi_y²⟩` for nearby selector perturbations. Moreover, a transfer matrix only from `−T` to `T` can conceal large amplification at intermediate times: endpoint boundedness does not bound the entire evolution through the degeneracy. Wronskian conservation constrains the product of singular values, not their maximum.

**Result:** No contradiction to the explicitly modewise result. Full nonlinear transverse-Sobolev evolution remains open, exactly as disclosed. The proposed endpoint diagnostic has narrower decision value than the phrase “discriminate continuum predictivity” suggests: failure in a specified norm can exclude a corresponding no-loss estimate, while endpoint success cannot establish continuous evolution in that norm throughout the interval. Nor does a failed no-loss estimate exclude well-posedness with derivative loss or in another topology.

### 3. Zero stiffness versus a demonstrated strong-coupling threshold

**Attack:** At `q0=0`, temporal normalization is still regular and the cubic vertex vanishes. The quartic term remains `−(δq)² phi_y²/(2f²)`. At fixed transverse cutoff its coefficient is finite, but finiteness is not smallness: its dimensionless strength involves `p²/f²`. On shell at nonzero `a0`, low base frequency can admit `p` of order `ε/|a0|`, defeating an expansion controlled solely by `ε/f`.

**Result:** The stated nonuniformity and risk are justified; neither a ghost nor a definite quantum cutoff follows. The circle rescaling changes propagation length to `L/|a0|`, retaining the normalized measure and all states, so it does not cure this risk by eliminating species. No singular temporal-normalization argument is needed. A fixed-cutoff, finite-mode classical model survives this attack, but it is not automatically weakly coupled. D033 does not claim otherwise.
