## Load-bearing inference

The load-bearing bridge is the passage from the partial matrix (18) to the

## Reconstruction

Conditionally, the core algebra is short. If the complete reduction really
produces

\[
M=\begin{pmatrix}0&-A^T&B^T\\ A&0&0\\-B&0&A_Q\end{pmatrix},
\qquad B=DT_e,
\]

with `rank A=12`, choose any 12-row restriction `A_c` that is invertible. The
Palatini block has inverse
`K_P^{-1}=((0,A_c^{-1}),(-A_c^{-T},0))`. Since the group primary couples only
to the `C_e` half, `( -B,0)K_P^{-1}(B^T,0)^T=0`. The residual group block is
therefore `A_Q`; for nonzero simple planar `Q`, its centralizer is
`span(T_03,T_12)`, so `rank A_Q=4`. This part is basis-independent under changes
of Palatini complement that preserve the asserted one-half coupling. The
scientific issue is that D017 has not derived that hypothesis from its complete
constraint inventory.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Complete matching/gluing Dirac matrix — blocking gap

Matrix (18) mixes defining primaries with `G_e,G_varpi`, which D017 itself says
arise from preserving temporal gluing-multiplier momenta. That is permissible
only if they are explicitly treated as secondary constraints in a staged Dirac
matrix. More importantly, (18) includes `p_epsilon+` but omits its natural
matching partner `Delta epsilon`; it likewise omits the corresponding
`(p_Omega+,Delta Omega)` block and relevant multiplier/group rows. D017 then
imposes matching by a configuration pullback rather than displaying the Dirac
bracket of this completed system.

Because `Delta epsilon=epsilon_+-g epsilon_-` and `Delta Omega` depend on `g`
(and the latter on derivatives of `g`), these omitted rows bracket the group
primaries as well as the copy momenta. Eliminating `(p_epsilon-,G_e)` first does
not by itself prove that subsequent matching transfers `D` only to the exterior
`C_e` row, with no induced connection-side or group-block term. Core gluing,
matching reactions, and the group constraints may be harmless, but that is the
claim to be demonstrated, not an available premise. Thus (24), and hence the
application of (26) to the actual system, is not yet established.

**Required correction:** label primary and secondary rows separately; append
the full coframe and connection matching pairs, their multiplier momenta, and
all group-dependent brackets; then perform the stated reductions by an explicit
Dirac Schur complement (or pull back the complete presymplectic potential and
show equivalence). State exactly which core-gluing rows vanish in the regular
persistent sector and why.

### 2. Palatini complement and residual rank — conditional result survives

For a nondegenerate spatial triad, treating `A=dP` as an `18 x 12` map of rank
12 and selecting an invertible `12 x 12` row restriction is dimensionally
consistent. Given matrix (24), zero correction is algebraic and does not depend
on which such complement is selected; `rank A_Q=4` and its two-dimensional
centralizer also agree with D012 and its critique.

The basis-independence claim is nevertheless only conditional: a completed
matching reduction that induces any coupling of `Phi_v` to the `C_varpi` half
generically yields a nonzero Schur term. D017 must therefore phrase (26) as a
consequence of the fully derived reduced incidence pattern, not as protection
against omitted sectors. This attack does not find an error in (25)-(26)
themselves; it exposes the missing premise from attack 1.

### 3. “Complete first preservation” — blocking claim-to-computation failure

Equations (27)-(35) collect bulk/interface Euler equations and a matching
tangency identity, but they do not display preservation of every surviving
primary after the proposed reductions. In particular, D017 does not calculate
the preservation equations for `Phi_v`, the six unselected Palatini
`C_varpi` combinations, or the surviving matching/group/multiplier constraints.
Equation (36)

\[
0\simeq-\delta H_{red}/\delta e-A^TU_varpi+T_e^*D^TU_v
\]

is only the formal Hamilton equation for one primary. Its conclusion-critical
term is left as an undefined functional derivative said to include the very
coframe, matching, and `H(K)` linearizations whose rank is at issue; moreover,
`T_e^*` has no declared domain or pairing. Consequently (36) cannot establish
that the first preservation system is complete, nor that its rank is genuinely
underdetermined by precisely the named data rather than altered by an omitted
constraint row. The final underdetermination may be plausible, but it has not
been reached by a complete first Dirac step.

**Required correction:** specify `H_red`, the trace spaces/pairings defining
`T_e^*`, and the full surviving-primary stabilization matrix. Separate rows
that create secondaries from rows that fix multipliers, and only then report its
rank dependence on the exterior background. If those data are intentionally
left unspecified, narrow the result to a formal prospective obstruction, not a
completed first-preservation result.
