# Internal critique

## Scope and independence

**Prominent limitation:** this is a fresh-session but same-model GPT-5.6 Sol
critique of work produced by `openai/gpt-5.6-sol`. It is procedurally separate,

The bounded target is D010's equations (9)--(18), especially the claim that the

## Frozen target

D010 claims, in the fixed-interface planar basic persistent `SO(1,3)` sector
with `b=\kappa_4^{-1}`, `H(K)=0`, matching, `\lambda=-Q`, `\mu=0`, and
`Q=g\cdot P^-`, that: (i) the exterior Palatini flux cancels the interface
moment map on the shell; (ii) the five edge pairs are inseparable from the bulk
Palatini defining constraints because the same coframe-momentum rows couple to
both `d\mathcal P_+` and `dQ`; and (iii) proper-boundary gauge retains five
pairs, while extended-gauge classification requires the combined Palatini
primary matrix and its first torsion/coframe preservation step.

## Load-bearing inference

The most load-bearing inference is not flux cancellation but the bridge from
the interface rank-ten block to the asserted **core** shared-row matrix in
(11). If the regular collapsed pullback annihilates the relevant persistent
`d\mathcal P_+` columns, then the displayed common-row obstruction is not an
exact established matrix statement. The exterior Gauss tangency equation (18)
would still obstruct an edge-only extended-gauge count, but for a narrower
reason.

## Reconstruction

From D006, after eliminating `(p_\lambda,\Phi_g)`, the interface potential has
the block `\langle Q(e_+),\delta v v^{-1}\rangle`. At the planar cut,
`rank(dQ_C)=5`, so its first-order defining matrix has rank ten. From the
unreduced Palatini bulk, `\mathcal C_\omega=\pi_\omega-\mathcal P(E)` and
`\mathcal C_e=\pi_e`, hence formally
`\{\pi_e,\mathcal C_\omega\}=-d\mathcal P`. If the `e_+` entering `Q` is
literally the trace of the bulk canonical coframe, the boundary contribution
and bulk Legendre map act on one coframe momentum distribution, so independent
elimination cannot be assumed before assembling the trace map and brackets.

Separately, D005's shell gives
`B_-=-P^-_C+\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}Q_C=0`; differentiation yields (18), up
to the stated right-trivialization convention. Thus allowed `dQ` variations on
the total Gauss surface are correlated with exterior bulk-flux and frame
variations. This does establish that exterior flux cancellation alone is not a
quotient or a local degree count.

## Falsification attempts

1. **Bulk variable versus boundary trace.** D005 treats `e_+` as the one-sided
core trace used directly in the boundary action, so introducing an independent
edge coframe is not supported. However, D010 alternates between `p_{e_+}` and
`p_{e_+,C}` in (9)--(11) without defining the trace map or the distributional
canonical momentum produced by a boundary kinetic term. If these are one row,
that must be represented as a bulk momentum with a boundary-supported
contribution; if they are separate variables, a gluing constraint and its
brackets are missing. **Outcome:** the qualitative warning against additive
rank is plausible, but (11) is not yet an exact canonical matrix statement.

2. **Survival and overlap of core `d\mathcal P_+`.** For persistent
`ab` components, D004's five-dimensional momentum contains the collapsed
coframe factor `E^4=\rho\eta`. Its regular pushed boundary pullback is zero.
Moreover, tangent variations preserving the equalizer have
`\delta E^4|_\Sigma=0`; variations of persistent `e^a` leave an `E^4` factor in
`d\mathcal P_+^{ab}`. Therefore the boundary-pulled persistent `ab` derivative
appears to vanish on exactly the five `dQ` coframe directions. A surviving
mixed `4a` momentum could couple to persistent coframes, but that lies outside
the retained `SO(1,3)` connection sector unless its reduction and primary rows
are explicitly included. **Outcome:** D010 has not shown that the relevant
core Palatini rows survive the regular collapse or overlap the five `dQ`
directions; the available formulas point against the stated overlap for the
persistent `ab` block. This materially narrows the claimed blocker.

3. **Count from boundary gauge choice.** Equation (17) is a shell cancellation,
and (18) is only its tangent condition. They correctly rule out inferring gauge
removal from zero total flux. But `\alpha|_C=0` establishes only that proper
Lorentz transformations do not quotient edge boundary values. It does not show
that all five interface pairs survive matching-primary preservation,
`H(K)=0`, or the frozen non-Lorentz Palatini chain. D010 acknowledges this in
qualifiers but later states unconditionally that five pairs remain. **Outcome:**
the proper-gauge result must be phrased as an unreduced boundary presymplectic
count, not a completed local physical count. For extended gauge, (18) does
support nonfactorization with the **exterior** bulk sector, but does not by
itself identify which full preservation step is sufficient.

## Findings

- **Revision required:** the exact shared-row claim involving the core
  `d\mathcal P_+` block is not established with the regular trace map and may
  vanish on the five relevant persistent coframe directions.
- **Revision required:** the notation `p_{e_+}` versus `p_{e_+,C}` hides the
  canonical trace/gluing choice on which the matrix claim depends.
- **Revision required:** “five pairs remain” under proper gauge is justified
  only as the interface cut/polarization count before the remaining constraint
  chain, not as a final physical count.
- **Surviving result:** the exterior sign and shell relation support (17), and
  (18) is sufficient to show that total-flux cancellation alone cannot classify
  or remove the edge block.
