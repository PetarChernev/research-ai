# Internal critique

## Scope and independence

**Prominent limitation:** this is a fresh-session but same-model GPT-5.6 Sol
internal critique. It is procedurally separate, not model-independent, and is
not independent verification. Agreement with D007 or its same-model
dependencies is not independent confirmation.

The bounded target is D007's exterior cut generator and shell cancellation,
including the Palatini momentum normalization and sign. I inspected only D007,
the permitted equations in D005/D006, and D004's incident Palatini sign.

## Frozen target

D007 claims, in the local fixed-interface, basic, small-`SO(1,3)`, rank-three
regime with ordered antisymmetric pairs and `dt`-first orientation, that the
total polarization is (13), the improved generators are (22), and on

\[
\pi^-\simeq P^-,\qquad \lambda=-Q,\qquad Q=g\cdot P^-
\]

the exterior coefficient cancels as
`-P^- - Ad_(g^-1)lambda=0`. It further claims that the rank-ten edge block
shares coframe-primary rows with the bulk Palatini system, and that final
classification requires stabilization/torsion and seed/corner polarization.

## Load-bearing inference

The load-bearing inference is that the interface moment map
`-Ad_(g^-1)lambda` and the exterior bulk improvement `-pi^-|_C` occur with the
displayed relative sign and equal normalization, so their weak sum vanishes on
the D005 shell. If this fails, the principal advance over D006—a completed
exterior moment map rather than a residual interface charge—fails.

## Reconstruction

From D005 (21), the exterior curvature term is

\[
{1\over4\kappa_4}\epsilon_{abcd}{\cal R}^{ab}\wedge e^c\wedge e^d.
\]

With `R=dt wedge (dot(varpi)-D varpi_t)+...`, `dt` first, and ordered
antisymmetric-pair summation, its kinetic term is exactly

\[
\int_{Y_-}\Pi^-_{ab}\wedge\dot{\boldsymbol\varpi}^{ab},\qquad
\Pi^-_{ab}={1\over4\kappa_4}\epsilon_{abcd}\mathbf e^c\wedge\mathbf e^d.
\]

No extra factor of two arises: the `1/4` already uses both ordered placements,
as in D005/D006. Since a one-form and a two-form commute under wedge exchange,
D007's `delta varpi wedge Pi^-` is the same canonical normalization.

For `delta_alpha varpi=-D alpha`, the direct canonical generator contains
`-int_Y <pi^-,D alpha>`. Covariant integration by parts gives

\[
-\int_{Y_-}\langle\pi^-,D\alpha\rangle
=\int_{Y_-}\langle\alpha,D\pi^-\rangle
-\int_{\partial Y_-}\langle\alpha,\pi^-\rangle .
\]

The spatial cut orientation induced from D004 is consistent with this minus
sign: although the spacetime exterior face has orientation `-o_Sigma`, the
`dt`-first spatial slice has interface boundary orientation `+o_C`. D006 gives
the exterior interface moment map `J_-^I=-Ad_(g^-1)lambda`. Hence the total
exterior coefficient is

\[
-\pi^-|_C-\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\lambda.
\]

It is legitimate only as a weak shell substitution to set the independent
canonical momentum to its primary value `pi^-\simeq Pi^-`, whose trace is
`P^-`. Then `lambda=-Q` and `Q=g\cdot P^-` imply
`Ad_(g^-1)Q=P^-`, so the coefficient vanishes. Thus the cancellation, including
its normalization and relative sign, is correct in the stated conventions.
It does not by itself prove that all admitted boundary transformations are
gauge or that the rank-ten form is unphysical.

## Falsification attempts

1. **Incidence, orientation, and ordered-pair attack — passed.** D004's
spacetime incident minus sign is compatible, not in conflict, with the
spatial-boundary sign used in the canonical integration by parts. The ordered
pair convention also preserves the `1/(4 kappa_4)` momentum. A single-term
orientation reversal would break the result, but a consistent common
reorientation changes both represented cut contributions together.

2. **Shared-row/non-direct-sum attack — revision needed.** Identifying `e_+`
with the trace of the bulk coframe does establish the important qualitative
point: `Q(e_+|_C)` is not a function of an unrelated edge coordinate, so its
bracket is boundary-supported in the same bulk coframe canonical system.
However, D007 alternates between an intrinsic trace (where there is no separate
`p_(e_+)`) and duplicated interface variables (where gluing constraints and
their brackets are required). Equation (16) and schematic (25) do not specify
the distributional trace phase space or the complete duplicated-variable
constraint matrix. They therefore justify “naive rank addition is invalid,”
but not yet the stronger presentation-independent assertion that no canonical
change/elimination can isolate a direct summand. This does not damage the flux
cancellation, but the structural conclusion should be narrowed or supplied
with one explicit phase-space presentation.

3. **Closure attack — revision needed.** Equation (24) is stronger than what
is displayed. To rotate every primary covariantly, a full canonical generator
must include actions on multiplier variables and momenta (and, depending on the
chosen trace presentation, gluing variables); these terms are not explicit in
(22). Corner admissibility and the seed transformation are assumed rather than
encoded. Covariance makes closure plausible and the text appropriately calls
the system only a candidate, but it does not establish the exact brackets in
(24) with no boundary extension. This overclaim likewise does not reverse the
weak cancellation in (23).

## Findings

- **Check passed:** the exterior Palatini momentum, cut sign, dressing sign,
  and D005 shell imply the stated weak cancellation.
- **Required revisions:** narrow the non-direct-summand claim to the
  demonstrated shared-variable/rank-nonadditivity statement unless a complete
  trace/gluing presentation is given; label (24) as conditional or display the
  omitted canonical terms and corner assumptions needed for it.
- **Residual physical risk:** zero total cut coefficient rules out treating
  the interface moment map alone as a physical charge, but does not prove a
  boundary gauge quotient or eliminate the rank-ten sector.
- **No blocking error** was found in the load-bearing cancellation itself.

## Machine-check candidates

After one trace-variable presentation is declared, a finite local exact
Poisson-matrix check could encode all bulk-boundary coframe, gluing, multiplier,
and edge canonical pairs. It should (a) reproduce the shared rows and test
whether the isolated rank-ten block can actually be block-separated, and (b)
compute the brackets claimed in (24), including possible boundary remainders.
Acceptance requires the displayed closure and absence of an unaccounted cut
term for arbitrary allowed parameters. Such a check would discriminate the two
unresolved exact assertions, but would still not settle stabilization, corner
gauge status, or the physical Dirac classification.

## Outcome

`revision-required`

The central exterior cancellation is correct and properly weak, but D007
overstates the exactness of the shared-row/direct-sum conclusion and Lorentz
closure before fixing the boundary-trace canonical presentation and omitted
primary/corner terms.
