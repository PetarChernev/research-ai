## Reconstruction

Integrating the dimensionless circle angle gives 2πaρ₅ per base volume. Subtraction of a fixed exterior vacuum reference leaves Δε=2πaρ₅−ρ₄. For a spherical wall, its induced proper worldvolume element is 4πR²√(1−v²)dt, so

\[
L=-4\pi\sigma R^2/\gamma-(4\pi/3)\Delta\epsilon R^3,
\qquad p=4\pi\sigma R^2\gamma v.
\]

The Euler–Lagrange equation, divided by 4πσ, is

\[
R^2\gamma^3\ddot R+2R\gamma v^2+2R/\gamma
+(\Delta\epsilon/\sigma)R^2=0.
\]

Since γv²+γ⁻¹=γ, this gives (4). The Legendre transform is E=4πσR²γ+(4π/3)ΔεR³. Its derivative is 4πσv times the same residual with the two curvature terms combined. Thus (4) implies energy conservation without division by v. The positive Hessian 4πσR²γ³ and smooth acceleration on R>0, |v|<1 give local radial evolution from admissible initial data. This reconstruction supports the stated restricted result, not a full mixed-dimensional initial-value problem.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Wrong pressure sign, dimensional normalization, or turning-point degeneracy

An expanding domain replaces exterior energy by interior energy; its volume contribution must therefore be +ΔεV in E and −ΔεV in L. These signs agree. The circle factor has length dimension, so aρ₅ and ρ₄ both have dimension length⁻⁴; σ has dimension length⁻³. Consequently E has dimension length⁻¹ and Δε/σ has the acceleration dimension length⁻¹. At rest the force gives R̈=−2/R−Δε/σ, correctly inward for positive excess density. Energy conservation alone loses the acceleration equation at v=0, but D027 derives that equation variationally first. **No defect found in this attack.**

### 2. Conserved finite energy mistaken for stable autonomous matter

For Δε<0 the rest energy is unbounded below as R grows. Its stationary radius R*=−2σ/Δε has E″=−8πσ, despite positive kinetic Hessian. For Δε=0, a wall released from rest has 1−v²=(R/R₀)⁴ and approaches the excluded collapse/null limit. Thus conserved energy and positive inertia cannot imply stability, global continuation, or formation from empty space. D027 explicitly makes these exclusions and reports the instability. **This narrows applicability but does not contradict its target:** local dynamics and an excess-energy observable do not require a stable particle.

### 3. Topological circle assignment mistaken for derived dimensional physics

Let two distinct fiber points lie at normal distance δ from the collapsed incidence locus. A path via that locus costs at most 2δ, irrespective of their fixed-radius angular separation. Therefore the quotient cannot simply inherit uniform product-circle global distances up to the wall. Independently assigning its wall measure nevertheless defines the effective functional; it does not resolve metric descent or field transfer. Moreover, all radial predictions depend on the interior only through Δε: varying a and ρ₅ while keeping aρ₅ fixed leaves this witness unchanged. No radial energy measurement here distinguishes a fifth direction from a conventional phase bubble.

**This is a real promotion barrier, already disclosed, not a false claim.** It blocks a predictive intrinsic dimensional interface or operational dimensional probe. It does not block the user's relaxed formulation-level target of a non-prescribed region with dynamics plus calculable energy: the varied region, assigned topology, and effective energy satisfy that narrower mapping role.
