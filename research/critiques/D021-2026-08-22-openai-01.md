## Frozen target

For
`rho in C^1([0,epsilon)) intersect C^2((0,epsilon))`, with
`rho(0)=rho'_+(0)=0` and `rho(s)>0` for `s>0`, D021 claims

\[
\limsup_{s\downarrow0}\frac{\rho''}{\rho}=+\infty,

## Load-bearing inference

The decisive inference is that any finite endpoint-neighborhood upper bound
`rho''/rho <= M` contradicts the two zero endpoint data and positivity. If this
comparison step failed, neither the universal limsup nor the curvature
conclusion would follow.

## Reconstruction

Assume `rho''/rho <= M` on `(0,delta)`. Positivity permits multiplication by
`rho` without reversing the inequality: `rho'' <= M rho`. Choose
`lambda>=0` with `lambda^2>=max(M,0)`. Then

\[
\rho''-\lambda^2\rho\le (M-\lambda^2)\rho\le0,
\]

and therefore

\[
\left[e^{-\lambda s}(\rho'+\lambda\rho)\right]'
=e^{-\lambda s}(\rho''-\lambda^2\rho)\le0.
\]

On every compact punctured interval this can be integrated classically. Since
`rho,rho'` extend continuously to zero with zero values, taking the lower
endpoint to zero gives `rho'+lambda rho<=0`. Hence
`(e^{lambda s}rho)'<=0`, and a second endpoint limit gives `rho(s)<=0`, a
contradiction. Thus the ratio has no finite upper bound on any endpoint
neighborhood; the nested-neighborhood definition of extended-real limsup then
gives `+infinity`.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Comparison proof, endpoint step, and minimal regularity

The signs survive for all finite real `M`: if `M<0`, `lambda=0` is allowed; if
`M>=0`, choosing `lambda^2>=M` makes both inequalities point the required way.
Positivity is used twice in substance: to convert the ratio bound to
`rho''<=M rho`, and to ensure `(M-lambda^2)rho<=0`. The endpoint passage uses
only continuity of `rho` and `rho'`, not boundedness or endpoint continuity of
`rho''`. Punctured `C^2` is sufficient because integration occurs first on
`[a,s]` with `a>0`. No hidden uniqueness theorem or division at `s=0` is
needed. This attack found no analytic defect.

### 2. Oscillatory witness and dimensional consistency

Writing `a=3/4`, `t=s^-2`, and `L=log rho=-s^-2+a sin t` gives

\[
L'=2s^{-3}(1-a\cos t),\qquad
\frac{\rho''}{\rho}
=4s^{-6}\left[(1-a\cos t)^2-a\sin t\right]
-6s^{-4}(1-a\cos t),
\]

so D021's differentiation is correct. At
`(sin t,cos t)=(4/5,3/5)`, the leading coefficient is `-119/400`; at
`t=3pi/2 mod 2pi`, it is `1+a>0`. The displayed positive sequences approach
zero and establish respectively liminf `-infinity` and limsup `+infinity`.
The exponential is strictly positive, and exponential decay dominates every
polynomial/trigonometric derivative, so its zero extension is smooth and flat.

There is, however, a required dimensional correction. D021 later declares
`[s]=[rho]=L`, but (13) has a dimensionful exponent and a dimensionless radius;
its subsequences likewise omit a length scale. A physically dimensionally
consistent witness is

\[
\rho_{\rm osc}(s)=\ell\exp\!\left[-(L/s)^2+	frac34
\sin((L/s)^2)\right],\qquad \ell,L>0,
\]

with sequences `s_n=L(alpha+2pi n)^(-1/2)` and
`tilde s_n=L(3pi/2+2pi n)^(-1/2)`. The corrected derivative has leading term
`4L^4 s^-6[(1-a cos t)^2-a sin t]` and subleading term
`-6L^2 s^-4(1-a cos t)`, so all sharpness conclusions are unchanged.

### 3. Curvature sign, lapse chain rule, and scope

For `E^3=ds`, `E^4=rho dtheta`, torsionlessness gives
`omega^{43}=rho' dtheta`, hence
`R^{34}=d(-rho'dtheta)=-rho'' ds wedge dtheta
=-(rho''/rho)E^{34}`. Thus D021's sign is correct: positive excursions of
`rho''/rho` produce arbitrarily negative `K_{s theta}`. With `ds=N dx`,

\[
\rho_s=\rho_x/N,\qquad
\rho_{ss}=N^{-2}\left(\rho_{xx}-(N_x/N)\rho_x\right),
\]

so the lapse powers, connection term, and overall curvature sign are correct.
The invariance statement is valid for the stated adapted radial charts with a
finite nonzero endpoint Jacobian. D021 appropriately treats `N->0`,
`N->infinity`, or insufficient differentiability as outside that regular
chart-equivalence class; it does not establish what singular charts mean in a
future generalized-space category. Its C023 statement is limited to
generalizing the radial-circle geometric input, not C023's stress, NEC,
integrability, or actual-limit conclusions, and its H003 wording remains a
restricted kinematical obstruction rather than a universal no-go. No scope
creep requiring correction was found.

## Findings
