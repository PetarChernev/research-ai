## Frozen target

D024 claims, in the fixed-interface, basic rank-three planar polar ansatz, that
a unit spacelike reduction field determines the unique minimal connection

\[
\Omega_V^{AB}=\omega^{AB}+V^AK^B-K^AV^B,
\qquad K^A=D_\omega V^A,
\]

subject to both conditions (8). Substitution in (2) gives (14); its dependent
variation (18)--(20) cancels only the coset part of D004's connection momentum
as in (27). On (30), the transverse reduction-field equation is satisfied, but
the independent coframe equation gives (34)--(35), excluding every `c>0`
member. The claim is explicitly restricted to this one primitive, the frozen
D005 shell, and the stated rank/basic/regularity regime.

## Load-bearing inference

The decisive bridge is not merely the cancellation of the `43` connection
row. It is the implication

\[
\text{dependent }\Omega_V
\Longrightarrow B_V=-C^{AB}P_{AB}
\Longrightarrow
\bigl(\delta\omega\text{ coset row cancels},\;
\delta E^C J_C\text{ remains}\bigr)
\Longrightarrow c=0.
\]

If the dependent variation supplied another coframe term, if the projector
left a `43` connection residual, or if an on-shell D005 chain-rule term could
cancel `J_C`, the negative result would fail.

## Reconstruction

Let `h=1-V V_flat` and `K=DV`, with `V.K=0`. Writing
`X=Omega_V-omega`, the two conditions (8) require `X V=-K` and
`hXh=0`. An antisymmetric tensor decomposes uniquely into stabilizer and coset
parts, so these conditions give

\[
X^{AB}=V^AK^B-K^AV^B,
\quad C^{AB}=(\omega-\Omega_V)^{AB}=2K^{[A}V^{B]}.
\]

The index placement is consistent: `(Omega-omega)^A{}_B V^B=-K^A`.
Because `K` transforms as a vector, `Omega_V` has the inhomogeneous connection
law and `C` transforms adjointly. Contracting the antisymmetric ordered pair
gives `C^{AB}P_AB=2K^AV^BP_AB`, hence (14).

Independent variation then yields

\[
\delta B_V=-2\!\int[(D\delta V^A+\delta\omega^A{}_CV^C)V^BP_{AB}
+K^A\delta V^BP_{AB}+K^AV^B\delta P_{AB}].
\]

Integration by parts gives the minus corner in (18) and
`F_A=2[V^B DP_AB+2K^B P_AB]`. The connection coefficient equals
`-delta omega^{AB} P^perp_AB`; therefore adding D004 leaves precisely
`delta omega^{AB} P^parallel_AB`, not zero generically. In adapted gauge,
`V=e4`, this cancels all `a4` rows. On the polar trace only `P43` and `P34`
survive, so the entire connection row cancels there.

Finally, both ordered components, `P43=-oSigma/(2 kappa5)` and
`P34=+oSigma/(2 kappa5)`, give

\[
B_V=-{2\pi c\over\kappa_5}\int_\Sigma o_\Sigma,
\qquad
\delta(S_++B_V)=-{2\pi c\over\kappa_5}\int_\Sigma\delta o_\Sigma.
\]

A compactly supported rank-preserving variation
`delta e^i=alpha e^i` has `delta oSigma=3 alpha oSigma`; free coframe
stationarity therefore excludes `c>0` (formally, the residual coefficient sets
`c=0`).

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Alternate direct variation in adapted gauge

I fixed `V=e4` only after deriving the covariant `V` equation. Directly in that
gauge, `Omega^{ab}=omega^{ab}`, `Omega^{a4}=0`, and the primitive is exactly the
coset Legendre term. Its `delta omega^{a4}` coefficient is minus the D004
coset momentum, while its coframe variation remains `-C delta P`. Explicit
ordered evaluation reproduces (31)--(34), including the factor two, the odd
fiber-form move, and the negative sign. The ungauge-fixed equation gives on
the polar background `F_4=-2c oSigma wedge dtheta/kappa5` and no transverse
component, so gauge fixing did not discard an equation that could rescue
`c>0`. **Attack failed.**

### 2. Generic projector/index test

For an arbitrary antisymmetric `P`, expansion of
`P^perp=P-hhP` gives

\[
\delta\omega^{AB}P^\perp_{AB}
=2\delta\omega^{AB}V_BV^CP_{AC}.
\]

This is exactly the negative of the connection term in (18), establishing
(27) with the ordered-pair convention. The uniqueness argument for (9) also
survives: a difference of two solutions has zero contraction with `V` and,
being then purely stabilizer-valued, is killed by its zero double projection.
The uniqueness is conditional on both parts of (8), as D024 states, and is not
uniqueness among all local induced-connection prescriptions. **Attack failed.**

### 3. Shell/covariance and limiting-case attack

If the covariantized D005 action is genuinely the frozen D005 functional
composed with the projected coframe and `Omega_V`, its chain-rule contributions
are sums of D005 Euler coefficients times derivatives of those composites.
Every such coefficient vanishes separately on the displayed shell (29), so
these terms vanish there and cannot cancel the nonzero D024 coframe residual.
This argument is on-shell and does not justify dropping those terms off shell;
D024 observes that limitation.

This attack did find one definite sign error. With D024's own convention

\[
\delta B=\int(\delta\omega\wedge {\cal B}_\omega
+\delta E\wedge {\cal B}_E+\delta V\,{\cal B}_V)
\]

and diamond definition (6), `B_E` is a three-form, so
`(alpha E) wedge B_E = - B_E wedge (alpha E)`. Integrating
`-D alpha wedge B_omega` by parts therefore gives

\[
\boxed{D{\cal B}_\omega-{\cal B}_E\diamond E
+{\cal B}_V\diamond V=0},
\]

not the all-plus identity in (25). The multiplier contribution still vanishes
by antisymmetry. This is a real covariance/normalization defect in an asserted
exact equation, but it is not used to derive (27) or (35). **Attack narrows the
result only by requiring revision of (25) and dependent commentary/checks.**

## Findings

### Revision required

1. **D024 (25) has the wrong sign on the coframe diamond term** under the
   explicit variation order in the paragraph before (24) and definition (6).
   The corrected identity is displayed above. Corresponding statements in
   D024 (587)--(589) remain qualitatively true, but the formula and proposed
   machine assertion at (621)--(622) need correction.

### Checks that passed

1. Equations (9), (11)--(14) are unique and gauge-covariant under the stated
   minimality condition, with consistent raised/lowered contractions.
2. Equations (15)--(20), including the factor `2` in the second `K P` term and
   the minus corner sign, are consistent.
3. Equation (27) leaves the stabilizer projection and cancels only the coset;
   it vanishes on the polar trace because its sole momentum is coset-valued.
4. Equations (31)--(35) have the correct ordered-pair factor, orientation, and
   independent-coframe consequence. More precisely, `c=0` is the formal Euler
   coefficient and lies outside the stipulated positive-slope core, so the
   result is exclusion of that branch rather than construction of a valid
   zero-slope member.
5. The projected D005 chain-rule terms vanish only on the complete D005 shell
   and cannot cancel (34) there. The transverse `V` equation was retained
   before adapted gauge and is zero on the benchmark.

### Nonblocking scope qualifications

The result is only a no-go for primitive (2) with minimal connection (9), the
fixed interface, D022 finite one-sided tangent class, frozen D005 composition,
and basic planar rank-three polar ansatz. It does not exclude added `V,K,R`
terms, another polarization, nonbasic modes, moving interfaces, or alternative
lower-regularity calculi. D024 mostly states these boundaries accurately and
does not establish a broad C002/C003 result.

of the no-go.
