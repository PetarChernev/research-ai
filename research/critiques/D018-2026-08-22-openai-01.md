## Reconstruction

On `s>0`, the D004 connection equation is injective for an invertible
five-coframe and gives `T^A=0`. Since

\[
dE^4=(\rho'/\rho)E^3\wedge E^4,
\]

Cartan's equation fixes

\[
\omega^{43}=+(\rho'/\rho)E^4=+\rho'd\theta,
\quad \omega^{34}=-\rho'd\theta.
\]

With `R=domega+omega wedge omega`, the quadratic term vanishes and

\[
R^{43}=+\rho''ds\wedge d\theta,
\quad R^{34}=-\rho''ds\wedge d\theta
=kE^3\wedge E^4,
\quad k=-\rho''/\rho.
\]

For each `A=0,1,2`, the curvature sum in D004 has four equal ordered terms:
two orders of `(B,C)=(3,4)` and two orders of the remaining coframe pair.
The prefactor `1/4` therefore leaves `k Xi_A/kappa_5`. The 24 permutations in
the cosmological term cancel its `1/24`, leaving
`-Lambda_5 Xi_A/kappa_5`. For `A=3,4`, curvature necessarily repeats an
epsilon index, so only `-Lambda_5 Xi_A/kappa_5` remains. Since all `Xi_A` are
nonzero on the open core, D018's chain follows exactly. A smooth scalar that is
identically zero on an open half-neighborhood has `rho(0)=rho'(0)=0`; the
affine open-core solution then has both integration constants zero.
