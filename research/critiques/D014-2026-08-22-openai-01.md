quantified class of N**, rather than merely a formally assignable cut one-form.

## Reconstruction

Assume a genuine omitted bulk contribution has pullback
`Theta_B=c<P^-,zeta>` and that tangent shell variations obey
`P^-=p`, `delta P^-=delta p`. Then linearity of field-space differentiation
does give

\[
\iota^*\Theta_{\rm tot}=(1+c)\Theta_E,
\quad
\iota^*\Omega_{\rm tot}=(1+c)\Omega_E,
\quad
J_{\rm tot}=(1+c)p.
\]

Thus `c=-1` cancels the displayed shell potential, two-form, and partial moment
map. The missing logical step is existential: covariance, dimensions, and
cut-locality of the proposed one-form do not establish an underlying spacetime
action, its differentiable Gauss generator, its companion variations, or
preservation of the D005 shell.

## Falsification attempts

1. **Stabilizer/generator count — material error.** D014 says D006 establishes
   a one-dimensional Lorentz stabilizer and therefore five effective orbit
   directions. D006 establishes instead that `dQ` has rank five and that its
   one-dimensional left kernel is generated by `T_12`; this is a structural
   null of the restricted momentum map, not the full coadjoint stabilizer. For
   planar `p` proportional to `T_03` (under the invariant identification), both
   `T_03` and `T_12` commute with `p`, so the coadjoint stabilizer is
   two-dimensional and the coadjoint orbit has dimension four. This does not
   prove that only four first-class generators can act on the full reduced edge
   space—`u` may transform even when `p` is fixed—but it invalidates D014's
   stated route to `r=5`. Five independent regular generators must be derived
   from the total action, not inferred as `6-1` from D006.

2. **F1 admissibility — algebra passes, counter-completion does not.** If (12)
   is granted as the complete pulled-back bulk potential, the `c=-1`
   cancellation is exact, dimensionally consistent, and compatible with the
   matching normalization. But D005's central variational lesson is that a
   desired field-space one-form cannot be treated in isolation: its coframe,
   connection/reference, and edge companion variations affect the Euler
   problem. Moreover `delta Theta_B` is nonzero generically, so `Theta_B`
   cannot simply be an exact variation of an added boundary functional; it
   would need to arise from a genuine first-order bulk/corner decomposition.
   D005 supplies the exterior incident sign `-delta varpi wedge P^-`, but not
   the asserted `u`-dressed kinetic term or its total Gauss generator. D014
   explicitly admits that no spacetime action realizing F1 was constructed.
   Consequently F1 is a useful algebraic cancellation ansatz, but it is not yet
   an instance of N's quantified “local differentiable Lorentz Gauss
   completion whose Euler shell contains” the stated equations. It therefore
   does not refute N as written.

3. **F2 first-class completion — conclusion assumed.** F2 assumes five regular
   constraints, closure, proper boundary parameters, compatible moment-map
   cancellation, and Hamiltonian vectors spanning a Lagrangian five-plane.
   Those are essentially the properties needed for zero-dimensional symplectic
   reduction. Equation `10-2r=0` then checks the conditional dimension count,
   but F2 supplies neither constraints nor brackets and is not a constructed
   countermodel. The erroneous stabilizer identification further removes its
   advertised structural motivation. F2 establishes only logical possibility
   under explicitly conclusion-bearing assumptions.

## Findings

- **Revision required:** relabel F1 as a formal cancellation ansatz showing
  underdetermination, not an admissible counter-completion refuting N, unless a
  spacetime action and differentiable total Gauss generator preserving the
  shell are supplied.
- **Revision required:** separate the rank-five `dQ` fact and `T_12` structural
  null from the two-dimensional coadjoint stabilizer; withdraw the inference
  that D006 itself supplies five effective first-class generators.
- **Revision required:** present F2 only as a conditional reduction template,
  not an explicit favorable completion.
- **Surviving result:** the actual Palatini classification remains
  inconclusive, and D014 correctly identifies the total cut polarization,
  Gauss generator, corner gauge group, regularity, and closure as missing.
  D014 also validly shows that the interface rank and partial moment map alone
  do not establish the physical classification. What fails is the stronger
  claim that a universal N, as quantified over genuine completions, has already
  been refuted.
