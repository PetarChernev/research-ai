# Internal critique

## Scope and independence

**Prominent limitation: this is a fresh-session but same-model
`openai/gpt-5.6-sol` re-critique of work produced by
`openai/gpt-5.6-sol`. It is procedurally separate, not model-independent, and
is not independent verification.** I inspected only the frozen packet, did not
execute O014, and do not count its same-model controls as independent evidence.
The question is whether the implementation defects in the first critique were
repaired, not whether C027 is physically complete.

## Frozen target

C027 is a restricted derived claim: under its fixed-interface, ordered-pair,
unit-spacelike-`V`, single-primitive, frozen-D005, and basic rank-three polar
assumptions, the minimality conditions give
`Omega_V=omega+V K-K V`; variation of `B_V=-2 integral K^A V^B P_AB(E)`
cancels only coset connection rows; and the polar specialization cancels the
`43/34` row but leaves a coframe equation forcing `c=0`, with transverse `V`
row and basic corner pullback zero. O014 declares only a finite exact local
algebra check. Tangent admissibility, global sectors, total moment maps,
alternative actions, functional preservation, and C002/C003 are expressly out
of scope.

## Load-bearing inference

The most load-bearing bridge is that one differentiated primitive graph,
after graded integration by parts, supplies Euler coefficients which combine
with an independently assembled D004 row to equal the double-projected
stabilizer momentum `hhP`. This bridge distinguishes a genuine dependent-`V`
calculation from target insertion and supports both the generic coset-only
cancellation and its polar consequence.

## Reconstruction

`primitive_graph()` now builds `-2 sum K^A V^B P_AB`. `vary_graph()` applies a
Leibniz rule, with `delta K^A=D(delta V^A)+delta omega^A{}_C V^C` and an
epsilon/wedge derivative of `P(E)`. `integrate_dkdv()` converts
`-2 D(delta V^A) V^B P_AB` into the boundary coefficient
`-2 delta V^A V^B P_AB` and bulk contribution
`2 delta V^A D(V^B P_AB)`; adding the direct `delta V` term yields
`2[V^B DP_AB+2K^B P_AB]`. Coefficients are then extracted from that graph and
compared with separately assembled D024 rows. This is a substantive repair of
the former manually inserted variation and IBP results.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Expression graph, graded signs, and generic `hhP`

The graph/IBP path is now real, and the `E`, `V`, and corner targets are
separately assembled rather than aliases of extracted rows. Shared helpers and
conventions still correlate the comparisons, but they are no longer the prior
tautologies. The graded coefficient extraction gives the D024 signs: moving
`delta E` past degree-one `K` changes the primitive's minus sign, while IBP
leaves a negative corner coefficient.

However, the supposedly independent `hhP` target has a hidden index-placement
error. `independent_hhp_matrix()` uses
`2*h[i][a]*h[b][j]*P[a][b]`, where `h` stores `h^A{}_B`. For a lower-index
momentum paired with `delta omega^{ij}`, D024 requires
`2 h_i{}^a h_j{}^b P_ab`; the omitted metric factors matter when `V` has a
timelike component. The chosen test vector `(0,3/5,0,0,4/5)` has no timelike
component and masks the defect.

A decisive exact counterexample is the unit spacelike vector
`V=(3/4,0,0,0,5/4)`. For the `01` row, the graph/D004 residual and the correctly
lowered projection contain
`(25/8) P01-(15/8) P14`, whereas the implemented `hhP` target gives
`(25/8) P01+(15/8) P14`. Thus clause (iii) is not correctly represented over
the stated `R^(1,4)` domain. This is a residual implementation defect, not a
declared finite-scope physics limitation.

### 2. Primitive support, normalization, and polar conclusions

The repaired polar path derives `P43=-oSigma/2` and `P34=+oSigma/2` from the
explicit coframe and epsilon, derives `K` from concrete `omega` and `V`, and
substitutes those data into the graph-derived bulk and boundary expressions.
Both ordered contributions produce `B/c=-1`; D004 and primitive connection
rows are separately nonzero and sum to zero. The graph-derived common coframe
rescaling is `-3c alpha oY` (its printed `+6*scale*theta*x0*x1*x2` at `c=2`
reflects canonical reordering of `oSigma wedge theta`), so positive `c` is
excluded. Transverse `V` zero, a nonzero longitudinal control, and corner zero
follow from concrete substitution and pullback rather than Boolean or target
assignments. No hidden polar target insertion or factor-of-two defect was found.

### 3. Noether contraction, mutations, and canonical record

The Noether routine contracts all three extracted rows with one
metric-antisymmetric `alpha`, then imposes `P(E)` and `DP=D[P(E)]`. Its three
pieces are separately nonzero, the graded residual is zero, and the all-plus
ordering leaves a 120-term residual. All six mutations now change upstream
semantics: the `delta omega V` branch of `delta K`, the IBP product rule, the
boundary rule, the primitive's dependent-connection graph, one ordered polar
momentum, or Noether grading. Unchanged downstream assertions detect each with
exit 1. This repairs the first critique's mutation objection.

## Findings

The first critique's central graph differentiation, IBP, polar derivation,
Noether, and semantic-mutation defects were substantially repaired. The trust
boundary is appropriately stated as a small custom exact-rational graded
kernel with shared OpenAI provenance, no CAS, floats, or sampling; a pass tests
only encoded finite algebra and cannot establish the scientific claim by
itself.

One conclusion-critical defect remains: the generic D004-to-`hhP` target has
incorrect index lowering and is tested on a vector that hides it. Therefore
O014 does not yet satisfy its generic projection criterion, despite the
canonical pass. The README's stale-result sentence should also be corrected,
but it is not the scientific blocker. The declared physical omissions remain
finite-scope limitations, not implementation failures.
