`(alpha E) wedge B_E=-B_E wedge (alpha E)`, while integrating
`-D alpha wedge B_omega` gives the positive `D B_omega` term. The corrected
identity is therefore exactly

\[
D{\cal B}_\omega-{\cal B}_E\diamond E
+{\cal B}_V\diamond V=0.
\]

Revised (25), its explanation, the covariance checklist, revision history, and
machine-check proposal all use this sign. The multiplier contribution remains
zero by antisymmetry. No stale all-plus assertion remains except where it is
explicitly identified as a mutation that must fail. Equations (27) and

the Noether identity. **Attack failed.**

### 2. Generic-projector and polar-residual attack

ordered `a4/4a` contributions sum to twice the `a4` component. Independently,
the polar substitution reproduces the negative sign and full factor in (33),
and therefore the residual
`-(6 pi c/kappa5) alpha oSigma` under the three-coframe rescaling. The complete
D005 shell has vanishing Euler coefficients, so its projected-field chain-rule
terms cannot cancel this residual on the benchmark. No revision-induced
inconsistency was found. **Attack failed.**
