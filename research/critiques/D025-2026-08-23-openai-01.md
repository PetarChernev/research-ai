# Internal critique

## Scope and independence

**Prominent limitation: this is a fresh-session, same-model
`openai/gpt-5.6-sol` critique of work produced by
`openai/gpt-5.6-sol`. It is procedurally separate but not model-independent,
and it is not independent scientific evidence.** O012 and O014, and the D024
critiques, share material conventions and/or model provenance and are not
independent confirmation.

The target is frozen exactly to class `A1`: local `SO(1,4)` scalar polynomial
four-forms generated by `V,E,K=DV,eta,epsilon`, of total exterior degree four
and with at most one `K`, modulo the stated algebraic quotient. Curvature `R`,
`DE`, `DK`, `K^2`, Hodge or inverse-coframe operations, extra fields,
nonbasic modes, sources, and moving interfaces are excluded. I judge only the
fixed-interface, rank-three, `c>0`, complete-independent-variation result in
that class, not all actions or tangent categories.

## Frozen target

D025 claims

\[
A1=\operatorname{span}\{I_0,I_1\},\qquad \dim A1=2,
\]

with the normalizations in D025 (1), and claims that mixed-row cancellation
requires `gamma=kappa_5^-1` while free common-triad rescaling requires
`gamma=0`; the zero-`K` term cannot alter either condition and its transverse
coframe row separately requires `lambda=0`. The conclusion is only that no
coefficient pair in this exact restricted class retains the stipulated
rank-three `c>0` polar background while canceling the free `43` row.

## Load-bearing inference

The most load-bearing inference is the conjunction of **class exhaustion** and
the two coefficient rows:

\[
A1=\mathrm{span}\{I_0,I_1\}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\begin{cases}
2\pi(\kappa_5^{-1}-\gamma)=0,\\
-6\pi\gamma c=0,
\end{cases}
\quad c>0,
\]

which has no solution for finite nonzero `kappa_5`. The polar contradiction is
not an exhaustion theorem by itself; any missed invariant with an independent
coframe or connection row could invalidate it.

## Reconstruction

At a point choose `V=e4` and write `q=V.E`, `e^a=h^a{}_AE^A`, and
`k^a=K^a`. In the zero-`K` sector, eta contractions contain a symmetric pairing
of two identical vector-valued one-forms and vanish; one `q` leaves three
Lorentz-vector indices, for which `SO(1,3)` has no invariant rank-three tensor;
two `q` factors wedge to zero. The sole survivor is
`epsilon_abcd e^a e^b e^c e^d`, covariantly `I0`. With one `K`, eta contractions
again leave an `E.E=0` factor, a `q` leaves the odd tensor product
`k e e`, and the sole epsilon survivor is
`epsilon_abcd k^a e^b e^c e^d`, covariantly `I1`. Extra polynomial powers of
`V` reduce to `V^2=1`, `V.K=0`, or two symmetric `V`s in epsilon.

For normalization,

\[
-C^{AB}\Pi_{AB}=-2K^AV^B\Pi_{AB}
=\frac1{3!}\epsilon_{ABCDE}V^AK^BE^CE^DE^E=I_1.
\]

Thus D024's primitive is `I1/kappa_5`. On the polar trace,
`K3=-c dtheta`, `Pi43=-oSigma/2`, and both ordered pair values give
`I1=-c oSigma wedge dtheta`. Adding D022's positive row gives the first
coefficient above. A common rescaling changes `oSigma` by `3 alpha oSigma`,
giving the second. Independently, `delta E3=f dtheta` gives
`delta I0=f oSigma wedge dtheta` and `delta I1=0`, hence `lambda=0`.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Class-exhaustion and quotient attack

The adapted-frame invariant count survives attacks by eta-only contractions,
`V.E`, arbitrary polynomial powers of `V`, orientation-odd epsilon terms, and
two-epsilon reductions. Because the group is `SO(1,4)`, the epsilon terms are
admissible scalars; no additional parity label creates another contraction.
The algebraic two-dimensional span is therefore persuasive.

However, the stated quotient is not precise enough for the exact dimension
claim. `A1` is not closed under `D`: differentiating its possible one-`K`
invariant three-form generates excluded `DK`, `DE`, and `K^2` jets. Moreover,
an exact four-form on `Y` is a corner action rather than an integration-by-parts
redundancy unless boundary support or a corner quotient is declared. D025
simultaneously quotients “actual covariant-exact/IBP redundancies” and retains
corners. Section 1.4 convincingly shows no relation between `I0` and `I1` in a
free jet algebra, but it does not define whether the quotient is purely
algebraic, `A1/(A1 intersect D J^3)` in a specified ambient jet complex, or an
action quotient with specified corner conditions. **This requires a scope
correction, although it does not presently supply a third algebraic invariant
or overturn the coefficient incompatibility.**

### 2. Normalization and complete-variation attack

Direct antisymmetric contraction reproduces `-C Pi=I1`; both ordered pair
orders are necessary. Re-expanding the variation reproduces the connection
coefficient `-gamma Pi_perp`, the coframe current
`J_C=(1/4) epsilon_ABCDE C^AB E^D E^E`, the reduction-field row
`H_A=2[V^B DPi_AB+2K^B Pi_AB]`, and the minus corner from graded integration by
parts. Adding D004 leaves
`Pi_parallel/kappa_5+(kappa_5^-1-gamma)Pi_perp`. The corrected Noether sign,
Lorentzian lowering, transverse `V` zero, and horizontal-degree corner zero are
consistent with the current 46/46 O014 result, but that correlated result is
support only. The zero-`K` transverse row also has the stated positive sign.
**No additional defect found.**

### 3. Polar-coefficient and allowed-tangent attack

Direct evaluation gives, respectively,

\[
\delta_{\chi43}:2\pi(\kappa_5^{-1}-\gamma),\quad
\delta_{\rm scale}:-6\pi\gamma c,\quad
\delta E^3:2\pi\lambda,
\]

with zero transverse `V` row and zero basic corner pullback. For `c>0` and
finite nonzero `kappa_5`, no `(lambda,gamma)` solves mixed cancellation and
free rescaling stationarity. Escapes by `c=0`, fixed coframe, rank at most two,
nonbasic data, or altered tangent restrictions leave the frozen class or
benchmark. **Attack failed.**

## Findings

### Revision required

Define the quotient used in `dim A1=2`. The cleanest restricted statement is a
purely algebraic quotient by wedge identities, `V^2-1`, and `V.K`; alternatively
specify an ambient differential jet algebra and use the intersection of its
exact subspace with `A1`, together with explicit corner conditions. The phrase
“actual covariant-exact/integration-by-parts redundancies” is otherwise
ambiguous because `A1` is not a differential subcomplex and corners are
retained.

### Checks that passed

- The algebraic stabilizer count finds only `I0` and `I1`.
- The ordered-pair normalization, Lorentz lowering, graded IBP sign, complete
  `omega/E/V` rows, D004 residual, and corner specialization are consistent.
- The five requested polar rows yield the stated coefficients and no allowed
  `(lambda,gamma)` escape within the frozen benchmark.

The required revision narrows and defines the theorem; it does not presently
change the restricted no-solution calculation.

## Machine-check candidates

A finite obligation is decision-relevant, chiefly for the **class-exhaustion
assertion**, which O012 and O014 do not test. Scientific Computation could
enumerate all degree-four contractions generated by `eta,epsilon,V` with four
one-form slots, separately for zero and one `K`, reduce by wedge antisymmetry,
`V^2=1`, and `V.K=0`, and require ranks `(1,1)` with normal forms `(I0,I1)`.
Negative controls should permit a second `K` and separately an Hodge operation,
each of which must enlarge or alter the enumerated space. Acceptance should not
include any exact/IBP assertion until the ambient complex and corner quotient
are defined. Such a passed finite check would establish only the encoded
invariant algebra, not physical completeness, tangent admissibility, or the
broader action class.

## Outcome

`revision-required`

The coefficient no-go survives the three attacks, but the exact
two-dimensional theorem currently invokes an underdefined exact/IBP quotient
for a class not closed under `D`. A scope-level definition is required before
the class-exhaustion claim is fully well posed.
