# Internal critique

## Scope and independence

This is a fresh-session **same-model** critique of frozen D026. Both producer
and critic use `openai/gpt-5.6-sol`; procedural separation is not model
independence, agreement is not independent confirmation, and this report is not
independent verification. The audit is limited to the connection-descent bridge
and its stated consequences for C025/C027/C028 and C002/C003.

## Frozen target

D026 claims that, under its local triviality, effective-descent, and `C1`
tangential-trace assumptions, `(A,gamma)` is an effective connection descent
object for the boundary-collapse map `q_+`; equivalently its boundary trace
obeys

\[
H_u[a]=1,\qquad \iota_{\partial_\theta}F_a=0.
\]

For `a=cT_{43}dtheta`, this gives the lattice condition
`exp(2pi c T43)=1`; at an allowed lattice point, a constant nonzero
`delta chi T43 dtheta` is claimed not to be a tangent even when `delta gamma`
varies. D026 explicitly limits itself to connection-only descent and imports no
action, coframe descent, reduction-field descent, or canonical classification.

## Load-bearing inference

The most load-bearing inference is the promotion from a boundary connection
that is gauge-equivalent to a pullback from `Sigma` to an effective connection
on the singular quotient object `X_+`. If that promotion is unavailable, the
holonomy result remains a valid **boundary-trace category restriction**, but it
is not yet a necessary-and-sufficient theorem for full connection descent over
`q_+`.

## Reconstruction

On `Y=Sigma x S1`, choose a base angle. Trivial vertical holonomy makes vertical
parallel transport a single-valued periodic frame change. In that frame
`a_theta=0`, and
`F_{theta mu}=partial_theta a_mu`; hence
`i_partialtheta F_a=0` makes the remaining coefficients theta-independent.
Thus the boundary connection is presentation-equivalent to
`pi_Y^* bar a`. Conversely a pullback connection has identity fiber holonomy
and zero vertical curvature. For the polar trace,

\[
\gamma_c(\theta_1,\theta_2)
=\exp[cT_{43}(\theta_2-\theta_1)]
\]

has the correct arrow orientation `p_2^*P -> p_1^*P`, satisfies
`gamma12 gamma23=gamma13`, and obeys D026's affine sign convention. Its
single-valuedness is exactly `exp(2pi cT43)=1`.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Full-collar/singular-quotient counterexample — **major but scope-repairable**

Take a collar connection whose tangential boundary trace is zero but whose
normal coefficient is angle dependent, for example
`A=f(theta) ds` near `s=0`, with nonconstant smooth periodic `f`. D026's
boundary equations see `a=0`, so `H=1`, `F_a=0`, and `gamma=1`; the object is
therefore admitted. But this field does not extend as an ordinary continuous
connection through the smooth `c=1` Cartesian cap, and its collar curvature
contains `f'(theta)dtheta wedge ds`. More generally D026 declares no
lower-regularity differential calculus on `X_+` that proves such unconstrained
normal/angular collar data are pullbacks of quotient connections.

Assumption 6 asserts effectiveness, but the displayed equations impose only
tangential trace compatibility and do not prove that assertion for this
rank-jumping, non-submersion quotient. Therefore Lemma 1 is sound as a theorem
about descent of the boundary connection along `Y -> Sigma`; it is not yet
established as sufficient for a full connection on a specified smooth or weak
quotient collar. Smooth dependence on `u` is not an additional defect at the
declared `C1` trace level—the parameter-dependent circle ODE supplies it—and
horizontal basicness is correctly supplied by the curvature condition after
vertical gauge fixing. The missing item is the claim-to-quotient bridge, not
another boundary equation.

### 2. Finite cocycle and presentation/gauge distinctions — **minor**

The explicit signs pass: with D026's convention,
`-d gamma_c gamma_c^{-1}=cT43(dtheta1-dtheta2)`, which converts
`cT43 dtheta2` to `cT43 dtheta1`; and
`h_c=exp(c theta T43)` sends both `a` and `gamma_c` to zero. It is also outside
`SO(1,3)_V` because `T43` does not stabilize `e4`.

The terminology should nevertheless be tightened. Equation (12) is an
isomorphism of descent presentations and, under an actual effective descent
equivalence, descends to an isomorphism between the corresponding downstairs
bundle-with-connection objects. Equation (13) instead characterizes a
representative preserving the **fixed** cocycle. Thus “not a genuine
downstairs gauge transformation” is safe only with D026's repeated qualifier
“in the fixed/original descent presentation”; it is not an intrinsic denial of
all downstairs gauge-isomorphism language. This does not alter the lattice or
tangent result.

### 3. Linearized, global-form, and overpromotion loopholes — **no issue**, with one minor wording repair

At an allowed polar background the non-Abelian holonomy derivative reduces in
the commuting `T43` direction to
`H^{-1}delta H=2pi delta chi T43`. Varying `gamma` cannot change the identity
holonomy required of every effective collapsed fiber. A periodic infinitesimal
presentation parameter would require
`partial_theta epsilon=-delta chi T43`, whose circle integral excludes nonzero
constant `delta chi`; disconnected large windings do not create an
infinitesimal tangent. No non-Abelian or large-winding loophole was found.

For the stated vector representation of `SO_0(1,4)`, `c=1` is safely allowed
and `pi_1(SO_0(1,4))=Z2` is consistent with the winding warning. The Spin
sentence should be made convention-exact rather than saying “generally”: for
the canonical lift of the vector-normalized planar generator,
`exp(2pi T_tilde)=-1` and identity holonomy requires the doubled/even lattice.
Other quotients or retained central isotropy are correctly excluded from the
chosen category.

D026 does **not** illicitly promote connection-only descent to the coframe,
`(A,V)`, action, or physical state space. Its check
`D_theta V=cT43 V != 0` correctly blocks the frozen adapted pair for positive
`c`. C025 remains a result in its explicitly broader free finite tangent class;
C027 and C028 retain that same free-tangent premise and are neither refuted nor
reproved. C002/C003 remain conjectural because D026 supplies no complete state
space, action, displacement variation, constraint system, or hyperbolicity
analysis.

## Findings

- **Blocking:** none within the explicitly narrowed boundary-trace category.
- **Major but scope-repairable:** D026 alternates between a proved boundary
  descent criterion and an unproved full effective connection descent claim on
  `X_+`. Assumption 6 cannot substitute for specifying the quotient connection
  category and proving effectiveness, especially because normal collar data are
  unconstrained.
- **Minor:** qualify the presentation/gauge language intrinsically, and state
  the Spin lift with a fixed lifted generator rather than “generally.”
- **No issue:** cocycle orientation and affine signs; the explicit `gamma_c`
  and `h_c`; exclusion of constant nonzero `delta chi` with variable
  `delta gamma`; adapted-subgroup distinction; and the limited ledger impact.

## Machine-check candidates

None recommended. The finite cocycle and linearized identities are already
transparent algebra; a machine pass would not establish effective descent for
the singular lower-regularity quotient or select its physical category.

## Outcome

`revision-required`

Minimal exact repair list:

1. Replace the claimed necessary-and-sufficient theorem for a connection on
   the local quotient object by a theorem for **boundary-trace descent along
   `Y -> Sigma`**, and rename `Conn_lr(q_+)` accordingly; **or** specify the
   weak/diffeological connection sheaf on `X_+`, state its collar regularity,
   and prove that the kernel-pair data are effective and that no additional
   normal/full-collar compatibility is required.
2. State explicitly that the current equations constrain only `i_Y^*A` and do
   not control angle-dependent normal collar coefficients such as
   `A_s(theta,0)ds`; do not call arbitrary admitted `A` a descended full-collar
   connection without the proof in item 1.
3. Keep the qualifier “fixed/original descent presentation” wherever (13) is
   used to deny downstairs gauge status, and replace the Spin-lattice sentence
   by the canonical-lift statement above.

No change to C025/C027/C028 or C002/C003 follows from this critique unless D026
is later promoted beyond the repaired boundary-trace scope.
