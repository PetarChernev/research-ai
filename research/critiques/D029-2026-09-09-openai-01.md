## Falsification attempts

1. **Spectrum completeness: hidden threshold or embedded bound state.** For the smooth bounded potential on the full line, eigenfunctions have the regularity needed for the intertwiner and its norm identity `||Af||²=E||f||²`. The free partner has no square-integrable eigenfunction at any energy, excluding embedded as well as subthreshold positive eigenvalues. At threshold the mapped zero-momentum wave is proportional to `tanh(as)`, bounded but not square-integrable; the other independent solution grows. The kernel of the first-order `A` is one-dimensional. **Derived assessment:** exactly one normalizable level survives this attack. The threshold resonance is not a second bound particle. The normalization and positive mass shift are consistent; the stated gap is in mass squared, not a universal laboratory energy gap.

2. **Autonomous motion versus an imposed profile.** The boundary sector selects a wall but not its center or velocity. Varying both fields and boosting the zero-probe solution supplies a genuine inertially moving selector, not an externally programmed trajectory. The translation norm is `∫phi_K'² ds=4av²/3>0`, giving the quoted positive kinetic coefficient and leading wave equation. Arbitrary accelerated rigid profiles and finite-amplitude probe transport are not established by these facts. **Derived assessment:** no contradiction with the bounded motion claim; D029 explicitly excludes prescribed acceleration, vacuum nucleation, and unrestricted backreaction times. Autonomous evolution must not be promoted to spontaneous selection of the topological sector.

3. **Fit to a localized extra-direction region.** The bound mode removes a low-energy transverse propagation channel relative to the permanent bulk; it does not create an extra accessible direction inside a finite three-space object. The wall is infinite in ordinary space, its probe tail has infinite support, and sufficiently energetic or decoupled probes retain bulk access. Moreover, `m0²<q<mu0²` can define a classical turning region without any on-shell bound mode at that invariant mass; `R_q` alone is not evidence for a populated accessible sector. **Derived assessment:** substantial target shortfall, not a false claim: D029 explicitly identifies the turning region as a classical diagnostic and disclaims the stronger geometry. This shortfall is compatible with W002's relaxed comparison remit.
