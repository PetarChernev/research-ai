## Reconstruction

\[
p_!\Theta_{5,\chi}=-{2\pi\over6\kappa_5}\epsilon_{abcd}
\,\delta\chi^{4a}e^b\wedge e^c\wedge e^d .
\]

The minus sign is from moving `dtheta` through three coframes; the core boundary
has the positive `oSigma wedge dtheta` orientation. For `a=3`,
`epsilon_3012=-1`, and each of the six permutations of `012` contributes
`-oSigma`, so

\[
\epsilon_{3bcd}e^b\wedge e^c\wedge e^d=-6o_\Sigma,
\qquad
p_!\Theta_{5,\chi^{43}}=+{2\pi\over\kappa_5}
\delta\chi^{43}o_\Sigma.

Thus free `delta chi43` gives
`epsilon_(3bcd)e^b wedge e^c wedge e^d=0`, contradicted by the displayed
rank-three triad. This single equation kills its nonzero `012` triple minor.
D022 also correctly states the stronger separate fact that freeing all four
mixed modes would kill every `3x3` minor and imply rank at most two; it does not
incorrectly attribute that stronger rank bound to the lone polar mode.

## Falsification attempts

1. **Sign, ordered-pair, and rank attack.** Recounting both antisymmetric
   orders, the three coframe placements already incorporated in D004's `1/6`,
   the fiber integral `2pi`, and the stated orientations reproduces the positive
   coefficient above. The mixed Euler equation is incompatible with the
   intended rank-three pullback. **Attack fails.**

2. **First-order tangent and independence attack.** On the resolved closure
   `M_+`, a smooth one-sided connection tangent with prescribed finite boundary
   trace `delta omega43=delta chi43 dtheta` can be extended into a collar, so it
   is free in D022's explicitly declared split field space. Palatini variation
   keeps `delta c` and `delta chi43` independent before the open-core torsion
   equation; substituting `chi43=c` beforehand would be an illicit metric-family
   restriction. Even on torsion-free polar-family tangents the term becomes
   `(2pi/kappa5) delta c oSigma` and remains nonzero. Fixing only `c` is
   insufficient; fixing both removes an Euler variation by boundary condition,
   yielding no transition equation and, with the fixed interface and absent
   displacement variation, no support for C002. **Attack fails within the
   declared split class.** Physical admissibility of that class remains a
   stated residual risk, not a defect in the conditional inference.

3. **Cancellation and geometry-separation attack.** D005's complete basic
   field list and action contain only persistent `SO(1,3)` connections and no
   `omega43`; hence its derivative with respect to `chi43` is identically zero.
   Its persistent shell equations can hold while the distinct mixed D004 row
   fails. D022 also keeps separate: (i) the D002 quotient with an open collapsed
   ray, (ii) a half-cap (`c=1` smooth axis or `c!=1` cone with tip curvature
   `2pi(1-c)`), and (iii) the fixed-cylinder zero extension whose jump curvature
   is `c delta_0 ds wedge dtheta`. The special torsion/curvature products and
   the illustrative whole-cylinder Palatini density are well-defined for the
   displayed ansatz and are expressly not promoted to a general BV action.
   Split bulk finiteness follows because both open-side curvatures vanish.
   **No hidden D005 cancellation or conflation contaminates the central
   conclusion.**
