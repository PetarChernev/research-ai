# Internal critique

## Scope and independence

**Prominent limitation: this is a fresh-session, same-model
`openai/gpt-5.6-sol` review of a derivation and executable produced by
`openai/gpt-5.6-sol`. It is procedurally separate but not model-independent;
agreement is not independent scientific evidence.** This audit is limited to
whether O014 implements its predeclared C027 claim-to-computation criterion. I
did not rerun the obligation or inspect beyond the supplied packet.

## Frozen target

C027 claims, under the fixed-interface and basic rank-three assumptions, that
the two minimality conditions uniquely give
`Omega_V=omega+V(D_omega V)-(D_omega V)V`; complete dependent variation of the
single primitive cancels only the four coset connection rows; and the polar
specialization cancels the `43/34` row but leaves a coframe equation setting
`c=0`, with transverse `V` residual and basic polar corner term zero. O014's
criterion additionally requires independent differentiation, integration by
parts with the negative corner sign, the graded Noether identity, and six
effective semantic mutations. The spec expressly excludes tangent
admissibility, global sectors, total moment maps, alternative actions, and
C002/C003. Those declared omissions are not defects found here.

## Load-bearing inference

The decisive computational bridge is not that target-shaped values can be
represented, but that one expression graph beginning at

\[
B_V=-2\int K^A V^B P_{AB}(E)
\]

is differentiated and integrated by parts to produce the `omega`, `E`, and
`V` Euler rows and corner term, after which independently assembled D004 and
polar data expose the surviving residuals. If the executable instead inserts
those Euler rows or residuals as expected values, its pass cannot discriminate
an error in D024's bridge.

## Reconstruction

A faithful finite check would construct `K=D_omega V`, `C=KV-VK`, and
`P=P(E)`, apply a derivation to `B_V`, canonicalize
`D(delta V) V P` by a graded Leibniz/integration-by-parts rule, and read off
`F_A=2[V^B DP_AB+2K^B P_AB]` and the negative boundary coefficient. The
derived connection coefficient must then be added to a separately assembled
generic D004 `P` row to obtain `hhP`. A separate polar substitution should
derive, rather than stipulate, the ordered-pair action coefficient, coframe and
transverse-`V` residuals, and corner pullback. Finally, the actual derived Euler
rows must be contracted with one consistently antisymmetric gauge parameter to
test the Noether polynomial.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Representation/index, projector, and uniqueness attack

Lines 153--186 use a nontrivial exact rational spacelike unit vector. The
projector identities, double-projected block, and ranks 10/6/4 are genuine
rational matrix computations; `result.json` records the corresponding exact
observations. The candidate added tensor `x=VK-KV` is inserted and then tested,
rather than solved for, but the homogeneous rank-10 calculation meaningfully
establishes uniqueness under the encoded two conditions. One representative
unit spacelike `V` is adequate for this fixed-signature orbit, although the
code does not itself establish covariance over the orbit.

The `independent-omega` mutation is not semantic: line 212 changes the
*expected rank* from 10 to 9 while leaving the connection construction and
constraint map untouched. Its exit 1 therefore shows only that a deliberately
wrong expected number fails, not that treating `Omega_V` independently would
be detected. This weakens mutation clause (vi), but the unmutated
projector/uniqueness rows themselves are substantive. **Attack materially
narrows, but does not invalidate, clause (i).**

### 2. Dependent variation, integration by parts, and generic residual attack

This attack succeeds decisively.

* At lines 234--236, `formula17` and `independently_grouped` are the same three
  already-constructed objects in a different addition order. No derivative of
  `primitive` (line 227) is taken. Thus
  `variation.equation17_complete` is a tautological self-comparison.
* `dK`, `direct_v`, and `connection` are manually populated with the desired
  terms. There is no implementation of `delta K=D delta V+delta omega V` as a
  derivation rule linking them to `primitive`. The `J_from_deltaP` comparison
  is useful: it compares epsilon differentiation of `P(E)` with a separately
  assembled current. It does not rescue the complete variation clause.
* Lines 244--252 directly define `F` by the target formula and set the corner
  coefficient to `-2`. No covariant Leibniz operation or integration by parts
  derives either result. The `delete-second-F` and `reverse-corner` processes
  merely perturb these inserted target values.
* Lines 254--256 set the generic residual equal to `parallel_map` unless the
  mutation directly replaces it by zero, then compare it with
  `parallel_map`. The derived connection coefficient is never added to a D004
  momentum row. Consequently clause (iii), including “leaves exactly `hhP`,"
  is assumed rather than tested; its mutation is likewise a guard on an
  assignment, not an independently computed assertion.

The recorded 34/34 outcome therefore does not satisfy criterion clauses (ii)
or (iii), despite meaningful subchecks for `delta P` and projector ranks.

### 3. Polar, Noether, corner, and mutation attack

This attack also succeeds.

* Lines 264--272 assign
  `d_bomega_plus_v = deltae_j`; this is precisely the covariance relation that
  should be computed. The graded polynomial then vanishes by construction
  because `deltae_j=-e_diamond` is only the generic degree-1/degree-3 swap.
  Neither `D B_omega` nor `B_V diamond V` is formed from the Euler rows, and
  the `alpha` symbols are not assembled as one metric-antisymmetric gauge
  parameter. The 24-monomial all-plus residual tests grading, not the complete
  Noether identity.
* The claimed alternate polar path inserts `P43=-1/2`, `P34=+1/2`, sets the
  ordered count, defines `bcoef=-ordered/2`, sets the transverse residual to a
  four-entry zero list, obtains the coframe row as `3*bcoef`, and declares the
  pullback zero through the Boolean `3 > 2` (lines 274--288). It never assembles
  `K`, `C`, wedge orientation, `F_A`, or an actual pullback. These rows restate
  the expected formulas and dimension argument.
* The remaining mutations alter the selected identity or inserted scalar
  directly. Their fresh-process exit codes in `result.json` demonstrate
  process isolation and assertion plumbing, but not discrimination by an
  independently computed scientific quantity.

Thus clauses (iv), (v), and the scientific-independence part of (vi) are not
implemented as declared. The basic-sector restriction itself is a declared
limitation; the defect is that even within that restriction the expected
values are mostly supplied rather than derived.

## Findings

O014 has sound exact arithmetic infrastructure and useful projector, rank, and
`delta P` subchecks. Nevertheless, its most load-bearing acceptance clauses
are assumption-encoded or tautological. The wrapper's recorded `passed`
outcome establishes that those assertions and mutations executed as written,
not that the complete dependent variation and polar bridge were independently
reconstructed. This is a blocking implementation defect for the declared
criterion, not evidence against C027 and not one of the spec's acknowledged
physics omissions.

The smallest scientifically faithful repair is to retain the rational graded
kernel and projector tests but replace the target-value assignments with: (1)
an explicit derivation plus graded integration-by-parts normalization starting
from `primitive`; (2) extraction of Euler and corner coefficients; (3) generic
D004 addition and polar specialization computed from independently assembled
coframes, connection, epsilon, and pullback maps; and (4) a Noether contraction
of those extracted rows. Each mutation must modify an upstream construction
while unchanged downstream assertions detect the residual. After any such
implementation change, the current `research/checks/O014/result.json` and its
34/34 interpretation must be treated as stale pending a new canonical run.

## Machine-check candidates

The repaired O014 itself is the concrete candidate: acceptance should require
canonical equality between coefficients extracted from the differentiated
primitive and separately encoded D024 coefficients, plus nonzero residuals
from upstream sign/factor mutations. This would settle the finite encoded
algebra only; it would still leave all physical omissions declared in the spec
unsettled.

## Outcome

`revision-required`

Decisive reason: O014's variation, integration-by-parts, generic residual,
Noether, and polar rows largely compare expressions with themselves or insert
the expected coefficients, so the recorded pass does not meet the
predeclared requirement to independently derive and discriminate the
load-bearing C027 bridge.
