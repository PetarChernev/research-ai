## Reconstruction

For the adapted witness,

\[
P_{40}=\frac{1}{12\kappa _5}\epsilon_{0bcd}E^bE^cE^d
=\frac{1}{2\kappa _5}dx^1\wedge dx^2\wedge ds.
\]

Thus tangential pullback kills the `ds` leg, whereas contraction first gives
`j_C^* iota_{partial_s}P_{40}=(2 kappa_5)^{-1}dx^1 wedge dx^2`.
For `delta E^1=chi dx^1`, the two ordered `(c,d)` terms similarly give
`j_C^* iota_{partial_s}dP_{40}=chi(2 kappa_5)^{-1}dx^1 wedge dx^2`.
This realizes exactly the distinction required by the first critique.

## Falsification attempts

1. **Factor, sign, and dimension attack on the normal witness.** The six
   permutations in `P_40` produce `6/12=1/2`; the two surviving ordered terms
   in its variation produce `2/4=1/2`. With `epsilon_0123=+1`, the displayed
   sign is positive. Since `[kappa_5]=L^3`, two coordinate one-forms contribute
   `L^2`, so contraction with `partial_s` yields `L^-1`, as D016 states. The
   witness is admissible because `ds` is the spatial normal, not the collapsed
   circle direction. **Attack does not expose an error.**

2. **Scope-regression attack.** Sections 1--2 and the conclusion now qualify
   the zero as tangential pullback/pushforward and explicitly retain nonzero
   normal contraction. Section 5 calls (27)--(31) weak covariant targets for a
   future canonical chain, states the local trace-surjectivity and smooth-bulk
   assumptions before deriving `r=s=0`, and disclaims a completed Dirac split
   or reduced bracket. Sections 3 and 6 restrict the D010 rejection to the
   unreduced duplicated-trace presentation and conclude that unspecified
   horizontal mixed connection and normal jets leave truncation consistency
   undecidable. **No material overclaim from the first version remains.**
