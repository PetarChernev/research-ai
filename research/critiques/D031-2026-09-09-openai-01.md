## Frozen target

D031, draft, states: “a finite intrinsic spatial complex admits an autonomous, local, thermally reversible triangle-attachment process that changes spatial local dimension from one to two, with an exactly calculable energy change and formation probability.” Its conclusion explicitly conditions this on model assumptions.

**Assumptions:** finite connected persistent simple graph; at most two candidate triangular cycles per edge; arbitrary subsets of those cycles occupied as equilateral two-simplices; finite uniform couplings, positive edge length, bath temperature, and attempt rate; external time and an ideal heat reservoir. The witness is a three-edge cycle with one dangling edge, initially unfilled. Under the supplied W002 scope, a lower-dimensional discrete analogue with actual incidence change is admissible. Relativistic completion, isolated dynamics, universal matter response, and a stable macroscopic object are not the target.

## Load-bearing inference

The critical representation bridge is **a change in the occupied face set represents a change in physical local topological dimension, not just a change in graph homology or an auxiliary occupancy label**. If physical space were instead only the persistent graph, the stochastic calculation could remain correct while the dimensionality claim disappeared.

## Reconstruction

Taking physical space to be the geometric realization of the entire complex makes a toggle substantive: all boundary edges already exist, so adding the triangle preserves simplicial closure; no higher simplex obstructs removal. At a previously bare edge interior, sufficiently small neighborhoods change from intervals to half-disks; new face-interior points have disk neighborhoods. This establishes the witness's local dimension change without inferring dimension from Betti numbers.

For addition, each of the three edges changes its exposed-boundary indicator by +1 if previously bare and by −1 if already incident to one face. Thus, with q previously occupied neighboring edges,

`ΔE = μA + τa(3−2q)`.

The heat-bath rates have ratio `w_forward/w_reverse = exp(−βΔE)`. Pairwise master-equation fluxes conserve probability, all subset states communicate at positive temperature, and the Gibbs distribution obeys detailed balance. These are autonomous stochastic choices on eligible cycles, not prescribed trajectories. In the two-state witness `ε=μA+3τa`; the rates sum to ν, yielding `dp/dt=ν(p_eq−p)`, `p_eq=(1+exp(βε))^−1`, and `p(t)=p_eq(1−exp(−νt))`. Mean geometry energy gain is `εp(t)` and mean reservoir gain is its negative. Here p is occupation probability, not cumulative probability of ever having formed a face.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Locality and dimension versus mere homology

**Attack:** killing a graph cycle need not prove dimensional growth, and toggles adjacent to existing faces need not change local dimension from one to two.

**Assessment:** the explicit neighborhood argument defeats the first objection for the stipulated realization and initially empty witness. The homology calculation is correctly subordinate. The second objection does narrow the wording: if an edge already has one face, adding the other changes a half-disk to a disk, both dimension two. Thus not every toggle makes an existing point undergo a 1→2 change. Likewise, “edges outside the attached face remain one-dimensional” is correct only for otherwise bare edges, not edges of other occupied faces. These are nonblocking generality clarifications because the target is existential and the witness satisfies it. Locality is bounded combinatorial support, including adjacent face occupancies; finite-area instantaneous creation has no causal speed bound. The source acknowledges that distinction.

### 2. Reservoir accounting and reversibility

**Attack:** positive-energy creation might hide external work, or detailed balance might be mistaken for microscopically reversible isolated dynamics.

**Assessment:** no contradiction within the assumed open model. A time-independent energy function and the specified local detailed-balance ratio consistently assign heat `Q_geom=ΔE`, `Q_res=−ΔE`, with zero protocol work. Reservoir entropy change is `−βΔE`, matching the log rate ratio. For negative ε, formation heats rather than drains the reservoir; ε=0 gives equal rates and zero jump heat. Energy units and dimensionless exponents are consistent. This is phenomenological bookkeeping, not a derivation of bath degrees of freedom or their energy cost. D031 explicitly limits “reversible” to detailed balance and does not claim geometry-only conservation. No blocking error found.

### 3. Vertex-probe blindness and target fit

**Attack:** unchanged vertex adjacency could make the supposed new space observationally equivalent to an occupancy model with invisible auxiliary faces.

**Assessment:** this is the strongest remaining scientific limitation. A vertex-only graph Laplacian is exactly unchanged, so vertex diffusion cannot operationally identify the additional interiors. The face-boundary matrix is an incidence diagnostic, not a derived matter measurement. Nevertheless the supplied W002 target permits a discrete ontology with explicit energy accounting, and D031 openly states probe blindness and missing universal coupling. The witness therefore meets the scoped combinatorial target, not a claim that matter experiences another direction. Lack of relativistic completion is a separate absent extension, not an error in the finite Markov model.
