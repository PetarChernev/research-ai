\[
G_{ij}=F\eta_{ij},\quad G_{33}=H,\quad G_{44}=J,
\]

where

\[
F=2p+A^2+2q+r,\quad H=3(A^2+q),\quad J=3(p+A^2),
\]

\[
\kappa_5(T_{00},T_{11},T_{22},T_{33},T_{44})
=(-(F+\Lambda_5),F+\Lambda_5,F+\Lambda_5,H+\Lambda_5,J+\Lambda_5).
\]

For a null vector, `(k^0)^2=sum_(a=1)^4(k^a)^2`; hence the `1,2`
terms cancel the energy term and the cosmological contribution cancels by

\[
\kappa_5T_{AB}k^Ak^B=(H-F)(k^3)^2+(J-F)(k^4)^2,
\]

with

\[
H-F=2A^2+q-2p-r,\qquad J-F=p+2A^2-2q-r.
\]

Since the two squared coefficients can be isolated with `e_0+e_3` and
`e_0+e_4`, all-null NEC is exactly their simultaneous nonnegativity. For the

\[

Flatness gives `A,p,A^2=O(s^N)` for every `N`; multiplication of `A` by the

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Lorentzian signs, normalization, and all-null reduction

I recomputed the effective diagonal tensor and null contraction above. The
timelike metric sign correctly gives `T_00=-(F+Lambda_5)/kappa_5`, not the
positive expression; `epsilon+P_1=epsilon+P_2=0`; and `Lambda_5` cancels from
every null contraction. With `kappa_5>0`, the NEC inequalities retain their
direction. The unwarped control `w=1` yields coefficients `H-F=J-F=-r`, and
the polar control `rho=s`, `w=1`, `Lambda_5=0` yields zero curvature and stress.
No sign, factor, or normalization counterexample was found.

### 2. Arbitrary flat-germ warp as a cancellation mechanism

For `f=w-1` smooth and identically zero on the left, Taylor remainder applied
to every derivative gives `f^(k)=O(s^N)` for arbitrary fixed `k,N`. Eventual
positivity and `w->1` make division by `w` harmless. Since `B` is only a fixed
polynomial divergence for each `nu`, `q=AB` remains flat. Neither oscillatory
nor sign-changing flat derivatives can cancel the positive polynomial leading
term of `r`; flatness controls magnitude, not merely a pointwise limit. This
establishes the quantifier over every allowed `w` for the two fixed profiles.
No hidden warp counterexample was found.

### 3. Absolute endpoint integrability and curvature-square side observation

Choose `epsilon` below the exact positivity threshold for `r`. Then

\[
\int_0^\epsilon \rho|r|\,ds
=\int_0^\epsilon\rho''\,ds
=\rho'(\epsilon)-\rho'(0)<\infty,
\]

with `rho'(0)=0`. Bounded upper and lower bounds on `w^3` convert this into the
needed estimate for the dominant stress term; triangle inequalities handle
`F+Lambda_5`, and every remaining warp/cosmological term is bounded times the
integrable weight `rho`. Thus the argument uses no cancellation between stress
components and no invalid removal of an absolute value. Direct multiplicity
counting of the ten sectional planes gives the optional side formula
`R_ABCD R^ABCD=4(3A^4+3p^2+3q^2+r^2)`; the Lorentzian timelike planes still
contribute positively because the timelike index is raised twice. The weight
`rho r^2` is an exponential times a finite inverse power and is integrable.
No defect was found in either the main `L1` claim or this explicitly secondary
curvature-square observation.
