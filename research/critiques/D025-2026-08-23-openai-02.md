## Reconstruction

### Decisive algebraic count

Choose the counting frame `V=e4` and decompose `E=qV+e`, with `q=V.E`, while
`K=k` is transverse. In the zero-`K` sector, every eta-only contraction of four
identical vector-valued one-forms contains a symmetric eta pairing against an
antisymmetric wedge and vanishes. Two `q` factors wedge to zero; one `q` leaves
three Lorentz vectors, but the stabilizer `SO(1,3)` has no invariant rank-three
tensor. The unique survivor is `epsilon_abcd e^a e^b e^c e^d`, namely `I0`.

With one `K`, eta contractions contain a vanishing `E.E` factor. One `q` leaves
`k e e`, again with no invariant odd-rank Lorentz tensor, and two `q`s vanish.
The unique epsilon contraction is `epsilon_abcd k^a e^b e^c e^d`, namely
`I1`. Additional `V`s reduce by `V^2=1`, vanish through `V.K=0`, or repeat `V`
inside epsilon. Thus the purely algebraic sector has ranks `(1,1)` and no third
operator.

### Polar coefficient check

On `V=e4`, `K3=-c dtheta`, with
`Pi43=-oSigma/2` and `Pi34=+oSigma/2`. From

\[
-C^{AB}\Pi_{AB}=I_1,

\[
I_1=-c\,oSigma\wedge dtheta,
\qquad B_{A1}=-2\pi\gamma c\int_\Sigma oSigma.

Consequently the positive D004/D022 mixed coefficient becomes

decisive row.
