## Reconstruction

Let `F(u,a0)=u·a0`. Associativity of the declared connection action gives

\[
F(h u,a_0)=h\cdot F(u,a_0),\qquad
F(ur^{-1},r\cdot a_0)=(ur^{-1}r)\cdot a_0=F(u,a_0).
\]

Applying `R_{r2}` and then `R_{r1}` gives
`u r2^-1 r1^-1=u(r1r2)^-1` and
`r1·(r2·a0)=(r1r2)·a0`; hence D013's convention
`R_{r1}R_{r2}=R_{r1r2}` is consistent. Left multiplication plainly commutes with this right action. The countermodel also works exactly:

\[
F(r^{-1},-dr\,r^{-1})
=-r^{-1}dr-d(r^{-1})r=0=F(1,0).
\]

For right-trivialized `zeta=delta u u^-1`, direct differentiation gives

\[
\delta a=u\,\delta a_0u^{-1}-D_a\zeta.
\]

With `r=exp(beta)`, the simultaneous right variation is
`zeta=-Ad_u beta` and `delta a0=-D_a0 beta`. Gauge covariance of the covariant derivative,

\[
D_a(Ad_u\beta)=Ad_u(D_{a_0}\beta),
\]

therefore yields `delta_beta a=0`, with both minus signs essential. The stated right Maurer--Cartan convention is also consistent: for `delta u u^-1`,
`delta zeta=+(1/2)[zeta wedge zeta]`. Thus (7)--(15), including signs and action order, survive reconstruction.

This proves invariance before integration by parts whenever the action depends on `(u,a0)` only through `a`. It does **not by itself** identify the complete cut potential after `a0` is admitted to vary. D005 (58) supplies the `u` endpoint potential under the fixed-seed substitution `delta a=-D_a zeta`; it does not supply a canonical seed potential for varying `a0`.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Composition/order and identical dressed connection — passed

The noncommutative order in (8) and (10) is correct under D013's composition convention, and the explicit nonconstant-`r` countermodel gives identical `a=0` exactly. This establishes duplicate dressing data. It does not determine whether the duplicates are quotiented, retained as null labels, or endowed with additional corner structure.

### 2. Right endpoint cancellation — revision required

D013 lines 311--318 assert cancellation between (22) and an endpoint generated from the `delta a0` term. The unintegrated identity `delta_beta a=0` strongly requires cancellation for the pullback of an action depending only on `a`, but the report does not display the complete variation with temporal and lateral orientations, nor a seed contribution to `Theta`. Equation (23) is an Euler-variation term over `Sigma`, not by itself a declared seed symplectic potential. Consequently the algebra proves an action/configuration kernel, while the specific canonical claim that the apparent cut moment map cancels in the adopted polarization remains underived. A boundary exact term or independent seed/corner term could change the cut Hamiltonian without changing `delta a=0`.

The result should therefore be narrowed: canonical cancellation follows only for the explicitly induced pullback polarization with no additional seed or corner term, or else after the missing complete endpoint derivation is supplied.

### 3. Stabilizer, seed-orbit quotient, and exterior action — material ambiguity

The broad three-way distinction is correct: `Stab(a0)` preserves a fixed seed; the full right seed-orbit relation removes factorizations; the exterior left action changes `a` and needs the total Gauss system. However, (22) and the statement that even a fixed-seed stabilizer transformation may be charged sit uneasily with the exact map kernel. If `r·a0=a0`, then `F(ur^-1,a0)=F(u,a0)` already, so in a pure pullback phase space every dependence through `a` is unchanged. A nonzero contraction of one displayed potential is not sufficient to turn that coordinate redundancy into a charged symmetry; either omitted bulk/end-cut pieces cancel it, or extra structure makes `u` separately meaningful and must be declared. Conversely, declining to quotient does not make duplicate labels physical.

The countermodel correctly shows that an equivalence declaration changes the configuration quotient, but it does not establish a distinct nonzero canonical charge for `P_N`. This wording needs revision. The separate conclusion that the exterior left action requires bulk cancellation, first-class closure/rank, and corner conditions is unaffected.
