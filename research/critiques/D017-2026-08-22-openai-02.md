## Frozen target

Under D017's explicit clean, constant-rank, fixed-seed shell assumptions, the
complete cut potential is claimed to pull back as

\[
\langle\lambda,\xi_g\rangle+\langle Q,\chi_v\rangle
\longmapsto \langle P^-_C,\zeta_u\rangle .
\]

Together with exterior Palatini this gives (29); eliminating a 12-by-12
Palatini defining complement gives zero self-Schur correction, six residual
connection-primary null combinations, and planar `rank(A_P)=4`. No first
preservation or physical classification is claimed because the trace pairing
and adjoint, boundary-compatible background, and total Hamiltonian are absent.

## Load-bearing inference

The load-bearing inference is not the algebraic cancellation itself, but the
bridge from that pulled-back potential to the asserted **complete surviving
coordinate/primary inventory** in (28)--(33). Independence of the pulled-back
one-form from `g` proves that the `g` tangent is presymplectically null; it does
not by itself eliminate `g`, identify that null direction as gauge, or remove
its zero-momentum primary from preservation.

## Reconstruction

Using `v=gu` and right Maurer--Cartan variables,

\[
\chi_v=\xi_g+\operatorname{Ad}_g\zeta_u,
\]

so on `lambda=-Q`,

\[
\Theta_C^I
=\langle-Q,\xi_g\rangle
 +\langle Q,\xi_g+\operatorname{Ad}_g\zeta_u\rangle
=\langle Q,\operatorname{Ad}_g\zeta_u\rangle.
\]

With `Q=g\cdot P^-` and an invariant pairing this is exactly
`<P^-_C,zeta_u>`. Adding `P(e) delta varpi` gives (25), hence, on the
`(e,varpi,u)` subspace, (29) with `B=I_C A`. For full-column-rank
`A:R^12 -> R^18`, an invertible row complement gives

\[
(-B,0)K_P^{-1}(B^T,0)^T=0.
\]

The six unselected connection combinations span `ker(A^T)`, and a nonzero
simple planar `P^-_C proportional T^*_{03}` has a two-dimensional Lorentz
centralizer, so `rank(A_P)=4`. This compact algebra is sound **on that stated
subspace**. The pulled-back form is also independent of `g`, however, so if `g`
remains a configuration variable the full primary form has an additional
six-dimensional null sector.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Omitted copy/matching/group rows

The revised complete-potential pullback successfully closes the prior concern
about an omitted **nonzero** induced connection-side or group self-block: under
the clean-shell assumptions, the `xi_g` coefficients cancel exactly, copy
momenta pull back trivially, and no `delta V` or `delta Omega` term is present.
Thus I found no silently discarded non-null kinetic direction in the displayed
pullback.

There is nevertheless a material inventory gap. D017 says both that pullback
occurs before quotienting any Lorentz direction (assumption 7) and that `g`
remains in the configuration matching map (item 2 after (24)), but (28)--(33)
then retain only `(e,varpi,u)`. Since the pulled-back potential is independent
of `g`, retaining `g` gives a zero-momentum primary/null tangent, not permission
to delete it. D006 itself described the shell form on `(u;g,e)` and removed `g`
only “after a local frame choice.” No such frame fixing, quotient, or algebraic
solution for `g` is declared here. Moreover `g` can still enter `bar a`, `K`,
and the matching/background-dependent Hamiltonian, so its preservation cannot
be presumed automatic merely from primary nullness.

**Required fix:** either retain the six `g` primaries/null rows in the reduced
inventory and in the prospective preservation list, or state and justify the
specific clean constraint, coordinate elimination, or gauge quotient that
removes `g`. A quotient would require revising the explicit “before quotienting
any Lorentz direction” premise and cannot be inferred from zero kinetic
coefficient alone.

### 2. Palatini Schur algebra and planar group rank

Conditional on the reduced incidence pattern, the numerical claims survive.
`A` has dimensions 18-by-12 and rank 12 at a nondegenerate spatial triad;
`B=I_C A` has planar rank five; coupling only to the `C_e` half forces the zero
self-Schur correction. Adding null rows to, or changing basis within, an
admissible connection-row complement does not alter that result. The six
`ker(A^T)` combinations and `rank(A_P)=4` are also correct at the declared
linearized planar point.

What does not survive as written is completeness of (33): absent an explicit
elimination of `g`, the primary matrix contains at least an additional
`0_{6x6}` group sector. This does not change the rank-four value of `A_P` or the
zero Palatini correction, but it materially enlarges the null inventory that
must undergo preservation.

### 3. Pre-preservation stopping claim

The narrowed scientific stopping claim is honest in its central point: no
functional trace adjoint or explicit total Hamiltonian has been defined, and
the listed background data are insufficient to compute stabilization. D017
appropriately refuses physical, first-class, or second-class conclusions.

Its wording is still too strong in two places. Equation (40) is introduced as
requiring “every bracket” but omits the surviving `g` primary unless attack 1
is resolved. Also “prior to first preservation” is globally ambiguous because
D017 already obtains (19)--(21) by preservation/free variation and calls them
secondary. The precise claim should be “prior to first preservation of the
post-shell reduced surviving primaries,” with the full surviving list stated.

## Findings

- **Revision required:** the potential pullback is correct under the declared
  local assumptions, but independence from `g` establishes a null direction;
  it does not establish elimination of `g`.
- **Passed conditional check:** (29), the zero Schur correction, six Palatini
  null combinations, and planar `rank(A_P)=4` are correct and
  complement-independent at the declared linearized point.
- **Revision required:** (33), (40), and the strongest conclusion omit the
  `g` null/primary sector or the missing argument that legally removes it.
- **Residual risk:** the clean shell reduction is assumed rather than proved;
  the report should continue to present it as a local conditional result.
- **Malformed/imprecise text:** line 627's literal
  `X=bar X+varepsilon delta X+O(varepsilon^2)` should be proper mathematics,
  `X=\bar X+\varepsilon\,\delta X+O(\varepsilon^2)`. Assumption 8 should also
  distinguish the spacetime pullback on `Sigma` (where `dt` exists) from the
  spatial cut `C` used in the rank calculation.

## Machine-check candidates

1. **Full shell-pullback kernel.** In coordinates `(g,u,e,varpi)` pull back the
   complete one-form and differentiate it. Expected result: (29) on
   `(e,varpi,u)`, zero contraction with every pure `delta g` tangent, and no
   connection-half coupling. Acceptance requires reporting the full kernel,
   not deleting the `g` sector. This leaves Hamiltonian preservation and the
   legitimacy of any quotient unsettled.
2. **Complement/mutation check.** For exact rational planar `A`, `I_C`, and
   `P^-_C`, require `rank A=12`, `rank(I_C A)=5`, zero Schur correction for
   every admissible row complement, six `ker(A^T)` rows, and `rank A_P=4`.
   Adding a controlled connection-half coupling should generically make the
   Schur correction nonzero. A pass would test only the finite linear algebra.
3. **Future preservation inventory.** Once a trace pairing, background, and
   total Hamiltonian are supplied, require a row for every `N_r`, every
   `Phi_u` component, both temporal-momentum families, and either every `g`
   primary or an explicit implemented reduction that removes it. This would
   not by itself establish a physical classification.
