# Internal critique

## Scope and independence

**Prominent limitation:** this is a fresh-session, same-model GPT-5.6 Sol
critique of a derivation produced by `openai/gpt-5.6-sol`. It is procedurally
separate but **not independent verification**, and agreement cannot be counted
as independent confirmation.

I reviewed the frozen D015 claim using only D004--D006, the latest duplicated-
trace decision in `research/DECISIONS.md`, and the specified D010/D013 internal
critiques. The review is limited to the Euler/presymplectic reduction and the
unreduced primary inventory; it does not attempt preservation or a degree count.

## Frozen target

`research/derivations/D015.md` claims that, on a fixed interface and **assuming**
fixed gauge-equivariant one-sided trace maps whose tangent maps lift arbitrary
compactly supported persistent boundary variations, action (10) is Euler-
equivalent and induced-presymplectically equivalent to the fixed-seed
Stueckelberg D005 action after gluing elimination. It further claims that the
unreduced primary matrix has no bulk/copy cross block, while the matrix arising
after gluing preservation requires a trace dual or collar prescription. The
other declared restrictions include the regular D004 equalizer, fixed seed and
circle period, proper temporal/lateral corner conditions, and the persistent
`SO(1,3)` sector.

## Load-bearing inference

The load-bearing inference is the auxiliary-field reduction

\[
 (A_\pm,B_\pm)\text{ equations}\Rightarrow \text{trace gluing},\qquad
 \text{bulk boundary equations}\Rightarrow
 (A_+,B_+,A_-,B_-)=(0,0,0,-P^-),
\]

followed by substitution into the copy equations. If the trace tangent is not
surjective, or if eliminating these variables leaves an endpoint potential,
the claimed equivalence fails and the primary presentation need not describe
the D005 branch.

## Reconstruction

The multiplier equations give (12). After first imposing the ordinary bulk
Euler equations, arbitrary lifted boundary tangents make the four remaining
bulk boundary coefficients vanish. D004 supplies zero persistent core
potential and exterior incidence `-delta(T_-^varpi varpi) wedge P^-`, so (11)
then yields

\[
A_+=B_+=A_-=0,\qquad B_-=-P^-.
\]

The copy equations consequently become

\[
Q+\lambda=0,
\quad P^-+g^{-1}\!\cdot\lambda=0,
\quad H+\mu=0,
\quad g^{-1}\!\cdot\mu=0,
\]

and, together with matching, give D005's shell. Pulling the action back to the
trace-gluing surface makes every gluing integrand identically zero. Since the
gluing terms contain no differentiated field, they contribute no temporal-cut
potential; the surviving `g,u` potential is exactly the D005 induced fixed-seed
potential. Before preservation, gluing occurs only in the canonical Hamiltonian,
not in the Legendre map, so separate bulk and copy defining-primary blocks are
consistent.

## Falsification attempts

1. **Form degrees, incidence signs, and completeness of (11).** Every interface
   pairing is a two-form wedged with a one-form. Moving a varied one-form past a
   two-form gives no sign. The exterior bulk term and exterior gluing term
   combine as `-delta T_-^varpi wedge(P^-+B_-)`; the `V_-` equation is
   `B_--g^{-1}lambda=0`, giving the required D005 sign after `B_-=-P^-`.
   The `g` and `u` integrations by parts reproduce the D005 covariant
   derivatives and endpoint terms. No missing interface variation was found,
   provided the named trace maps and persistent reduction are fixed structures
   (so, in particular, `T_+^omega` has no hidden coframe variation).

2. **Whether trace-surjectivity justifies elimination.** Trace-surjectivity is
   sufficient only in the standard ordered argument: compactly supported
   variations first impose the bulk Euler equations, after which lifted
   boundary variations isolate the coefficients in (11). Under that reading,
   eliminating `A/B` transfers rather than deletes the D005 boundary equations.
   This is a conditional theorem, however: D004 does not prove existence,
   continuity, simultaneous compatibility, or gauge-equivariance of the
   affine persistent connection trace/lift used by D015. D015 mostly states
   this correctly. Its result must not be cited as an existence theorem for
   those trace maps or for a globally compatible collar.

3. **Cut potential and primary inventory.** Direct `2+1` inspection confirms
   that `S_G` has no velocities. The only interface velocities are those of
   `g` and fixed-seed `u` inside the dressed connections, giving the D006
   potential and, after `v=gu`, the `(g,lambda_C)` and `(v,Q_C)` blocks.
   Coframes, copy connections, matching/gluing multipliers, temporal components,
   and the fixed seed add no omitted velocity. Affine connection traces enter
   preservation, not the primary Legendre map. Thus no bulk/copy primary cross
   block is generated. This conclusion does not extend to the secondary matrix:
   matching/gluing brackets there require the deferred trace adjoint/collar
   semantics, exactly as D015 states.

## Findings

- **No blocking issue found** in the conditional Euler reduction, signs, induced
  cut potential, or unreduced primary block decomposition.
- The trace/lifting hypothesis is conclusion-critical and unsupported as an
  existence claim. The result is valid only for a fixed, field-independent
  persistent reduction and trace construction satisfying the stated tangent
  lifting property.
- “No cut potential” applies to the algebraic gluing action, not to the D005
  dressing terms; D015 correctly retains the latter's `g,u` endpoint structure.
- “No bulk-copy cross block” is a primary-order statement only. It gives no
  additive reduced rank, first-class classification, or physical degree count.

## Machine-check candidates

For the director and Scientific Computation, a useful discriminating check is
an exact component variation of (8)--(10), treating the four trace tangents as
named linear operators. Acceptance should require all coefficients and endpoint
terms in (11), followed by symbolic elimination yielding (12)--(15), with
mutations of the exterior incidence sign and either dressing sign forced to
fail. This would check algebra and normalization, but not trace-map existence,
continuum surjectivity, or secondary brackets.

A later collar obligation should be attempted only after its semantics are
frozen: encode a finite trace operator and declared adjoint, verify that the
primary matrix has zero bulk/copy cross block before preservation and that trace
blocks appear after preserving gluing. Passing such a finite check would not
establish the continuum theorem or constraint closure.

## Outcome

`no-blocking-issue-found`

The frozen result survives the bounded attacks as a conditional theorem. It
does not establish that the required trace-surjective affine maps exist, and it
does not settle the trace-aware preservation system.
