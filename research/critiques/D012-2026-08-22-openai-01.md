## Reconstruction

For a genuine field-space functional `F_C`,

`delta(Theta_C + delta F_C) = delta Theta_C`

follows from `delta^2=0`; hence rank and kernel are unchanged. Conversely, the shell one-form `theta=<Q,dv v^-1>` cannot equal `-delta F_C` on an open neighborhood where `delta theta` has nonzero rank.

On the submanifold `delta Q=0`, evaluate the two-form on right-trivialized group tangents `chi_i=delta_i v v^-1`:

`Omega_Q(chi_1,chi_2)=<Q,[chi_1,chi_2]>`.

For the planar nonzero simple bivector `Q=q T_03^*`, invariance of the Lorentz pairing identifies the kernel with the stabilizer of `Q`. The Lorentz brackets give

`z(Q)=span{T_03,T_12}`:

the boost in the `03` plane and rotation in its orthogonal `12` plane commute, while the remaining four generators form two nondegenerate commutator pairings into the `03` component. Thus the pullback on the six group directions has nullity two and rank `6-2=4`. Equivalently it is the pullback to `SO(1,3)` of the four-dimensional coadjoint-orbit form. This confirms (11)-(13) for nonzero simple `Q`; larger stabilizers or `Q=0` are correctly excluded.

The calculation concerns allowed configuration tangents, not by itself a gauge classification. If “fixed `Q`” means one fixed component-valued datum, transformations that move it are not symmetries of that boundary problem even though all `v` directions can be used to calculate the pulled-back orbit form. D012 mostly preserves this distinction.

## Falsification attempts

### 1. Exact versus non-exact boundary shifts — passed, with a wording boundary

Equations (8)-(9) are sound for a single-valued differentiable field-space functional `F_C`. A nonzero `delta<Q,Z>` rules out a local primitive on the stated rank-ten stratum. A term that supplies `-<Q,Z>` as a new canonical potential is therefore not an endpoint shift `delta F_C`; it changes the symplectic structure, restricts the field space, or introduces new variables. Global/multivalued functionals are outside the local claim and should not be conflated with exact shifts. No blocking sign or normalization issue appears here.

### 2. Fixed coframe and fixed momentum versus unchanged D005 equations — revision needed

The coframe-Dirichlet row correctly says that fixing `e_+` removes the `delta e_+` equation `H(K)+mu=0`; exterior free-coframe variation can still imply `mu=0`, but `H(K)=0` no longer follows.

For momentum fixing, however, D012 should state the changed Euler system more precisely. At the planar point `rank(dQ)=5`, `delta Q=0` leaves the three-dimensional kernel of `dQ` among the eight spatial coframe variations. It therefore does not simply delete the core coframe equation: it retains only the projection of `H(K)+mu` onto `ker(dQ)`, while the exterior variation still constrains `mu` under the other stated assumptions. Quoting the full D005 equation `H(K)=0` afterward is an additional restriction. The table's verdict (“different polarization”) is directionally correct, but “same residual orbit calculation as coframe Dirichlet” and the conclusion's collective treatment obscure this projected-equation distinction. This does not overturn rank four, but it matters to the advertised faithful comparison with unchanged D005 dynamics.

### 3. Total moment map (16)-(17) — only a necessary sign target, not derived

D005 supplies the covariant incident variation `-delta varpi wedge P^-`; D006 supplies the interface moment map `Ad_(g^-1)Q` on shell. Their relative sign and matching relation make `Ad_(g^-1)Q-P^-` the necessary cancellation target. But neither dependency derives the exterior spatial-slice symplectic potential, bulk Gauss generator, integrations by parts, corner conditions, or differentiability equation. D012 explicitly concedes this in (17)-(19), yet elsewhere calls (16) the “smallest total exterior corner coefficient” and says the total moment map is “forced.” Those formulations overstate what was computed. Equations (16)-(17) are a necessary relative-sign/completion target; they are not yet a derived total moment map or generator. The stronger frozen wording should be narrowed consistently.

No polarization choice was found to be explicitly mislabeled as a gauge classification: D012 correctly rejects fixed `u`, fixed background, and fixed-`Q` restrictions as proofs of gauge nullness.

## Findings

- **Revision-driving issue:** (16)-(17) must be labeled consistently as a necessary sign and differentiability target, not a derived total bulk-plus-corner moment map.
- **Required clarification:** the fixed-momentum row retains projected coframe equations on `ker(dQ)`; it is neither identical to coframe Dirichlet nor unchanged D005 dynamics.
- **Checks that survived attack:** exact shifts preserve `Omega`; fixed nonzero simple planar `Q` gives centralizer `span{T_03,T_12}` and rank four; fixing `u` removes the block only by tangent-space restriction.
- **Residual risk:** the rank-four statement assumes the full six `v` tangents are present for the orbit-form calculation, while admitted gauge transformations may be only the stabilizer. D012 recognizes this, but the table should keep configuration rank and gauge group visibly separate.
