# D017: Exterior-interface reduced primary algebra and preservation blocker

## Question addressed

In the duplicated exterior-trace presentation of the D005 fixed-seed
Stueckelberg branch, what happens to the five planar `dQ_C` directions after
legitimate defining/gluing/matching reduction, and is the first exterior Dirac
preservation step defined by the currently frozen data?

## Branch charter

This branch uses the complete interface cut potential to reduce the duplicated
copies and matching sector before assembling the exterior Palatini primary
matrix.

## Distinction from sibling branches

This differs from adding the isolated D006 rank-ten block to a bulk rank,
identifying a copy momentum with a boundary-supported bulk distribution, or
treating a zero total moment map as a quotient. I did not inspect D015 or D016.
Parallel same-model work is not independent evidence.

`dQ_C` before matching. No perturbative claim beyond first-order linearization
of the primary matrix is intended.

## Derivation

### 1. Exterior Palatini defining primaries

The exact exterior kinetic term is

\[
L_{4,\mathrm{kin}}
=\int_{B_-}\dot{\boldsymbol\varpi}^{ab}\wedge
 \mathbf P_{ab}(\mathbf e).                                    \tag{11}
\]

The spatial canonical pairs and primary defining constraints are

\[
(\boldsymbol\varpi^{ab}_{(1)},\pi_{\varpi,ab}^{(2)}),
\qquad
(\mathbf e^a_{(1)},\pi_{e,a}^{(2)}),                            \tag{12}
\]
\[
\mathcal C_{\varpi,ab}
:=\pi_{\varpi,ab}-\mathbf P_{ab}(\mathbf e)\simeq0,
\qquad
\mathcal C_{e,a}:=\pi_{e,a}\simeq0.                            \tag{13}
\]

The temporal scalars `varpi_0,e_0` have three-form primary momenta
`pi_varpi0,pi_e0\simeq0`. No copy momentum is identified with any bulk
momentum.

### 2. Explicit gluing/matching action and staged constraints

The exterior-copy gluing action is

\[
S_{\mathrm{glue}}^-
=\int_\Sigma\Big[
r_a\wedge(\epsilon_-^a-j_-^*e^a)
+s_{ab}\wedge(V_-^{ab}-j_-^*\varpi^{ab})\Big].                 \tag{14}
\]

The D005 interface action is

\[
S_I=\int_\Sigma\Big[
K^{ab}\wedge Q_{ab}
+\mu_a\wedge\Delta\epsilon^a
+\lambda_{ab}\wedge\Delta\Omega^{ab}\Big].                   \tag{15}
\]

All terms have total form degree three on `Sigma`.

The constraint stages must not be conflated:

**Primary constraints from the Legendre map.** Besides (13) and the temporal
bulk momenta, all ordinary momenta of

\[
\epsilon_\pm,
\quad V_-,\Omega_+,
\quad r,s,\mu,\lambda                                           \tag{16}
\]

vanish, except for the group momenta generated by the complete cut potential.
In the `(g,v)` chart,

\[
\Theta_C^I
=\int_C\Big[
\langle\lambda_C,\xi_g\rangle
+\langle Q_C,\chi_v\rangle\Big],                               \tag{17}
\]

so

\[
\Phi_g:=p_g-\lambda_C\simeq0,
\qquad
\Phi_v:=p_v-Q_C\simeq0,
\qquad p_{\lambda_C}\simeq0.                                  \tag{18}
\]

The pair `(p_lambdaC,Phi_g)` is the familiar rank-twelve first-order defining
block. It must not be interpreted as the matching equation `lambda=-Q`.

**Secondary algebraic constraints.** Preservation of the temporal gluing and
matching multiplier momenta gives the spatial equations

\[
G_e:=\epsilon_{-,C}-T_e\mathbf e=0,
\qquad
G_\varpi:=V_{-,C}-T_\varpi\boldsymbol\varpi=0,                  \tag{19}
\]
\[
\Delta\epsilon_C=0,
\qquad
\Delta\Omega_C=0.                                               \tag{20}
\]

Preservation/free variation of the core and exterior copy fields supplies the
remaining algebraic shell equations

\[
Q+\lambda=0,
\qquad
P^-+g^{-1}\!\cdot\lambda=0,
\qquad
H(K)+\mu=0,
\qquad
g^{-1}\!\cdot\mu=0.                                            \tag{21}
\]

Thus (3) is a secondary shell, not part of the initial primary matrix. The copy
momenta, equations (19)--(21), and their reaction multipliers form the
configuration/conormal sector assumed clean in assumption 7.

### 3. Complete shell-potential reduction

This step closes the matching/group gap without postulating a partial Dirac
matrix. Start from the complete cut potential (17), not from either term alone.
Using (10),

\[
\Theta_C^I
=\int_C\Big[
\langle\lambda_C,\xi_g\rangle
+\langle Q_C,\xi_g+\operatorname{Ad}_g\zeta_u\rangle\Big].      \tag{22}
\]

Pull back first to `lambda=-Q`. The two `g` terms cancel exactly:

\[
\left.\Theta_C^I\right|_{\lambda=-Q}
=\int_C\langle Q_C,\operatorname{Ad}_g\zeta_u\rangle.          \tag{23}
\]

Then use `Q=g\cdot P^-` and invariance of the pairing:

\[
\boxed{
\left.\Theta_C^I\right|_{\mathrm{shell}}
=\int_C\langle P^-_C,\zeta_u\rangle.}                          \tag{24}
\]

Equation (24) displays the fate of every disputed group/copy piece:

1. `p_lambdaC` and `Phi_g` form the ordinary defining pair before shell
   pullback; `lambda=-Q` then cancels the complete `g` kinetic coefficient.
2. `g` remains in the configuration matching map, but it has no surviving cut
   kinetic block on the shell. This is cancellation in the complete potential,
   not deletion of a group row by fiat.
3. The exterior copy momentum remains zero. Equations (19) replace
   `epsilon_-` by the regular trace of `e`; they never set its momentum equal to
   a bulk distribution.
4. The core-copy momenta remain zero. Equations (20)--(21) replace
   `epsilon_+` by `g epsilon_-` and its momentum polynomial `Q` by `g.P^-`.
5. Connection matching replaces `V_-,Omega_+` by matched traces in the
   Hamiltonian constraints, but it induces no cut kinetic connection term in
   (24). Any such term would have appeared as a surviving `delta V`,
   `delta Omega`, or group coefficient in the pullback of (17); none does.
6. The factorization `v=gu` is undone. The boundary group coordinate carrying
   momentum is `u`, with momentum `P^-_C(e)`. The coordinate `g` is **retained**:
   because (24) is independent of `delta g`, its shell-pulled Legendre image has
   six zero-momentum primaries
   \[
   \Psi_g:=p_g\simeq0.                                          \tag{24a}
   \]
   Thus `g` is a six-dimensional null sector, not a deleted coordinate. There
   is no unexplained residual `v` block and no Lorentz quotient or frame fixing.
7. A full seed-orbit right quotient is not introduced. With fixed `a_0`, the
   only right kernel is its exact stabilizer; proper outer-corner conditions
   leave no additional local generator in this branch.

Under assumption 7, pullback of the complete potential is equivalent to
eliminating the clean copy/matching conormal pairs in any legal order. This
proves their **net presymplectic effect** without requiring an undeclared
distributional trace bracket.

### 4. Reduced exterior primary potential and finite matrix

Adding the exterior Palatini potential to (24) gives

\[
\boxed{
\Theta_{\mathrm{red}}
=\int_{B_-}\mathbf P_{ab}(\mathbf e)\wedge
 \delta\boldsymbol\varpi^{ab}
+\int_C\langle P^-_C(\mathbf e),\delta u\,u^{-1}\rangle.}       \tag{25}
\]

No additional **nonzero** group/connection block is hidden: the only nonzero
group self-block is the Maurer--Cartan coadjoint block generated by the second
term of (25). The retained `g` coordinate contributes the separate zero block
associated with (24a).

Choose a local finite chart at one boundary point. Let

\[
A=d\mathbf P:\mathbb R^{12}\to\mathbb R^{18},
\qquad
B=dP^-_C=I_C A:\mathbb R^{12}\to\mathbb R^6,                   \tag{26}
\]

where `I_C` is the declared finite trace incidence. On a nondegenerate spatial
triad,

\[
\operatorname{rank}A=12,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}B=5                                         \tag{27}
\]

on the planar interface stratum. The reduced defining primaries are

\[
\mathcal C_e=p_e\simeq0,
\qquad
\mathcal C_\varpi=p_\varpi-\mathbf P(\mathbf e)\simeq0,
\qquad
\Phi_u=p_u-P^-_C(\mathbf e)\simeq0,
\qquad
\Psi_g=p_g\simeq0.                                              \tag{28}

Their linearized bracket matrix is

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb M_{\mathrm{red}}
=\begin{pmatrix}
0&-A^T&B^T&0\\
A&0&0&0\\
-B&0&A_{P}&0\\
0&0&0&0_6
\end{pmatrix}_{(\mathcal C_e,\mathcal C_\varpi,\Phi_u,\Psi_g)},
\qquad
A_P(X,Y)=\langle P^-_C,[X,Y]\rangle.}                           \tag{29}

The boundary derivative `B` couples only to the coframe-momentum half
`C_e`. This incidence pattern is now derived from the complete potential (25),
not assumed after a partial matching matrix. The final row and column display

### 5. Palatini defining reduction, six null combinations, and complement independence

Since `A` is an `18 x 12` full-column-rank map, `ker A^T` is six-dimensional.
Choose a decomposition of the eighteen connection-primary rows into

\[
\mathcal C_\varpi=(\mathcal C_\varpi^c,N_r),
\qquad r=1,\ldots,6,                                            \tag{30}
\]

such that the `12 x 12` restriction `A_c` associated with
`C_varpi^c` is invertible and the `N_r` span `ker A^T`. The manifest
nondegenerate defining block is

\[
K_P=\begin{pmatrix}0&-A_c^T\\A_c&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
K_P^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}0&A_c^{-1}\\-A_c^{-T}&0\end{pmatrix}.  \tag{31}
\]

Because `Phi_u` couples only to `C_e`, its Schur self-correction is

\[
(-B,0)K_P^{-1}\binom{B^T}{0}=0.                                 \tag{32}
\]

This zero is conditional on precisely the assumptions used to derive (29): a
local nondegenerate triad, a finite incidence `I_C`, and no connection-half
coupling from an extra corner/seed potential. It is not a statement about a
singular trace or degenerate Palatini stratum.

The result is complement-independent. Any two admissible complements differ by
an invertible change among the twelve eliminated rows plus additions of the
null rows `N_r`. The inverse Palatini form still has zero `C_e-C_e` block, while
`B` has no component on the connection half. Hence (32) is unchanged. This is
the coordinate expression of restricting the same presymplectic form (25), not
a preferred choice of twelve connection components.

After this defining reduction, the surviving pointwise primary matrix is

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb M_{\mathrm{surv}}
=0_{6\times6}^{(N)}\oplus A_P\oplus0_{6\times6}^{(g)}
\quad\text{on}\quad
(N_1,\ldots,N_6;\Phi_u;\Psi_g).}                               \tag{33}
\]

At the planar point,

\[
P^-_C=\frac{1}{2\kappa_4}T_{03}^*\,dx^1\wedge dx^2,             \tag{34}
\]

so the centralizer is `span(T_03,T_12)` and

\[
\operatorname{rank}A_P=4,
\qquad
\dim\ker A_P=2.                                                 \tag{35}
\]

Thus the five `dQ_C` cross-pairs of the isolated interface presentation do not
survive as five additional defining pairs. They are absorbed into the ordinary
Palatini defining reduction. What remains at the primary level is six
Palatini connection-primary null combinations and a rank-four coadjoint group
block with two null directions, together with six zero-momentum `g` primaries.
The extra `g` zero block changes neither the Schur correction (32) nor
`rank(A_P)=4`. None of these fourteen null directions is called physical,
gauge, or finally second class before preservation.

### 6. Exact matching and total-Gauss tangency identity

From `epsilon_+=g epsilon_-`, `b=kappa_4^-1`, and (9),

\[
Q=g\cdot P^- .                                                  \tag{36}
\]

For `xi_g=delta g g^-1`, its exact tangent relation is

\[
\boxed{
\delta Q=[\xi_g,Q]+g\cdot\delta P^-.}                           \tag{37}
\]

The total exterior Lorentz boundary moment map in the unshifted polarization is

\[
B_-=-P^- -\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\lambda.                    \tag{38}
\]

On `lambda=-Q`, its tangent equation is

\[
\delta B_-
=-\delta P^-+
 \operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}(\delta Q-[\xi_g,Q])=0.              \tag{39}
\]

Substitution of (37) makes (39) an identity. Hence zero total moment map and
its tangency impose no additional rank on the five momentum directions after
matching. They are necessary consistency relations, not the missing quotient.

### 7. Preservation is not yet a defined Dirac operation

The reduced-primary algebra (29)--(35) is finite and fully stated. A first
Dirac preservation step would additionally require every bracket

\[
\dot N_r=\{N_r,H_T\},
\qquad
\dot\Phi_u=\{\Phi_u,H_T\},
\qquad
\dot\Psi_g=\{\Psi_g,H_T\},
\qquad
\dot\pi_{e_0}=\{\pi_{e_0},H_T\},
\qquad
\dot\pi_{\varpi_0}=\{\pi_{\varpi_0},H_T\}                      \tag{40}
\]

in one declared phase space. The current frozen data do not define these
brackets. A pointwise incidence `I_C` specifies `B` in (29), but not a
functional trace domain, boundary pairing, or adjoint `T_e^*` needed to compare
the boundary variation of the `u` term with bulk coframe variations. Nor does
the planar induced triad specify the exterior spatial curvature, temporal
coframe/connection, or reference data that enter the linearized Hamiltonian.

Accordingly, no honest surviving-primary stabilization table can yet be
filled. The precise status is:

| surviving primary | covariant equation expected from the action | why its Hamiltonian preservation row is undefined |
|---|---|---|
| six `N_r` in `ker A^T` | projections of the exterior connection/torsion chain | requires the explicit total Hamiltonian and background-dependent projection basis |
| six components of `Phi_u` | fixed-seed orbit equation `D_bar a Q=0`, plus its coupling to matching | requires the trace adjoint and explicit `a_0,K,bar a` background |
| six components of `Psi_g=p_g` | tangency of the shell-pulled matching/frame sector | although absent from the pulled-back potential, `g` can enter `bar a`, `K`, and matching-dependent Hamiltonian terms; their explicit reduced Hamiltonian dependence is required |
| `pi_varpi0` | spatial torsion/Gauss equation | requires the declared functional bulk-boundary pairing |
| `pi_e0` | spatial coframe equation | requires `mathbf R`, cosmological/background data, and boundary conditions |

The covariant Euler equations are retained only as **targets** identifying the
missing coefficients:

\[
\mathcal T_{ab}
=\frac1{2\kappa_4}\epsilon_{abcd}
 \mathbf t^c\wedge\mathbf e^d=0,                                \tag{41}
\]
\[
\mathcal E_a
=\frac1{2\kappa_4}\epsilon_{abcd}
 \mathbf R^{bc}\wedge\mathbf e^d
-\frac{\Lambda_4}{6\kappa_4}\epsilon_{abcd}
 \mathbf e^b\wedge\mathbf e^c\wedge\mathbf e^d=0,             \tag{42}
\]
\[
D_{\bar a}Q=0.                                                   \tag{43}
\]

Writing `Q=Q_C+dt wedge Q_t`, equation (43) has temporal coefficient

\[
\dot Q_C+[\bar a_0,Q_C]-D_CQ_t=0.                               \tag{44}
\]

Equations (41)--(44) do not constitute a Dirac stabilization matrix. They show
that its coefficients depend on `mathbf R,e_0,varpi_0,a_0,K,bar a`, on the
trace domain/pairing, and on the admissible boundary conditions.

Therefore the exact stopping obstruction is **prior to first preservation of
the post-shell reduced surviving primaries**: the earlier preservation of
gluing/matching multiplier momenta has already produced the secondary shell
(19)--(21), but preservation of `(N_r,Phi_u,Psi_g,pi_varpi0,pi_e0)` is not
defined until the additional data are supplied. It would be incorrect to claim
that all four physical outcomes have been tested by a completed post-shell
preservation step.

### 8. Narrow outcome test

The reduced-primary algebra permits only these bounded statements:

1. **Autonomous rank-ten remnant:** excluded at the defining-primary level
   under assumptions 7--8, because the zero Schur correction absorbs the five
   cross-pairs into the exterior Palatini defining reduction.
2. **Regular first-class removal:** not tested. The total moment-map tangency is
   the identity (39), not a quotient, and the surviving null rows have not been
   preserved. In particular, primary nullness of `g` is not a Lorentz quotient.
3. **Physical reduced two-form:** not tested. `A_P` is a rank-four intermediate
   primary block, not a physical symplectic form.
4. **Second-class remnant:** not tested. Its verdict requires brackets of
   `N_r` and the two null combinations of `Phi_u` with the secondary system.

The classification is therefore underdetermined because preservation of the
post-shell reduced surviving primaries is not yet defined, not because a
completed preservation matrix has an unknown numerical rank.

No persistent exterior equation sources a mixed `SO(1,4)` row. This does not
prove that the core mixed truncation is preserved; that requires the omitted
five-dimensional core equations and is outside this bounded exterior result.

## Dimensional check

With `[e]=[epsilon]=L`, `[varpi]=[V]=[a]=[g]=[u]=1`, and
`[kappa_4]=L^2`,

\[
[P^-]=[Q]=[\lambda]=[s]=1,
\qquad
[\mu]=[r]=L^{-1}.                                                \tag{45}
\]

Every term in (14), (15), (17), (24), and (25) is dimensionless after form
integration. `A` and `B` both differentiate dimensionless momenta with respect
to a length-valued coframe, so the corresponding matrix entries have matching
dimensions. The factor `2pi` appears only through
`b=2pi gamma=kappa_4^-1`.

Sign and normalization checks:

1. On the planar cut,
   `P^-_03=(2 kappa_4)^-1 dx1 wedge dx2`, agreeing with D006/D008's ordered-pair
   factor.
2. The two `xi_g` terms in (22) have opposite coefficients only when
   `lambda=-Q`; a sign mutation leaves a nonzero `g` block.
3. The exterior incident sign in (38) makes (39) vanish. Reversing only that
   sign doubles, rather than cancels, the planar area momentum.
4. Under a nonidentity dressing, (37) requires the frame term `[xi_g,Q]`.
   Omitting it breaks covariance and (39).
5. Orientation reversal changes `P,Q`, both terms in (24), and both terms in
   (39) together; ranks are unchanged.
6. `A_P` is adjoint-covariant. Although (34) uses an adapted `03` label, its
   rank and two-dimensional centralizer are conjugacy invariants.
