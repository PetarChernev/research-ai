# D034: Adversarial dimensionality and predictivity audit

## Question addressed

Map baseline theories for dynamical dimensionality: can a non-prescribed extra-spatial-direction region have autonomous dynamics and a calculable energy or probe response? Compare literal, emergent, and operational meanings without assuming the prior Palatini circle-collapse formulation.

## Branch charter

Slot H. Challenge the broad intuition without presuming the old formulation. Distinguish intrinsic, emergent and probe-relative dimension and formulate minimum operational tests for an extra direction. Derive at most three scoped obstructions or countercontrols addressing autonomous compact support, information under disappearance/reopening, and species/universal observability. Identify compatible weakened requirement sets and tests that could genuinely falsify future toy models. Do not claim a general continuum no-go from D001 or D021. Do not inspect siblings. Stop with a concise admissibility map and decision rubric.

## Distinction from sibling branches

This is a necessary-condition audit, not a construction or a vote for one
ontology. It tests three logically separable promises using ODE uniqueness,
reduced-state transfer, and two-probe principal symbols. Only the frozen packet,
QUESTION, this scaffold, D001, and D021 were read; no sibling outputs were
inspected. Parallel GPT production is not independent evidence.

## Target claim

**Target:** determine which combinations of exact support, autonomy,
information preservation, and geometric universality survive three cheap
attacks. No universal impossibility theorem is assumed. A dimension diagnostic
must not turn absence of experimental access into absence of physical states.

## Assumptions

**Assumptions, local to their tests:** (1) a canonical real scalar on flat
`3+1` spacetime with a regular potential, then its massless control; (2) smooth
Hamiltonian evolution of complete reduced data, with no omitted boundary flux;
(3) positive quadratic scalar probes in a locally homogeneous region with a
fixed auxiliary spatial coordinate. These are separate diagnostic systems,
not sectors of a proposed unified theory.

Use `c=hbar=1`, signature `(-,+,+,+)`, and one persistent time. Scalar
variations vanish at temporal endpoints and spatial infinity. A static planar
attachment is `C^1`, has no surface source, and is zero on one side. Wave data
are smooth and compactly supported on an initial Cauchy slice. Canonical
reduction precedes physical state comparisons; charged boundary transformations
are not automatically gauge. Probe directions are noncompact locally, or
periodic with explicitly stated length. No gravitational equation is assumed.

## Notation

**Definitions and operational tests:** `d` is total spacetime dimension;
`n=d-1` is spatial dimension. On regular intrinsic strata, test local covering
dimension and dimension of derivations of the specified physical function
algebra. Require both to be `d=4` outside and `d=5` inside, with no exterior
fiber states. Coordinate/coframe rank alone fails this test. Singular-interface
dimension needs its own category, not continuation of a chart label.

For probe species `a`, a positive spatial operator `L_a` defines return density
`P_a(tau)` and spectral dimension `d_s,a=-2 d log P_a/d log tau`, where
`tau` has dimension length squared. Also require a propagation experiment:
independently resolved endpoints and momentum variation along the proposed
extra direction. A spectral slope alone can represent internal degeneracy or
nonstandard dispersion. This is **operational dimension**, indexed by species,
state, boundary conditions, and energy window.

**Emergent geometric dimension** means a stated coarse-graining limit supplies
local neighborhoods, measure, and approximately common probe principal metric,
with controlled errors across a nonzero scale window. Finite spectra or one
matching exponent do not establish that limit. These are proposed acceptance
definitions, not derived existence claims.

## Derivation

### Attack 1 — Exact equilibrium support is stronger than autonomous motion

Let `phi` be a varied selector, not a prescribed profile, with exact action

\[
 S=\int d^4x\left[-\tfrac12\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\phi-V(\phi)\right],
 \qquad \ddot\phi-\nabla^2\phi+V'(\phi)=0.                 \tag{1}
\]

**Assume** `V'(0)=0` and locally Lipschitz `V'`. A static planar profile
`phi(s)` satisfies the exact ODE `phi''=V'(phi)`. If it joins the identically
zero exterior at finite `s=0` without a derivative jump, its data are
`phi(0)=phi'(0)=0`. Writing `Y=(phi,phi')` gives
`Y'=(Y_2,V'(Y_1))`, a locally Lipschitz first-order system. The zero solution
has the same data; local uniqueness forces zero throughout the connected
classical continuation. **Derived obstruction:** this canonical regular
planar model cannot have a nontrivial exactly supported static selector.
This is an ODE statement, not multidimensional unique continuation or D021's
curvature theorem. Surface sources, degenerate gradient terms, nonsmooth
forces, and varied domains invalidate its hypotheses.

**Exact countercontrol:** set `V=0` and take compact initial data in a ball
of radius `R`. With

\[
 e=\tfrac12(\dot\phi^2+|\nabla\phi|^2),\quad
 j=-\dot\phi\nabla\phi,\quad \partial_t e+\nabla\cdot j=0,
 \quad |j|\le e,                                             \tag{2}
\]

the energy outside the expanding ball obeys
`dE_out/dt=integral_(r=R+t)(j_r-e)dA <=0`. Initially it is zero and energy
is nonnegative, so it stays zero. Decay at infinity fixes `phi=0` there.
The support evolves autonomously with finite conserved energy and exact
exterior zeros. This establishes propagation of an initially present region,
not formation from the exact vacuum, stable equilibrium, or intrinsic extra
dimension. Defining `Omega(t)={phi!=0}` is only a selector convention until
an actual ontology/probe coupling is supplied. Thus exact support plus autonomy
is not itself contradictory.

### Attack 2 — Reopening needs complete data, not equal local mode counts

Let `F:P_in -> P_mid` map complete incoming reduced states to collapsed-time
states. If smooth reversible Hamiltonian transport holds, its exact condition
is

\[
 F^*\Omega_{mid}=\Omega_{in}.                                \tag{3}
\]

If `DF(v)=0`, then for every `w`,
`Omega_in(v,w)=Omega_mid(DF(v),DF(w))=0`; nondegeneracy implies `v=0`.
Hence **derived obstruction:** a smooth symplectic transfer cannot delete a
physical tangent direction. In a finite-dimensional truncation this implies
`dim P_mid >= dim P_in`. More basically, if `F(z_1)=F(z_2)` for distinct
physical states, a single-valued reopening law `G` cannot satisfy
`G(F(z_i))=z_i` for both. Forward dissipative predictivity alone does not
require such reversibility; calling it information preserving would be wrong.

**Countercontrols:** fields in different spatial dimensions need not have
different finite phase-space dimensions; both may have infinitely many modes.
Radiation and interface fields can carry the data. For a finite illustration,
let `(q,p)` be a disappearing pair and `(Q,P)` a retained pair. The transfer
`(q',p',Q',P')=(Q,P,q,p)` preserves
`dq wedge dp+dQ wedge dP` exactly. Incoming core information can reside in
retained variables rather than in a surviving extra coordinate. This swap is
an algebraic control, not a disappearance action.

In fields, apply (3) only after including integrated symplectic flux and edge
variables: a subregion is not closed merely because its bulk has shrunk.
Already-gauged null directions are not physical losses; nonzero surface
charges cannot be quotiented by declaration. The first failure of the naive
mapping is therefore identifying a local bulk mode count with the complete
reduced state space. No field-theoretic dimension no-go follows.

### Attack 3 — Identical dimension exponents do not imply one geometry

For two independent massless test fields on local coordinates `(t,x,y)`,
with `x` three-dimensional, take the exact quadratic densities

\[
 \mathcal L_a=\tfrac12\left[\dot\psi_a^2-|\nabla_x\psi_a|^2
                         -c_a^2(\partial_y\psi_a)^2\right],
 \qquad c_a>0.                                               \tag{4}
\]

Fourier substitution gives `omega^2=|k|^2+c_a^2 k_y^2`. Both kinetic
energies are positive. The Gaussian return density per coordinate volume is

\[
 P_a(\tau)=\int {d^3k\,dk_y\over(2\pi)^4}
 e^{-\tau(|k|^2+c_a^2k_y^2)}
 ={1\over c_a(4\pi\tau)^2},\qquad d_{s,a}=4.                 \tag{5}
\]

Yet if `c_1!=c_2`, their characteristic forms
`diag(-1,1,1,1,c_a^2)` are not proportional: the time entry fixes the
proportionality to one. A common coordinate transformation cannot make
nonproportional forms proportional. **Derived obstruction:** equal spatial
spectral dimension does not establish probe-universal causal geometry.
Species-dependent coordinate rescalings evade only a comparison that discards
common events and rods. Conversely `c_1=c_2` supplies a common principal
metric for these two probes, not universal interactions or gravity.

For periodic `y` of length `ell`, replace the last integral by
`ell^-1 sum_m exp[-tau c_a^2(2pi m/ell)^2]`. At
`tau >> ell^2/c_a^2` only the zero mode remains and `d_s,a ->3`.
This is accessible `3+1` behavior while the physical fifth direction remains.

## Approximations

Equations (1)–(5) are exact in their declared models; no perturbative expansion
or numerical fit is used. The compact-probe large-`tau` limit has relative
correction `O(exp[-4pi^2 tau c_a^2/ell^2])`. Treating (4) as a probe of a
varying medium would additionally require a local wavelength/variation-scale
expansion; no such background or error bound is claimed here.

## Validity regime

The support obstruction is classical, static, planar, and source-free. The
wave control is a flat-space initial-value example. Equation (3) concerns
complete reduced states where a smooth symplectic map exists; singular strata
need separate treatment. Equation (5) assumes homogeneous positive coefficients
and the specified measure. None establishes a finite-energy dimensional
particle, nucleation, gravity, or quantum unitarity.

## Dimensional check

`[x]=[y]=[t]=L`, `[phi]=L^-1`, `[V]=[e]=[j]=L^-4`, and
`[V']=L^-3=[phi'']`; total wave energy has dimension `L^-1`.
In (4), `[psi_a]=L^-3/2` for the five-coordinate action, `c_a` is dimensionless,
`[k]=[omega]=L^-1`, and `[P_a]=L^-4`. Canonical pairs are normalized so
`[qp]=[QP]=1`, making (3) dimensionless.

## Limiting-case checks

The zero scalar vacuum solves (1) and stays zero under unique evolution;
motion is not vacuum nucleation. Equation (2) checks signs and energy flux.
The swap preserves canonical normalization. Equal probe speeds remove the
two-species cone mismatch. At `c_a=0` the noncompact heat integral diverges,
not smoothly becoming a three-dimensional trace: infinitely many frozen
`y` labels remain. Proper state removal differs from a vanishing coefficient.

## Conclusion

**Derived, scoped admissibility map — not existence or independent verification:**

| Compatible requirement set, not excluded here | Declared relaxation / remaining gate |
|---|---|
| Autonomous, exact transient support; positive energy | Initial region allowed; intrinsic dimension and equilibrium unproved |
| Regular scalar equilibrium with nonzero tails | Relax exact support; derive finite energy and stability separately |
| Reversible disappearance with radiation/edge storage or prior gauge reduction | Include full retained state and charges; construct transition dynamics |
| Scale-dependent accessible direction with species-dependent probes | Relax literal disappearance and universal geometry; report probe/window |
| Exact intrinsic strata plus complete transfer and common probe metric | Strong target remains open; none of the three tests supplies it |

**Cheap decision rubric / staged roadmap:** first label ontology and retained
states; reject semantic upgrades. Next demand a varied selector and one energy
or probe equation; distinguish initial-region motion, formation, and stability.
Then choose only the relevant discriminator above: endpoint uniqueness for
static exact support, transfer injectivity for disappearance, or two-species
cones for universality. A failure rejects that conjunction of promises, not
every dimensional theory. Only survivors merit transition well-posedness and
stability work. Producer: **`openai/gpt-6-astra`**. Evidence category:
**analytic necessary-condition diagnostics with exact countercontrols**.
