## Target claim

**Derived-result target:** a positive-energy classical scalar theory admits moving interfaces between zero and nonzero internal hopping, and its homogeneous-core probe spectrum is
\[
\boxed{\omega_n^2=|\mathbf k|^2+m^2+4g^2q_0^2\sin^2(\pi n/N).}

At zero links there remain **N exterior probe modes**, not one. A finite internal direction is operationally meaningful through transport and, for large N, a restricted continuum-like dispersion window. Intrinsic total spacetime dimension remains d=4 everywhere.

## Assumptions

- **Model assumptions:** fixed Minkowski base, signature (-,+,+,+), natural units; a fixed cycle with integer N≥4; real canonical fields; g, λ, v positive and m²≥0. No gauge quotient, gravity, or site-identifying constraint.
- Every link shares a single varied strength q(x). This correlation is a model restriction, not a result of independent link alignment. Site-local preparation/readout is physically allowed.

## Derivation

### 1. Autonomous action and link equation — exact

Take the single construction
\[
S=\int d^4x\left[-\frac12\partial_\mu q\partial^\mu q-V(q)
-\frac12\sum_j(\partial_\mu\phi_j\partial^\mu\phi_j+m^2\phi_j^2)
-\frac{g^2q^2}{2}\sum_j(\Delta_j\phi)^2\right].
\]
Every cycle edge is counted once. Variation of its last term with respect to q gives −g²qΣ_j(Δ_jφ)²δq; V′=λq(q−v)(2q−v). Thus the **exact equations of motion** are
\[
\boxed{\Box q-\lambda q(q-v)(2q-v)-g^2q\sum_j(\Delta_j\phi)^2=0,}
\qquad (\Box-m^2)\phi_j-g^2q^2(L\phi)_j=0.
\]
With π_q=∂_tq and π_j=∂_tφ_j, the Hamiltonian density is
\[
\mathcal H=\tfrac12[\pi_q^2+|\nabla q|^2]+V(q)
+\tfrac12\sum_j[\pi_j^2+|\nabla\phi_j|^2+m^2\phi_j^2
+g^2q^2(\Delta_j\phi)^2]\ge0.
\]
No kinetic rank changes at q=0. The coupled principal part remains N+1 ordinary wave operators. Translation invariance conserves the full stress-energy; an externally frozen q would not describe its energy exchange with probes.

### 2. What selects and moves the region — exact diagnostic, bounded approximation

At φ=0, a static planar wall satisfies q″=V′(q). Multiplying by q′ and using the vacuum endpoints gives (q′)²/2=V. Choosing the increasing branch gives q′=√λ q(v−q), hence
\[
q(z)=\frac{v}{1+e^{-\sqrt\lambda v(z-z_0)}},\qquad
\sigma=\int dz\,[\tfrac12(q')^2+V]
=\int_0^v dq\sqrt{2V}=\frac{\sqrt\lambda v^3}{6}.
\]
Its Lorentz boost, with z−z_0 replaced by γ(z−ut−z_0), is an exact moving solution for |u|<1. There is no imposed spacetime-dependent coupling or interface trajectory. Region boundaries may be diagnosed by the level set q=v/2; this does not make that level a physical singularity.

For a localized three-space example, specify smooth initial data with q=v inside a ball, q=0 outside a larger ball, an interpolating shell, and ∂_tq=φ=0. Energy is finite and the equations determine its subsequent motion. Finite base propagation speed preserves an exactly zero exterior beyond the causal enlargement of the initial support. This establishes an initial region with autonomous evolution, not nucleation from the exact vacuum. Smooth wall solutions themselves have tails, not exact stationary compact support.

For a large nearly planar spherical shell of radius R and thickness δ=(√λv)⁻¹, the leading thin-wall energy is 4πσR². The collective action −4πσR²√(1−Ṙ²) gives R̈=−2/R at rest: equal-energy vacua provide tension but no balancing volume pressure. This estimate does not assert an exact bubble solution; stability is not obtained.

### 3. Probe spectrum and actual internal transport

Linearize at φ=0 in either homogeneous vacuum q_0=0 or v. Probe backreaction on q starts quadratically in φ, so the linear probe sector decouples exactly. With normalized eigenvectors
\[
u_j^{(n)}=N^{-1/2}e^{2\pi i nj/N},\quad
Lu^{(n)}=[2-2\cos(2\pi n/N)]u^{(n)},
\]
inserting φ_j=u_j^{(n)}e^{−iωt+i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x} gives the boxed target spectrum. Complex notation imposes conjugate pairing for real fields: n and N−n represent two real modes except n=0 and, for even N, n=N/2. There are precisely N real probe oscillators per base momentum.

For q_0>0 and small internal momentum p_n=2πn/L_int (with signed n near zero),
\[
\omega_n^2=|\mathbf k|^2+m^2+p_n^2
-\frac{a^2p_n^4}{12}+O(a^4p_n^6).
\]
This is an extra spacelike propagation dispersion to leading order, not an exact continuum fifth dimension. A many-mode window requires N≫1 and 2π/L_int≪|p|≪a⁻¹. N=4 is the smallest chosen diagnostic ring, not a continuum approximation.

Mass eigenvalues alone could describe flavors. The stronger witness is a site-local response: at base momentum zero, initialize φ_j(0)=Aδ_{j0}, ∂_tφ_j(0)=0. The equation gives
\[
\phi_1(t)=\tfrac12 A g^2q_0^2t^2+O(t^4).
\]
Neighbor readout is nonzero for an open link and identically zero for q_0=0 in the linear homogeneous problem. Repeated nearest-neighbor evolution transports a disturbance around the cycle. Fourier diagonalization does not erase this response; it changes the representation of local sources and detectors. Conversely, without the physically specified local coupling algebra, calling the same finite mass matrix an extra dimension would add no testable content.

### 4. Exterior accounting — exact

At q_0=0, all N masses are m: links vanish but neither canonical coordinates nor initial data vanish. There is additionally one selector fluctuation with mass² V″(0)=λv²; at q=v its mass² is the same. Thus the vacuum has N+1 scalar degrees of freedom, all positive-kinetic. For m=0, all N exterior probes are massless. For m>0, a threshold above m accesses N, while below m it accesses none—not a distinguished single probe.

Taking gq_0→∞ at fixed N instead sends N−1 relative-mode gaps to infinity and leaves the uniform mode. This is a low-energy projection in a singular parameter limit, not zero hopping and not disappearance of sites. Taking q_0→0 does the opposite: a→∞ and every internal gap closes. These limits directly block an interpretation of the zero-link exterior as an otherwise identical one-species vacuum.
