# D015: Duplicated-trace action and primary inventory

## Question addressed

Does the adopted duplicated-trace presentation of the fixed-interface planar D005 Stueckelberg branch yield a faithful combined Palatini/interface primary system whose first preservation classifies the residual C016 rank-ten block, or does it expose a precise coupled-sector obstruction?

## Branch charter

Using the director decision `Freeze duplicated traces and proper outer-corner gauge for the first convergence cycle`, define the extended action with independent one-sided persistent copies `(epsilon_+,Omega_+)` and `(epsilon_-,V_-)`, explicit covariant gluing multipliers to the regular D004 bulk traces, and the unchanged D005 matching/Stueckelberg interface functional evaluated on those copies. State every bundle frame, form degree, trace map, sign, and gauge action. Use fixed seed `a_0`, no extra seed/corner potential, small core/exterior Lorentz transformations allowed at the internal cut but vanishing at temporal endpoints and the lateral boundary. Derive the complete 2+1/3+1 canonical variable and primary-constraint inventory and the nonzero defining-primary brackets. Prove or refute classical and presymplectic equivalence to the intrinsic-trace D005 action by eliminating the gluing Euler pairs; do not infer additive rank before that reduction. Stop with an exact obstruction if the trace map or elimination is not well-defined; do not claim a final physical degree count.

## Distinction from sibling branches

This branch tests the director-frozen **duplicated-trace** presentation itself.
It does not use an intrinsic/distributional boundary momentum and it does not
perform the first Dirac-preservation calculation.  Its distinctive device is to
keep the bulk boundary values, four independent interface copies, and all four
gluing multiplier pairs separate through the Legendre map, and only then to
eliminate the gluing Euler equations.  This directly attacks the ambiguity
identified by the critique of D010.  In accordance with the first-pass barrier,
I did not inspect the parallel sibling artifacts D016 or D017.  Parallel GPT
sessions are procedural separation, not independent evidence.

## Target claim

The target is the following exact, nonperturbative proposition.  Given regular
one-sided trace maps with the lifting property stated below, the extended action
(15) is classically Euler-equivalent and, in the induced pullback polarization,
presymplectically equivalent to the fixed-seed Stueckelberg version of D005.
Its unreduced primary system is nevertheless a different first-order
presentation: bulk and copy coframe momenta are separate, and gluing appears
only upon preservation of multiplier momenta.  The target also asks whether the
unreduced primary Poisson matrix is defined without a distributional trace
bracket.  No final gauge or physical degree count is sought.

## Derivation

### 1. Exact extended action

The unchanged bulk actions are
\[
\begin{split}
S_5={}&\int_{M_+}\left[{1\over12\kappa_5}\epsilon_{ABCDE}R^{AB}
 \wedge E^C\wedge E^D\wedge E^E
-{\Lambda_5\over120\kappa_5}\epsilon_{ABCDE}E^A\wedge\cdots\wedge E^E\right],\\
S_4={}&\int_{M_-}\left[{1\over4\kappa_4}\epsilon_{abcd}{\cal R}^{ab}
 \wedge e^c\wedge e^d
-{\Lambda_4\over24\kappa_4}\epsilon_{abcd}e^a\wedge e^b\wedge e^c\wedge e^d\right].
                                                                    \tag{7}
\end{split}
\]
Every interface integral below uses \(+o_\Sigma\).  Introduce
\[
 S_I=\int_\Sigma\left[K^{ab}\wedge Q_{ab}
 +\mu_a\wedge\Delta\epsilon^a+\lambda_{ab}\wedge\Delta\Omega^{ab}\right], \tag{8}
\]
and the four gluing terms
\[
\begin{split}
S_G=\int_\Sigma\{&A_{+a}\wedge(\epsilon_+^a-T_+^E E^a)
 +B_{+ab}\wedge(\Omega_+^{ab}-T_+^\omega\omega^{ab})\\
&+A_{-a}\wedge(\epsilon_-^a-T_-^e e^a)
 +B_{-ab}\wedge(V_-^{ab}-T_-^\varpi\varpi^{ab})\}.                \tag{9}
\end{split}
\]
Thus the promised action is
\[
 \boxed{S_{\rm ext}=S_5+S_4+S_I+S_G,
 \qquad a=u a_0u^{-1}-du\,u^{-1}.}                               \tag{10}
\]
All summands are three-forms on \(\Sigma\).  The signs in (9) are a convention,
but once chosen they fix the solved gluing multipliers below.  No D005 trace
variable remains in addition to the copies.

### 2. Complete interface variation

**Exact relation.** Before imposing any equation, varying (10), covariantly
integrating only the \(g\) and \(u\) terms, and suppressing unchanged bulk Euler
forms gives
\[
\begin{split}
\delta S_{\rm ext}|_\Sigma=\int_\Sigma\{&
 \delta\Omega_+^{ab}\wedge(Q+\lambda+B_+)_{ab}
 +\delta V_-^{ab}\wedge[B_--g^{-1}\!\cdot\lambda]_{ab}\\
&+\delta\epsilon_+^a\wedge[H_a(K)+\mu_a+A_{+a}]
 +\delta\epsilon_-^a\wedge[A_{-a}-g^{-1}\!\cdot\mu]_a\\
&+\delta A_{+a}\wedge(\epsilon_+-T_+^EE)^a
 +\delta B_{+ab}\wedge(\Omega_+-T_+^\omega\omega)^{ab}\\
&+\delta A_{-a}\wedge(\epsilon_--T_-^ee)^a
 +\delta B_{-ab}\wedge(V_--T_-^\varpi\varpi)^{ab}\\
&-\delta(T_+^EE)^a\wedge A_{+a}
 -\delta(T_+^\omega\omega)^{ab}\wedge B_{+ab}\\
&-\delta(T_-^ee)^a\wedge A_{-a}
 -\delta(T_-^\varpi\varpi)^{ab}\wedge(P^-+B_-)_{ab}\\
&+\delta\lambda_{ab}\wedge\Delta\Omega^{ab}
 +\delta\mu_a\wedge\Delta\epsilon^a\\
&-\langle\xi,D_{\bar a}Q+D_{\bar V}\lambda
                    +\mu\diamond\bar\epsilon\rangle
 -\langle\zeta,D_a(g^{-1}\!\cdot Q)\rangle\}\;\\
&+\int_{\partial\Sigma}\left[
 \langle Q+\lambda,\xi\rangle
 +\langle g^{-1}\!\cdot Q,\zeta\rangle\right].                 \tag{11}
\end{split}
\]
In the fourth line, \(P^-\) comes solely from the exterior bulk incidence
term.  There is no analogous core term by D004.  The signs of the last two
lines follow from
\(\int\langle Q,D\xi\rangle=\int_{\partial\Sigma}\langle Q,\xi\rangle
-\int\langle DQ,\xi\rangle\).  The proper endpoint/lateral condition makes the
last integral vanish in the Euler problem, but it is displayed to fix the
polarization.

### 3. Algebraic gluing elimination and comparison with D005

The four multiplier equations are exactly
\[
 \epsilon_+=T_+^EE,\quad\Omega_+=T_+^\omega\omega,
 \quad\epsilon_-=T_-^ee,\quad V_-=T_-^\varpi\varpi.              \tag{12}
\]
By local trace-surjectivity, the independent bulk boundary variations in (11)
give
\[
 A_+=0,\quad B_+=0,
 \qquad A_-=0,
 \qquad B_-=-P^-.                                                \tag{13}
\]
The copy equations then reduce to
\[
 Q+\lambda=0,
 \quad P^-+g^{-1}\!\cdot\lambda=0,
 \quad H(K)+\mu=0,
 \quad g^{-1}\!\cdot\mu=0.                                    \tag{14}
\]
Together with matching, the \(g\) equation, and the fixed-seed \(u\) equation,
these are precisely D005 equation (32) after the replacements
\((e_+,\omega_+,e_-,\varpi)\mapsto
(T_+^EE,T_+^\omega\omega,T_-^ee,T_-^\varpi\varpi)\) and
\(\delta a=-D_a\zeta\).  In particular the algebraic shell is
\[
 \Delta\epsilon=\Delta\Omega=0,
 \quad\lambda=-Q,
 \quad\mu=0,
 \quad Q=g\cdot P^-,
 \quad H(K)=0,                                                    \tag{15}
\]
and the Stueckelberg equation is \(D_a(g^{-1}\!\cdot Q)=0\).

This proves **classical Euler equivalence**, conditional only on the declared
trace maps and lifting property.  It also proves equivalence of the covariant
presymplectic potentials after pullback to (12): the algebraic gluing action has
no differentiated field and hence contributes no endpoint potential; (11)'s
two group endpoint terms become exactly the D005 induced fixed-seed potential.
Thus the induced reduced presymplectic forms agree.  This is not equality of
the unreduced presymplectic spaces: the extended space contains copy and
multiplier null directions.

Nor are the **unreduced primary matrices equal**.  The extended matrix has
separate bulk and copy momentum rows plus multiplier-momentum rows.  Equality
arises only after preserving and eliminating the gluing pairs.  No
distributional bracket between a bulk field and its trace has been used.

### 4. Exact bulk Legendre split

Let \(Y_D\) be a spatial cut and define the densitized spatial Palatini momentum
by the identity
\[
 \int_{Y_D}\delta\omega^{IJ}\wedge P^{(D)}_{IJ}
 =\int d^{D-1}x\,\delta\omega_i^{IJ}\,{\cal P}^{i}_{(D)IJ}(E).    \tag{16}
\]
This definition fixes all factorials and signs relative to the ordered-pair
convention.  The exact bulk canonical coordinates and primary constraints are:

* core: \((E_i^A,\pi^i_{E A})\),
  \((\omega_i^{AB},\pi^i_{\omega AB})\), and temporal
  \((E_t^A,\pi^t_{EA})\), \((\omega_t^{AB},\pi^t_{\omega AB})\), with
  \[
   \pi^i_{EA}\simeq0,\quad
   \pi^i_{\omega AB}-{\cal P}^{i}_{(5)AB}(E)\simeq0,
   \quad\pi^t_{EA}\simeq0,\quad\pi^t_{\omega AB}\simeq0;       \tag{17}
  \]
  there are respectively 20, 40, 5, and 10 component rows per spatial point;
* exterior: \((e_m^a,\pi^m_{ea})\),
  \((\varpi_m^{ab},\pi^m_{\varpi ab})\), and temporal components, with
  \[
   \pi^m_{ea}\simeq0,\quad
   \pi^m_{\varpi ab}-{\cal P}^{m}_{(4)ab}(e)\simeq0,
   \quad\pi^t_{ea}\simeq0,\quad\pi^t_{\varpi ab}\simeq0;       \tag{18}
  \]
  there are 12, 18, 4, and 6 component rows.

The mixed core rows \(AB=4a\) are included in (17).  Their preservation may
diagnose a sourcing inconsistency, but this derivation does not perform it.

 \{\pi_E,\pi_\omega-{\cal P}_{(5)}(E)\}=-d{\cal P}_{(5)},
 \qquad
 \{\pi_e,\pi_\varpi-{\cal P}_{(4)}(e)\}=-d{\cal P}_{(4)},             \tag{19}

with delta functions on the corresponding spatial slice.  Equation (19) is an
ordinary same-dimensional bulk bracket, not a trace bracket.

### 5. Complete interface primary inventory

Decompose a one-form as \(X=X_tdt+X_A dx^A\), and a multiplier two-form as
\(Y=Y_C+dt\wedge Y_{tA}dx^A\).  Every listed component is a canonical
coordinate with its own momentum:

1. Copy coframes: \(\epsilon_{\pm A}^a,\epsilon_{\pm t}^a\), with all
   \(p_{\epsilon_\pm}\simeq0\).  Keep these momenta distinct from
   \(\pi_E,\pi_e\).
2. Copy connections: \(\Omega_{+A}^{ab},\Omega_{+t}^{ab}\) and
   \(V_{-A}^{ab},V_{-t}^{ab}\), with all momenta zero.
3. Matching multipliers: \((\lambda_C^{ab},\lambda_{tA}^{ab})\) and
   \((\mu_C^a,\mu_{tA}^a)\), with
   \(p_{\lambda_C},p_{\lambda_{tA}},p_{\mu_C},p_{\mu_{tA}}\simeq0\).
   The mixed components impose the spatial matching equations upon
   preservation; \(\lambda_C\) enters the group momentum.
4. Gluing multipliers: all spatial and mixed components of
   \(A_+,B_+,A_-,B_-\), with all conjugate momenta zero.  Their preservation,
   not the primary Legendre map, gives (12).
5. Temporal copy components are algebraic multipliers and have zero momenta.
   The spatial copy connection components also have zero momenta; there is no
   interface \(\dot\Omega_+\) or \(\dot V_-\).
6. Group coordinates \(g,u\in SO(1,3)\) have right-trivialized momenta
   \[
    \Phi_g:=p_g-(Q_C+\lambda_C)\simeq0,
    \qquad
    \Phi_u:=p_u-\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}Q_C\simeq0.             \tag{20}
   \]
   There is no momentum for \(a\) or \(a_0\): \(a\) is the composite (2) and
   \(a_0\) is fixed.

For clarity, the locally invertible change \(v=gu\) puts the cut potential in
the exact form
\[
 \Theta_C^{I}=\int_C[\langle\lambda_C,\delta g g^{-1}\rangle
                  +\langle Q_C,\delta v v^{-1}\rangle],          \tag{21}
\]
so an equivalent primary basis is
\(p_g-\lambda_C\simeq0\), \(p_v-Q_C\simeq0\).  In this basis every nonzero
interface primary block is transparent:

* \(p_{\lambda_C}\) brackets nondegenerately with \(p_g-\lambda_C\)
  (rank 12 in the six group plus six multiplier directions);
* \(p_{\epsilon_+A}\) brackets with \(p_v-Q_C\) through \(-dQ_C\);
* group-momentum rows bracket among themselves through the exact
  Maurer--Cartan matrices
  \((A_\lambda)_{IJ}=\langle\lambda_C,[T_I,T_J]\rangle\) and
  \((A_Q)_{IJ}=\langle Q_C,[T_I,T_J]\rangle\).

All other interface primary brackets vanish in this basis.  On the planar
rank-three coframe, \(\operatorname{rank}dQ_C=5\), the second block has rank
10, and the isolated interface matrix has rank \(12+10=22\), reproducing the
C016/D006 result.  This rank statement does not include bulk preservation or
gluing reduction and is not a physical count.

The **complete unreduced primary matrix is therefore well-defined** as the
direct sum of (19), the interface blocks above, and zero multiplier/temporal
rows.  There is no primary cross-block between bulk and copies.  At the next
stage, preservation of gluing introduces \(T_\pm\) into secondary brackets;
constructing those brackets requires a declared functional trace dual or an
equivalent collar regularization.  That later trace-aware Dirac matrix is not
defined by the present primary calculation, and I do not manufacture it using
a boundary delta function.

### 6. Lorentz action and proper outer corners

For small transformations \(h_+,h_-\), the bulk fields transform in their
usual bundles, the copies and traces transform in parallel, and
\[
 g\mapsto h_+gh_-^{-1},\qquad u\mapsto h_-u,
 \qquad a\mapsto h_-ah_-^{-1}-dh_-h_-^{-1}.                      \tag{22}
\]
The matching and core gluing multipliers transform coadjointly under \(h_+\);
the exterior gluing multipliers transform coadjointly under \(h_-\).  Every
difference in (8)--(9) is therefore tensorial.  Parameters vanish at temporal
endpoints and the lateral boundary but may be nonzero at the internal cut.

With fixed \(a_0\), a right transformation is admitted only if
\[
 u\mapsto ur^{-1},\qquad r\cdot a_0=a_0.                          \tag{23}
\]
It leaves \(a\), hence the full action, exactly unchanged.  Infinitesimally
\(D_{a_0}\beta=0\).  In the proper function class \(\beta\) also vanishes on
the outer temporal/lateral boundary, so parallel transport implies
\(\beta=0\) on each connected component.  Thus no nontrivial right quotient or
right corner charge is inferred here.

This is the careful fixed-seed conclusion required by the D013 critique:
\(\delta_\beta a=0\) proves a configuration/action kernel, while
presymplectic nullity follows here only because the polarization is explicitly
the induced pullback (21) with no seed or extra corner term.  A nonzero
contraction of one partial endpoint term would not by itself establish a
charge.  The total left Lorentz moment map combines the bulk and interface
terms; on (15), the persistent core contribution is zero and
\(-P^-+\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}Q=0\) outside, as in C017.  This shell
cancellation is not a proof of first-class closure.

## Dimensional check

Since \([\kappa_D]=L^{D-2}\), each Palatini integrand in (7) is dimensionless.
With \([b]=L^{-2}\), \([Q]=1\) as an integrated two-form and
\([H]=L^{-1}\).  Hence \([\lambda]=[B_\pm]=1\) and
\([\mu]=[A_\pm]=L^{-1}\); every term in (8)--(9) is dimensionless.
Group momenta in (20) are dimensionless.  Coframe canonical momenta have
dimension \(L^{-1}\), while connection momenta are dimensionless, consistent
with (16)--(20).  Both sides of every gluing equation have the same one-form
dimension and bundle frame.

## Limiting-case checks

1. **Switch off gluing multipliers after imposing their equations.** Substitution
   of (12) into (10) gives the D005 intrinsic-trace action exactly; no factor of
   two or duplicate trace functional remains.
2. **Exterior incidence sign.** Equations (13)--(14) give
   \(B_-=-P^-\) and then \(P^-+g^{-1}\lambda=0\).  Reversing the exterior
   incidence sign would instead contradict D005 (32), so this tests the sign.
3. **No polarization, \(b=0\).** Then \(Q=H=0\), and the nondegenerate shell
   cannot satisfy \(Q=g\cdot P^-\) for a rank-three exterior triad.  This agrees
   with D005's need for the polarization term.
4. **Identity frames.** For \(g=u=1\) and \(a_0=a\), (3)--(4) reduce to ordinary
   differences; all dressing signs in (11) reduce to the undressed multiplier
   equations.
5. **Regular collapsed core.** The persistent core Palatini boundary term stays
   zero, while mixed \(4a\) bulk primary rows remain in (17).  Thus the
   calculation neither revives the disputed D010 shared row nor silently drops
   the mixed-sector diagnostic.
