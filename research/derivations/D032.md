# D032: Matrix-operator emergent extra direction

## Question addressed

Map baseline theories for dynamical dimensionality: can a non-prescribed extra-spatial-direction region have autonomous dynamics and a calculable energy or probe response? Compare literal, emergent, and operational meanings without assuming the prior Palatini circle-collapse formulation.

## Branch charter

Slot F. Explore a minimal matrix/operator Hamiltonian where geometry and an extra direction are read from dynamical operator configurations or a probe Laplacian, not a pre-existing fifth coordinate or fixed link chain. Derive one controlled spectral/energy witness, identify how a localized region would be selected without prescribing matrix blocks, and be candid if only global dimension phases are obtained. Address gauge invariance, positivity, finite-N and locality costs. Stop at one model card and smallest decisive local-region test.

## Distinction from sibling branches

The microscopic space is a full matrix algebra, not a manifold, chain, or cell
complex. All matrix entries vary; no nearest-neighbour graph is assigned. A
probe derives its spatial operator from their commutators. This differs from
fixed-site deconstruction even though both have finitely many modes: the latter
specifies sites and link incidence before varying link strengths. This branch
tests whether spectral geometry alone supplies a *region*. No sibling outputs
were consulted; separate GPT sessions are not independent evidence.

## Target claim

**Derived-result target:** a positive classical matrix Hamiltonian admits a
zero-energy noncommutative equilibrium whose probe has a two-dimensional
spectral window, while its collapsed equilibrium has no spatial propagation.
This is one global opening witness, **not** the requested localized addition of
exactly one spatial direction. The mismatch is an explicit triage outcome, not
a claim that a sphere realizes a `3+1/4+1` defect.

## Assumptions

**Assumptions.** There is one continuous external time and three dimensionless
Hermitian `N × N` matrices, with fixed finite `N`. Simultaneous unitary
conjugation is gauge redundancy. Kinetic energy is positive; this is classical
mechanics, not a Lorentz-covariant gravitational theory. Couplings are constant
in time and carry no spatial profile. There is no spatial boundary or prescribed
exterior. Initial matrices and velocities satisfy the Gauss constraint. Time
variations have fixed endpoints. Natural units are `c=hbar=1`.

**Relaxations.** No exact continuum dimension, asymptotically three-dimensional
space, local defect, universal matter coupling, or quantum stability is assumed
or obtained. A second spatial direction rather than a single additional one
emerges in the example; obtaining an exterior and a one-direction contrast is
a separate gate.

## Notation

`X_i`, `i=1,2,3`, are matrices; `A_0` is a Hermitian gauge multiplier and
`D_t X_i=dot X_i-i[A_0,X_i]`. `I>0` has units inverse energy, `E_0>0` has units
energy, and `mu>0` is dimensionless. `Tr` is the unnormalized matrix trace.
`epsilon_123=+1`. `F_ij=i[X_i,X_j]+mu epsilon_ijk X_k` is Hermitian, with the
repeated `k` summed. `P_i=I D_t X_i`. A probe `Phi` is a Hermitian adjoint
matrix; `ell>0` is a fixed length setting its propagation units. `s` is heat
time (length squared). `d_s` below is spatial spectral dimension, never total
spacetime dimension `d`.

## Derivation

### Autonomous dynamics and positivity

**Exact model definition:**

\[
 L={I\over2}\sum_i\operatorname{Tr}(D_tX_i)^2-V,
 \qquad V={E_0\over2}\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Tr}F_{ij}^2,
 \qquad H=\sum_i{\operatorname{Tr}P_i^2\over2I}+V\geq0.
\]

Variation gives `I D_t^2 X_i=-gradient_i V`, with the gradient defined by
`delta V=sum Tr(gradient_i V delta X_i)`, and variation of `A_0` gives
`sum [X_i,P_i]=0`. Thus a polynomial autonomous matrix ODE, not an imposed
geometry trajectory, governs all entries. Temporal gauge `A_0=0` is available
on a time interval; the constraint must still hold. Trace invariance makes the
constraint compatible with evolution, and time independence conserves `H`.

Both `X_i=0` and `X_i=mu J_i` have `F_ij=0`, hence are exact static minima.
The latter uses an irreducible representation only to exhibit a solution; the
Hamiltonian does **not** restrict configurations to that representation or to
block-diagonal matrices. Being a minimum establishes nonnegative potential
Hessian, not attraction or a nucleation mechanism.

For clarity about actual dynamics, the symmetric trajectory `X_i=a(t)J_i`
is an invariant family: rotational covariance leaves the force proportional to
`J_i`. With `C=N j(j+1)`, direct substitution gives the **exact restriction**

\[
 F_{ij}=a(\mu-a)\epsilon_{ijk}J_k,\qquad
 L_{\rm rad}={IC\over2}\dot a^2-{E_0C\over2}a^2(\mu-a)^2,
 \qquad I\ddot a=-E_0a(\mu-a)(\mu-2a).
\]

The sign follows by differentiating the squared potential. Nonzero initial
radial velocity permits global motion; no time-dependent coupling is used.
This special initial-data family does not establish formation from generic
matrices. In particular, a zero-energy collapsed state remains collapsed.

### One spectral opening witness

**Exact probe definition:** in the test-probe limit take

\[
 \Delta_X\Phi=\ell^{-2}\sum_i[X_i,[X_i,\Phi]],\qquad
 H_\Phi={1\over2}\operatorname{Tr}\bigl(\Pi^2+\Phi\Delta_X\Phi\bigr).
\]

Here the probe kinetic normalization assigns `Phi` units energy to the power
`-1/2` and `Pi=dot Phi` units energy to the power `1/2`. Cyclicity gives
`Tr(Phi Delta_X Phi)=ell^{-2}sum Tr([X_i,Phi]^dagger[X_i,Phi])>=0`.
Backreaction is neglected by sending probe amplitude to zero.

On `X_i=a J_i`, adjoint generators `L_i Phi=[J_i,Phi]` obey the same angular
momentum algebra. The matrix space is `j tensor j*`, which decomposes into
one spin-`l` multiplet for every integer `l=0,...,2j`. Its dimension check is
`sum_(l=0)^(N-1)(2l+1)=N^2`. The adjoint Casimir therefore yields

\[
 \boxed{\omega_l^2=\lambda_l={a^2\over\ell^2}l(l+1),
 \qquad g_l=2l+1,\quad 0\leq l\leq N-1.}
\]

This is an exact instantaneous spectrum; a fixed frequency interpretation
requires a static background, notably `a=mu`. For `a=0` every mode has zero
spatial stiffness. That is collapsed propagation with `N^2` surviving probe
variables, **not** deletion of microscopic degrees of freedom.

Set `u=s a^2/ell^2`. The heat trace is exactly
`K(s)=sum_(l=0)^(N-1)(2l+1)exp[-u l(l+1)]`. For
`N^-2 << u << 1`, replacing the sum by its continuum leading term gives

\[
 K(s)\simeq\int_0^\infty(2l+1)e^{-ul(l+1)}dl={1\over u},
 \qquad d_s=-2{d\log K\over d\log s}\simeq2.
\]

The substitution `z=l(l+1)` fixes the normalization exactly in the integral.
This is the single opening witness: a finite-matrix state supports a resolved
two-spatial-dimensional probe spectrum. The effective spectral radius is
`ell/|a|`; `X_i` are dimensionless operator generators, not physical embedding
coordinates whose Euclidean radius should be identified with that radius.

### Why this is not yet a local region

Conjugation sends `Delta_X` to a unitarily equivalent operator, so the spectrum
and heat trace are gauge invariant. A chosen set of matrix indices is not a
region. Nor does a reducible solution `mu J_i` with several representation
blocks automatically describe a bubble: each block is an algebraic component,
and probes include off-diagonal interblock variables. There is no derived
three-space exterior or interface metric here.

**Conjectured next diagnostic:** use a relational Hermitian observable
`Q(X)` and its spectral projector `P_B=1_B(Q)` to define a candidate patch.
Both must transform by conjugation. A windowed heat trace using
`Phi -> P_B Phi P_B` is then gauge invariant, but neither locality nor the
choice of relational observable follows merely from invariance. A patch must
also exhibit short-range propagation and remain coupled to its exterior.
No such dynamically selected patch is constructed. Only global equilibria and
a global invariant trajectory are obtained; localized formation, motion, and
stability are all unproved.

## Approximations

The finite-`N` spectrum, positivity, and radial restriction are exact. The heat
trace approximation is a large-`N`, intermediate-resolution asymptotic, not
coupling perturbation theory. Infinite-sum corrections are relative `O(u)`;
the cutoff tail is exponentially suppressed when `u N^2 >> 1` (with polynomial
prefactors). The displayed dimension is leading order in that joint window.
The vanishing-probe-amplitude limit drops quadratic probe backreaction on `X`.

## Validity regime

Finite `N>1`, positive `I,E_0,ell,mu`; a broad spectral window needs `N>>1`.
The frequency witness applies to static solutions. No adiabatic theorem for a
time-varying spectrum is asserted. There are no physical spatial coordinates or
boundary conditions to smuggle in an extra axis. Three operator labels and the
epsilon coupling bias the allowed global geometry; they do not explain why
one and only one extra direction would appear locally.

## Dimensional check

`D_t X` and `A_0` have energy dimension one; `P` is dimensionless. Both terms
of `H` have energy dimension one. The radial equation has energy dimension one
on each side. `Delta`, `lambda`, and `omega^2` have dimension energy squared;
`u`, `K`, and `d_s` are dimensionless. Positive sums of Hermitian squares fix
the potential sign; the double commutator, not its negative, is positive with
the stated trace inner product.

## Limiting-case checks

At `N=1` only `l=0` remains: no spectral window. At fixed finite `N`, `s->0`
gives `K->N^2` and `d_s->0`; at `s->infinity` for an irreducible nonzero state,
`K->1` and again `d_s->0`. Thus dimension two is neither exact ultraviolet
dimension nor an open-stratum theorem. At `a=0`, `K=N^2` at all resolutions;
taking this limit inside the intermediate window is invalid. Gauge
conjugation and global operator rotations preserve energy and spectrum. No
spatial translation conservation or relativistic dispersion is established.

## Unresolved concerns

The main blocker is **region selection**, not a spectral normalization issue.
Representation minima are degenerate and the demonstrated conservative system
has no dissipative attraction. Gauge-invariant global spectra do not determine
local geometry. Fixed matrix size retains hidden degrees of freedom at collapse.
The spectrum opens two spatial dimensions, not the target single direction.

**Staged roadmap:** first audit the global witness and interblock interpretation;
next require a connected emergent base with relational patches; only then ask
for a localized dimension contrast and its energy. The smallest decisive
local-region assertion is: unrestricted matrix dynamics supports a connected
configuration whose relational local heat traces differ by one in spatial
spectral dimension over the same resolution window, with nonzero controlled
probe communication across the boundary. No prescribed block decomposition
may serve as that assertion's evidence. The Hessian diagnostic is a cheap
pruning step, not a substitute for it. Stop here rather than repairing the
potential to manufacture a patch.
