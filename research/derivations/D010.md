# D010: Linearized total Lorentz Dirac chain and the inseparable bulk-flux blocker

## Question addressed

Within the fixed-interface planar basic persistent `SO(1,3)` sector of
`D005`/`D006`, what is the minimum complete bulk-plus-interface Lorentz
generator and boundary polarization needed to decide whether C016's
Stueckelberg rank-ten block is first-class gauge, physical edge structure, or
a second-class remnant?

## Branch charter

Linearize the combined D005 bulk-plus-interface Lorentz sector about the planar
branch and build the bounded Dirac--Bergmann chain needed to classify only the
edge/reference variables: primary defining constraints, Lorentz Gauss
secondaries, relevant brackets, and local degree count. Freeze all non-Lorentz
gravitational constraints unless consistency forces them in. Stop and document
any unavoidable coupling that defeats this truncation.

## Distinction from sibling branches

This branch uses the unreduced first-order canonical variables and follows the
Dirac--Bergmann preservation chain far enough to test whether an
interface-only reduction is a consistent subproblem. Its distinctive attack is
the mixed primary bracket between the shared coframe momentum, the bulk
Palatini momentum-defining constraint, and the Stueckelberg momentum-defining
constraint. It does not infer a classification from covariance or from the cut
presymplectic rank alone. No sibling-wave artifact was inspected.

## Primary constraints

### Ordinary first-order defining constraints

Before eliminating any Legendre relations, the bulk primaries are exactly

\[
 \mathcal C_\omega^+
 :=\pi_\omega^+-\mathcal P_+(E)\simeq0,
 \quad \mathcal C_e^+:=\pi_e^+\simeq0,
 \qquad

 :=\pi_\varpi^--\mathcal P_-(e_-)\simeq0,
 \quad \mathcal C_e^-:=\pi_e^-\simeq0.                \tag{9}
\]

The temporal connection momenta also vanish,
`pi_{omega_t}^+\simeq pi_{varpi_t}^-\simeq0`; their preservation produces the
Lorentz Gauss secondaries below. The zero momenta of lapse-like coframe
components belong to the frozen non-Lorentz gravitational chain.

From (7), the interface defining primaries are

\[
 p_\lambda\simeq0,
 \quad \Phi_g:=p_g-\lambda_C\simeq0,
 \quad \Phi_v:=p_v-Q_C(e_+)\simeq0,
 \quad p_{e_+,C}\simeq0,                              \tag{10}
\]

together with zero momenta for `mu`, spatial connection traces, and temporal
multiplier components that have no interface velocity. In the equivalent
`(g,u)` chart these are D006's
`p_g-Pi_C\simeq0` and
`p_u-Ad_{g^{-1}}Q_C\simeq0`.

The pair `(p_lambda,Phi_g)` is a nondegenerate rank-12 canonical defining
block. After it is eliminated, `(p_{e_+,C},Phi_v)` has rank ten because
`rank(dQ_C)=5`. **This rank-ten primary block is a first-order defining block;
it is not, by itself, a physical second-class obstruction.**

### The defining blocks do not factor between bulk and edge

The relevant exact canonical brackets, written as differentials of the two
Legendre maps, are

\[
 \{p_{e_+},\mathcal C_\omega^+\}
 =-d\mathcal P_+(\bar E),
 \qquad
 \{p_{e_+,C},\Phi_v\}=dQ_C(\bar e_+),                 \tag{11}
\]

up to the common sign convention for canonical brackets. Both entries share
the *same* `p_{e_+}` row. Therefore the rank-ten interface matrix cannot be
Dirac-eliminated independently of the bulk Palatini defining matrix. Replacing
`pi_omega` by an independent BF momentum would remove the first bracket, but
would change the unmodified D005 bulk theory and is forbidden by the frozen
objective.

Equation (11) is the first exact obstruction to an autonomous edge/reference
constraint count.

## Secondary Lorentz Gauss constraints from both bulks and interface

Integrating (13) by parts gives the bulk secondary densities

\[

The same integration exposes the boundary moment maps

\[

The minus bulk-flux signs follow directly from

Let `chi=delta g g^{-1}`. Linearizing the exterior boundary constraint gives

Equation (18) is the second exact obstruction: every allowed edge momentum

\[

 \{G_-[\alpha],G_-[\beta]\}=G_-[[\alpha,\beta]],

## Local count with assumptions

After eliminating only `(p_lambda,Phi_g)`, the isolated Stueckelberg cut block

There are then two conditional statements:

- **Proper boundary gauge (`alpha|_C=0`).** No Lorentz boundary gauge parameter
  removes these pairs. They remain in the chosen boundary phase space. This is
  a boundary-condition result, not proof that they are unavoidable physical
  degrees in every polarization.
- **Extended boundary gauge.** The six Lorentz parameters act jointly on the

  `N_phys=(dim phase space-2F-S)/2` cannot be applied to the edge block alone:
  neither `F` nor `S` factorizes because of (11) and (18). The total count needs
  the bulk Palatini primary rank and its preserved torsion/coframe constraints.

Thus the requested Lorentz-only local physical count is **undecidable**, not
zero and not five.

## Test of whether five pairs are removed, remain, or are undecidable

1. They are **not shown removed** merely because (17) makes the total boundary
   moment map vanish. A zero moment map is a constraint surface, not the
   quotient, and its tangent equation (18) contains bulk variables.
2. They **remain as five pairs** if all Lorentz parameters vanish at `C`, but
   that conclusion is imposed by a fixed-boundary polarization.
3. They are **undecidable in the requested extended-gauge, Lorentz-only
   truncation**, because the same coframe momentum enters both bulk and edge
   defining constraints (11), while total Gauss matching ties the edge momentum
   to the exterior Palatini flux (18).

No desired constraint has been imposed by hand, and no primary defining block
has been relabeled a physical obstruction.
