# D024: Minimal SO(1,4)/SO(1,3) reduction-field bridge

## Question addressed

Can the external full `SO(1,4)` reference connection in D023 be replaced by
the connection induced algebraically by one spacelike unit reduction field
`V`, while retaining the exact finite-slope flat benchmark, a freely selected
`c>0`, a rank-three interface triad, and the frozen persistent D005 shell?

The bounded answer below is negative. The replacement is well-defined and
unique after one precise minimality condition is stated. It cancels exactly the
coset connection rows, including `43`, and its constrained `V` equation is
compatible with the polar data. Its compulsory coframe equation, however,
sets the polar coset connection to zero and hence gives `c=0`. Thus there is no
positive-slope stationary datum left to classify. Off shell, the `43` tangent
is not a pure gauge tangent: it changes a local covariant norm and generically
the circle-holonomy conjugacy class. A final gauge/superselection/edge
classification would in any event require a total cut presymplectic form,
mixed moment map, admitted boundary gauge parameters, and observable algebra
not supplied by frozen D005.

All results are exact at classical order. No perturbative expansion is used.

## Derivation

### 1. Exact projectors and unique minimal induced connection

From `V^2=1`,

\[
 h^2=h,\qquad h^A{}_BV^B=0,\qquad V_AK^A={1\over2}D(V^2)=0.   \tag{7}
\]

Require an induced connection to satisfy both

\[
 D_{\Omega_V}V=0,
 \qquad h^A{}_Ch^D{}_B\Omega_V^{CB}
       =h^A{}_Ch^D{}_B\omega^{CB}.                            \tag{8}
\]

The second condition is the required minimality condition: retain exactly the
stabilizer block of `omega`, adding no independent stabilizer one-form. Solving
(8) gives

\[
 \boxed{\Omega_V^{AB}=\omega^{AB}+V^AK^B-K^AV^B,\qquad
 C^{AB}=K^AV^B-V^AK^B=2K^{[A}V^{B]}.}                         \tag{9}
\]

Indeed, `(Omega_V-omega)^A{}_B V^B=-K^A`, proving the first equation in
(8), while double projection kills the added term. Conversely, the difference
of two solutions of (8) has zero contraction with `V` and zero double
projection, hence is zero. Without the second condition, “the unique induced
connection” would be false: an arbitrary `so(1,3)_V` one-form could be added.

For the gauge convention of D005,

\[
 \omega' =g\omega g^{-1}-dg\,g^{-1},\qquad V'=gV,             \tag{10}
\]

one obtains exactly

\[
 h'=ghg^{-1},\quad K'=gK,\quad
 \Omega'_V=g\Omega_Vg^{-1}-dg\,g^{-1},\quad C'=gCg^{-1}.     \tag{11}
\]

Thus (2) is `SO(1,4)` invariant. In adapted gauge `V=e_4`,

\[
 K^a=\omega^{a4},\quad K^4=0,
 \quad\Omega_V^{ab}=\omega^{ab},\quad\Omega_V^{a4}=0,
 \quad C^{a4}=\omega^{a4}.                                   \tag{12}
\]

This proves that `Omega_V` is the persistent Lorentz block, not an external
reference. `V` is an internal de Sitter reduction field. It is not the FGP
unit normal obtained from a spacetime foliation and inverse tetrad, and it is
not held fixed under full `SO(1,4)` gauge transformations.

### 3. Complete independent variation of the mixed term

Integrating only the `D delta V` term by parts yields

\[

where

\[

`D(delta V^A V^B P_AB)=D delta V^A wedge V^B P_AB

### 8. Precise classification test and proof of insufficiency

\[

\]

with the boundary conditions on `alpha` fixed. A fixed holonomy/flux conjugacy

frozen D005 cannot complete (39) for a mixed `a4` parameter:

4. the D004 core bulk cut symplectic contribution in the finite one-sided

Therefore neither “gauge”, “superselected”, nor “physical edge pair” may be
