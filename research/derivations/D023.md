## Derivation

### 1. Exact rank-three reduction of `P^(5)`

On `Y`, only `e^0,e^1,e^2` are nonzero. Therefore a nonzero triple coframe in
`P^(5)_{AB}` must use exactly `012`, and the only nonzero ordered momentum
components are `P_43` and `P_34`. Since `epsilon_43012=-1`, every permutation
of `012` gives the same negative oriented form:

\[
 \epsilon_{43CDE}e^C\wedge e^D\wedge e^E=-6o_\Sigma.          \tag{8}
\]

Hence, exactly,

\[
 P^{(5)}_{43}=-{1\over2\kappa_5}o_\Sigma,
 \qquad P^{(5)}_{34}=+{1\over2\kappa_5}o_\Sigma,              \tag{9}
\]

with every other component zero on the displayed rank-three trace.

Now use `K34=-K43=-k dtheta`. Because moving `dtheta` through the three
coframes gives `dtheta wedge oSigma=-oSigma wedge dtheta`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
 K^{43}\wedge P_{43}^{(5)}
   &=+{k\over2\kappa_5}o_\Sigma\wedge d\theta,\\
 K^{34}\wedge P_{34}^{(5)}
   &=+{k\over2\kappa_5}o_\Sigma\wedge d\theta.
\end{aligned}                                                   \tag{10}
\]

The two ordered pair values add. Ordinary circle integration therefore gives

\[
 \boxed{B_{\rm ref}\big|_{\rm polar}
 =-{2\pi\over\kappa_5}\int_\Sigma k\,o_\Sigma.}              \tag{11}
\]

This independently recovers the required coefficient rather than inserting
the D022 answer. In particular,

\[
 \left.\delta B_{\rm ref}\right|_{\delta\chi^{43}}
 =-{2\pi\over\kappa_5}\int_\Sigma
   \delta\chi^{43}o_\Sigma,                                   \tag{12}
\]

which has exactly the opposite sign to the D022 core boundary row.

### 2. Complete field-space variation

The exact momentum variation follows directly from (6):

\[
 \delta P^{(5)}_{AB}={1\over4\kappa_5}\epsilon_{ABCDE}
 \delta E^C\wedge E^D\wedge E^E.                              \tag{13}
\]

Varying (1), without imposing any reference treatment, gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \delta B_{\rm ref}
 =-\int_Y\big[&(\delta\omega_+^{AB}-\delta\bar\Omega^{AB})
                  \wedge P^{(5)}_{AB}
              +K^{AB}\wedge\delta P^{(5)}_{AB}\big]\\
 =\int_Y\big[&-\delta\omega_+^{AB}\wedge P^{(5)}_{AB}
              +\delta\bar\Omega^{AB}\wedge P^{(5)}_{AB}\\
 &+\delta E^C\wedge J_C(K,E)\big],                            \tag{14}
\end{aligned}
\]

where moving `delta E` past the one-form `K` fixes the plus sign and

\[
 \boxed{J_C(K,E):={1\over4\kappa_5}\epsilon_{ABCDE}
             K^{AB}\wedge E^D\wedge E^E.}                    \tag{15}
\]

Equations (13)--(15) are exact. They exhibit every direct variation of the
single primitive: connection, coframe, and reference. There is no derivative
of `E` in (1), hence no coframe corner term. Reference-orbit corner terms arise
only after (4) is substituted and integrated by parts below.

Adding the D004 core boundary potential `+int_Y delta omega P` cancels the
first term in (14) identically for **all ten** `SO(1,4)` connection components:

\[
 \boxed{\left.\delta(S_++B_{\rm ref})\right|_Y
 =\int_Y\left[
   \delta\bar\Omega^{AB}\wedge P^{(5)}_{AB}
   +\delta E^C\wedge J_C(K,E)\right].}                         \tag{16}
\]

The complete persistent D005/exterior variation is unchanged and vanishes on
its already displayed shell. It neither changes nor cancels either term in
(16).

### 3. Cancellation is a polarization change, not mixed momentum matching

Equation (16) has no `delta omega_+` boundary coefficient. Thus (1) does not
balance the C025 row against a new dynamical mixed momentum. It performs the
standard covariant Legendre transformation

\[
 \delta\omega\wedge P\quad\longmapsto\quad
 -K\wedge\delta P+\delta\bar\Omega\wedge P.                   \tag{17}
\]

The old free-connection Euler row is removed, while coframe and reference rows
replace it. Consequently, cancellation (12) by itself supplies no transition
equation for `chi43`, no equation selecting `c`, and no proof that a formerly
free polar datum is controlled.

On the polar sector, combining the bulk row with the complete variation of
(11) gives the particularly transparent exact formula

\[
 \boxed{\left.\delta(S_++B_{\rm ref})\right|_{43}
 ={2\pi\over\kappa_5}\int_\Sigma
 \left[\delta\bar\chi^{43}\,o_\Sigma-k\,\delta o_\Sigma\right].}
                                                                    \tag{18}
\]

The `delta chi43` terms have canceled. Since rank-preserving triad variations
include local rescalings `delta e^i=alpha e^i`, for which
`delta oSigma=3 alpha oSigma`, the free-coframe equation from (18) requires

\[
 \boxed{k=\chi^{43}-\bar\chi^{43}=0}                            \tag{19}
\]

on the rank-three polar ansatz. This is the reduced content of the tensorial
coframe equation `J_C=0` relevant here. It is not asserted that `J_C=0` forces
the entire full connection difference `K` to vanish in every sector.

After the bulk torsion equation is imposed, `chi43=c`, so (19) becomes

\[

\]

Thus the companion coframe equation anchors the polar slope to the reference;

### 6. Gauge-orbit reference and its compulsory edge/corner terms

Substitute (4) into the reference term of (16). Covariant integration by parts

\[
\begin{aligned}

 &=-\int_Y D_{\bar\Omega}\zeta^{AB}\wedge P_{AB}^{(5)}\\
 &=\int_Y\zeta^{AB}D_{\bar\Omega}P_{AB}^{(5)}
   -\int_{\partial Y}\zeta^{AB}P_{AB}^{(5)}.                  \tag{22}
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore the orbit treatment replaces (21) by

\[
 \boxed{D_{\bar\Omega}P^{(5)}=0,\qquad
 \Theta_h^{\rm corner}=-\int_{\partial Y}\zeta^{AB}P^{(5)}_{AB}.}
                                                                    \tag{23}
\]

The edge equation and corner potential are compulsory; dropping either would
not be the variation of (1). Proper `h` variations at all temporal/lateral

makes the `43` corner moment map nonzero on the desired rank-three branch.

On a matching polar background `barOmega=omega_+=c T_43 dtheta`, equation

\[
 D_{\bar\Omega}P^{(5)}=0                                      \tag{24}
\]

because `P` is constant and its sole normal bivector commutes with `T_43`.
Thus the orbit equation does not itself degenerate the triad. The obstruction

\[
 H_0={\cal P}\exp\oint\Omega_0,
 \qquad H_{\bar\Omega}=h(0)H_0h(0)^{-1}.                       \tag{25}
\]

For the polar connection this class is that of
`exp(2pi c T43)`. Continuous variation of `c` changes this class generically

rank-degenerating equation (21). Hence the gauge-orbit treatment has the exact

\[
 \boxed{\begin{array}{ll}
 \text{fixed seed orbit:}&\text{nondegenerate, but polar holonomy/amplitude is
 fixed up to conjugacy;}\\
 \text{freely varied seed:}&P^{(5)}=0\text{ and rank}\le2.
 \end{array}}                                                   \tag{26}

For integer `c`, in particular the polar value `c=1`, a single-valued winding

Finally, even with proper corners, the new full `SO(1,4)` field `h`, its

### 7. Full `SO(1,4)` covariance and the reduction stopping boundary

As a standalone boundary functional, (1) is exactly `SO(1,4)` invariant:

\[

 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad B_{\rm ref}'=B_{\rm ref}.          \tag{27}

This test succeeds only because `barOmega` is a full connection and (1) sums
all ten ordered antisymmetric components. A purported primitive containing
only the scalar `43` coefficient would require two independently specified

The full variation (16), likewise, cancels all connection rows, not only `43`.

Therefore:
