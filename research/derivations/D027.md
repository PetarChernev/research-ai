# D027: Intrinsic mixed-dimensional free-boundary route

## Question addressed

Map baseline theories for dynamical dimensionality: can a non-prescribed extra-spatial-direction region have autonomous dynamics and a calculable energy or probe response? Compare literal, emergent, and operational meanings without assuming the prior Palatini circle-collapse formulation.

## Branch charter

Do not continue D025 counterterms or D026 collar descent. ## Distinction from sibling branches

The varied object is the domain carrying a circle, not a radion on a permanent
bulk, a matter trap, a link matrix, or a propagation coefficient. A topological
incidence rule removes exterior circle points altogether. The price is that
stratumwise measures do not supply a compatible metric or matter calculus at
the incidence locus. The witness below is therefore an **effective
mixed-dimensional free-boundary toy**, not an intrinsic gravitational theory.
Only the frozen branch matrix was consulted; sibling GPT sessions supply no
independent evidence.

## Target claim

**Target:** obtain autonomous motion and conserved excess energy for an initially
present spherical extra-fiber region from a moving-domain action. **Derived
witness:** equations (3)–(5), exact within the declared spherical toy. No claim
of nucleation, stable particles, or predictable matter transfer is targeted.

## Assumptions

1. **Assumption:** a fixed Minkowski bookkeeping base has signature
   `(-,+,+,+)` and one persistent time; gravity is absent. Spatial domains
   \(\Omega_t\subset\mathbb R^3\) have smooth, timelike moving boundaries.
2. **Assumption:** each interior base point carries a spacelike circle of fixed
   proper radius \(a>0\); exterior points carry none. Radius stabilization is
   imposed, not derived. Only domain embeddings are varied.
3. **Assumption:** bulk phases contain constant vacuum energy densities
   \(\rho_5\) and \(\rho_4\), and their junction has positive tension
   \(\sigma\). There are no propagating bulk fields in this witness. Its
   missing information-transfer law is not hidden in a matching condition.
4. Variations vanish at initial/final times and at spatial infinity. For the
   reduced problem specify \(R(t_0)>0\), \(|\dot R(t_0)|<1\). Evolution ends
   before collapse, loss of smoothness, or a null wall; continuation through
   these events is not assumed. Energies subtract the homogeneous exterior
   vacuum on the same base volume.

## Notation

\(W=\{(t,\mathbf x):\mathbf x\in\Omega_t\}\) is the interior base world
tube, \(B\) the base, and \(\Gamma=\partial W\) its timelike wall.
\(\theta\sim\theta+2\pi\) is dimensionless. \(h\) is the metric induced
on \(\Gamma\) from the base. The interior product metric is
\(ds_5^2=ds_B^2+a^2d\theta^2\), only on its regular open stratum.
Define \(\Delta\epsilon=2\pi a\rho_5-\rho_4\), the interior-minus-exterior
energy per base three-volume. For spheres, \(R\) is radius,
\(v=\dot R\), and \(\gamma=(1-v^2)^{-1/2}\). Natural units
\(c=\hbar=1\) are used. Total spacetime dimension is \(d\); spatial
dimensions are respectively three and four, not spectral dimensions.

## Derivation

### 1. Ontology and the first missing bridge

**Definition of a candidate topological object:** at fixed time take the
disjoint union of \(\overline\Omega_t\times S^1\) and
\(\mathbb R^3\setminus\Omega_t\), identifying every
\((\mathbf y,\theta)\) with the single exterior boundary point
\(\mathbf y\in\partial\Omega_t\). Its moving version uses \(W\).
The resulting regular open spacetime strata have topological dimensions
\(d=5\) and \(d=4\). The wall is a lower-dimensional incidence locus,
not an ordinary common hypersurface: the interior boundary before quotient
has dimension four, while the exterior wall has dimension three. No physical
exterior fifth-coordinate points occur in this construction. The base is a
fixed projection/scaffold, not an additional copy of interior physical matter.

**Conjectural bridge, already incomplete:** this topological quotient is not
a compatible Lorentzian differential space just because metrics exist on
its open strata. In particular, the circumference remains \(2\pi a\)
right up to a locus where all its points are identified. A smooth finite-radius
metric does not descend there. If spatial distance is instead defined by
infima of path lengths allowing travel through the collapsed locus, two
near-wall fiber points can connect with cost at most twice their normal
distance to it. Thus global fiber distances cease to be those of a uniform
product circle near the wall. Neither that path metric nor a differential
calculus for it is used below. Replacing this junction by a smooth shrinking
radius would be a different model and requires a separate regularity analysis.

### 2. Explicit stratumwise moving-domain action

**Assumed effective action:** prescribe the open-stratum product measures and
a base-induced wall measure independently:

\[
 S=-\rho_4\int_{B\setminus W}d^4x
   -\rho_5\int_{W\times S^1}a\,d\theta\,d^4x
   -\sigma\int_\Gamma\sqrt{-\det h}\,d^3\xi.                 \tag{1}
\]

This is an actual functional of the embedding of \(W\), not a prescribed
dimension profile. Integration uses the projection and stratum data; it is
not claimed to follow from one smooth quotient volume form. The infinite
exterior action is regulated by a fixed large base box and subtracted before
variation. **Exact reduction within (1):** circle integration and vacuum
subtraction give

\[
 S_{\rm exc}=-\Delta\epsilon\int_Wd^4x
             -\sigma\int_\Gamma dA_{\rm proper}.             \tag{2}
\]

The \(2\pi a\) normalization matters: equating densities in different
dimensions directly would be dimensionally wrong. This reduction also shows
the witness's explanatory limit: its domain dynamics is indistinguishable
from an ordinary two-phase thin wall with the same \(\Delta\epsilon\).
It does not operationally detect a fifth direction.

### 3. One witness: spherical energy and autonomous shape motion

For \(\Omega_t=\{r<R(t)\}\), the induced wall volume is
\(4\pi R^2\sqrt{1-v^2}\,dt\). **Exact spherical restriction of (2):**

\[
 L=-4\pi\sigma R^2\sqrt{1-v^2}
    -\frac{4\pi}{3}\Delta\epsilon R^3,\qquad
 p=4\pi\sigma R^2\gamma v.                                \tag{3}
\]

The radial Euler–Lagrange equation is
\[
 \frac{d}{dt}(R^2\gamma v)
 =-2R/\gamma-(\Delta\epsilon/\sigma)R^2.
\]
Using \(d(\gamma v)/dt=\gamma^3\ddot R\) and
\(\gamma v^2+\gamma^{-1}=\gamma\), this becomes

\[
 \boxed{\ddot R=-\frac{2(1-v^2)}R
                  -\frac{\Delta\epsilon}{\sigma}(1-v^2)^{3/2}.} \tag{4}
\]

Thus initial data, not an inserted trajectory, select the region's subsequent
motion. The reduced kinetic Hessian is
\(\partial^2 L/\partial v^2=4\pi\sigma R^2\gamma^3>0\).
Its Legendre energy is

\[
 \boxed{E=4\pi\sigma R^2\gamma
              +\frac{4\pi}{3}\Delta\epsilon R^3,\qquad\dot E=0.} \tag{5}
\]

Indeed differentiation gives
\(\dot E=4\pi\sigma v[2R\gamma+R^2\gamma^3\ddot R+
(\Delta\epsilon/\sigma)R^2]\), whose bracket vanishes by (4),
including at turning points without division by \(v\). Bulk energy gained
by expanding the domain is balanced by wall energy lost, and conversely.
The smooth radial ODE is locally predictive for \(R>0, |v|<1\); this does
not establish a full mixed-dimensional initial-value theorem.

## Approximations

Equations (1)–(5) are exact in the postulated sharp-wall, fixed-radius,
vacuum-only sector; there is no perturbative truncation inside that sector.
As an effective description they omit wall thickness, bending elasticity,
radion modes, bulk excitations, and gravitational backreaction. No error order
for those omissions has been derived. Spherical symmetry is a restricted
sector, not a controlled estimate for nonspherical solutions. The instantaneous
rest check below uses \(v=0\) exactly, not a nonrelativistic extrapolation.

## Validity regime

The model requires smooth timelike walls and positive \(a,\sigma\).
It permits either sign of \(\Delta\epsilon\). A putative microscopic
wall thickness \(\ell\) would require \(R\gg\ell\) and curvature and
acceleration scales well below \(\ell^{-1}\), but no such completion is
supplied. Equation (1) respects base Poincaré transformations and wall
reparametrizations; (3) fixes base inertial time and a center. This is not
general covariance of the singular physical object. No gauge or gravitational
constraint sector has been defined.

## Dimensional check

With length unit \(L\), \([a]=[R]=[t]=L\),
\([\rho_5]=L^{-5}\), \([\rho_4]=[\Delta\epsilon]=L^{-4}\),
and \([\sigma]=L^{-3}\). All terms in (1) are dimensionless;
\([L_{\rm radial}]=[E]=[p]=L^{-1}\), since \(v\) is dimensionless.
Both terms in (4) have dimension \(L^{-1}=[\ddot R]\).
The wall area factor is \(4\pi R^2\), not the area of a four-spatial-
dimensional sphere: the wall measure is deliberately base-induced.

## Limiting-case checks

At rest, \(\ddot R=-2/R-\Delta\epsilon/\sigma\): positive interior
excess density and tension both drive contraction. For degenerate phases,
\(E=4\pi\sigma R^2\gamma\); from rest at \(R_0\), conservation
implies \(1-v^2=(R/R_0)^4\), consistent with inward acceleration.
For \(\Delta\epsilon<0\), a static critical radius
\(R_*=-2\sigma/\Delta\epsilon\) exists, but
\(E_{\rm rest}''(R_*)=-8\pi\sigma<0\): it is not stable even radially.
Negative volume energy also makes unrestricted growth energetically favorable;
positive kinetic energy is not global vacuum stability. These checks concern
the same energy witness, not a particle solution. The \(R\to0\) endpoint
is outside the evolution claim, so finite energy there proves no nucleation
or annihilation law. Taking \(a\to0\) in coefficients does not prove
convergence of the physical state space or of its matter spectrum.

## Unresolved concerns

**Staged applicability map:**

* **Available now:** a finite-energy spherical domain for finite \(R,v\),
  autonomous motion, and positive radial inertia. Exact open-stratum dimensions
  and absent exterior circle points hold for the stated topology only.
* **First blocker:** specify compatible functions, traces, derivatives,
  measures, and causal/energy flux at the unequal-dimensional incidence. The
  independently assigned wall measure is extra physical input, not derived
  geometry. The first mapping failure is already metric/calculus descent,
  before Einstein dynamics or quantization.
* **Next applicability gate:** a probe with circle dependence needs a trace
  rule. Continuity on the quotient would force its boundary value to be
  independent of \(\theta\); this does not itself tell us what happens to
  nonzero circle modes, their energy, or incoming exterior data at a moving
  wall. Reflection, storage, or conversion require an action and a domain.
* **Deferred:** formation from empty \(W\), collapse continuation,
  nonspherical stability, topology change, universal probes, gravity, and
  quantum theory. A prescribed topology sector and initial bubble solve none
  of these. Exact intrinsic geometry is substantially more costly than the
  current effective energy calculation.

**Smallest useful next assertion:** on one prescribed smooth subluminal wall
segment of this quotient, does a finite-energy scalar variational domain
with single-valued quotient traces give unique linear evolution and a closed
wall-plus-field energy balance without adding hidden exterior fiber data?
This is a bounded incidence test, not a demand to complete gravity. Failure
would sharply limit the toy's upgrade; success would establish only a probe
calculus for that chosen junction, not nucleation or universal coupling. Stop
here rather than choosing successive repairs.
