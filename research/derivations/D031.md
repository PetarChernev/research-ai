# D031: Variable-incidence cell-complex dynamics

## Question addressed

Map baseline theories for dynamical dimensionality: can a non-prescribed extra-spatial-direction region have autonomous dynamics and a calculable energy or probe response? Compare literal, emergent, and operational meanings without assuming the prior Palatini circle-collapse formulation.

## Branch charter

Slot E. Explore an ontology in which a spatial cell/simplicial complex itself can acquire/remove higher-dimensional cells via autonomous local transitions. This must differ from fixed links on a background chain: dimension follows incidence/local neighborhoods, not a label. Specify one finite legal local move, reversible or stochastic/quantum dynamics with energy accounting, and a probe/dimension diagnostic, using a low-dimensional analogue if needed. State causality, continuum, energy, and locality limitations; no unsupported GR/path-integral claims. Stop at one concrete move model and roadmap.

## Distinction from sibling branches

The physical state is the geometric realization of a simplicial complex, not
a field of dimension labels. A move creates an entire open triangle interior
and its incidence with existing edges. Its absence means those spatial points
do not exist. The persistent graph is a genuine limitation, but its vertex
adjacency does not encode the newly created space: all edge weights remain
unchanged. This differs materially from deconstruction on a fixed chain and
from changing a kinetic tensor on a fixed continuum. No sibling outputs were
read; parallel GPT work is not independent evidence.

## Target claim

**Target, derived below:** a finite intrinsic spatial complex admits an
autonomous, local, thermally reversible triangle-attachment process that
changes spatial local dimension from one to two, with an exactly calculable
energy change and formation probability. This is a combinatorial analogue,
not a relativistic quantum theory or a stable dimensional particle.

## Assumptions

**Model assumptions.** Time is one external persistent real parameter. The
system is coupled to a uniform reservoir of fixed positive temperature; the
reduced geometry is not an isolated energy-conserving system. Take a finite,
connected simple graph with at least one three-edge cycle and with at most
two such cycles incident on any edge. Vertices and edges persist; triangles
may appear or disappear. Every edge has intrinsic length a>0, and occupied
triangles are equilateral Euclidean cells glued isometrically along edges.
There is no physical ambient plane or bulk. All triangles use identical
couplings, with no prescribed space-dependent driving.

Boundary conditions: no vertices or edges enter through the outer graph
boundary; the heat reservoir exchanges energy but no graph elements. The
initial distribution is specified independently of the transition law. The
witness starts with no face. Natural units c=hbar=k_B=1 are conventions only;
there is no postulated Lorentz metric or causal cone. No kinetic field or
negative-energy kinetic term is present in this stochastic model.

## Notation

G is the persistent graph; T is its set of three-edge cycles, interpreted as
possible simplices rather than physical empty interiors. F is a subset of T;
K(F) contains G and precisely the faces in F. Its physical space is |K(F)|.
N_2=|F| counts faces. For an edge e, n_e is its number of incident occupied
faces and N_b counts edges with n_e=1. A=sqrt(3)a^2/4 is a face area.
The area coefficient mu and line tension tau define the energy below; tau>=0,
while mu may have either sign. beta=1/T_R is inverse reservoir temperature,
nu>0 an attempt rate, and P_F(t) a state probability. Spatial dimension is
denoted s, not d; d would be the total spacetime dimension if a suitable
spacetime construction existed. B_j denotes an oriented simplicial boundary
matrix from j-chains to (j-1)-chains over the real numbers.

## Derivation

### 1. Legal move and physical dimension — exact

For any sigma in T, toggle its membership in F. Addition is legal because
all three boundary edges and vertices already belong to K; removal is legal
because there are no higher cells for which sigma is a required face. Thus
both operations preserve simplicial closure and are mutual inverses. They
change the chain-group dimension dim C_2 by one, not a label on an unchanged
cell. Only the closed edge-neighborhood of sigma is involved.

An interior point of an unfilled cycle's edge, away from vertices and any
occupied adjacent face, has an interval neighborhood and s=1. After filling,
an interior point of that same edge has a half-disk neighborhood, of local
topological dimension two; the new face interior has disk neighborhoods with
s=2. Edges outside the attached face remain one-dimensional away from their
junctions. The interface need not be a manifold. At a branching vertex we
make no smooth-tangent-space claim. This is literal local topological change
within the stipulated piecewise-Euclidean ontology, not a spectral-dimension
estimate or an emergent macroscopic direction.

### 2. Autonomous law and energy accounting — exact model equations

Choose the finite-state energy

    E(F) = mu A N_2(F) + tau a N_b(F).

The graph's constant baseline energy is subtracted. N_b measures exposed
face edges, not bare graph edges: an edge with no attached face contributes
zero. Suppose an absent sigma is added, and q of its edges already carry
one face. Because each edge has at most two possible faces, its three edges
previously have occupation zero or one. For 3-q edges the boundary count
increases by one; for q edges it decreases by one. Hence

    Delta E = E(F union {sigma}) - E(F)
            = mu A + tau a(3-2q).                         (1)

Removal has the negative difference evaluated for the corresponding pair.
This is local even though E is written as a global sum. Define, for every
legal neighboring pair F,F', the heat-bath rate

    w(F -> F') = nu/[1 + exp(beta(E(F')-E(F)))].           (2)

The exact master equation is

    dP_F/dt = sum_{F' adjacent F}
             [w(F' -> F)P_F' - w(F -> F')P_F].            (3)

Pairwise cancellation gives sum_F dP_F/dt=0. Positive finite rates and a
finite number of states give a nonexplosive, autonomous process. In
particular, the entire trajectory is not prescribed by initial data.
The ratio of opposite rates is exp(-beta Delta E), so

    pi_F = exp(-beta E(F))/Z,
    pi_F w(F -> F') = pi_F' w(F' -> F),                   (4)

where Z sums over all subsets F. Every subset communicates by toggles, so
this positive-temperature stationary distribution is unique. “Reversible”
here means detailed balance, not reversible individual dissipative histories.

At each jump the geometry receives heat Q_geom=Delta E, the reservoir
receives Q_res=-Delta E, and external protocol work is zero. Equation (3)
therefore gives d<E>/dt=sum_F P_F sum_F' w(F->F')Delta E.
An ideal fixed-temperature reservoir is an assumption, not a derived finite
Hamiltonian completion. There is no claim of geometry-only energy conservation.

**Region selection:** the two-dimensional region is the union of occupied
face interiors with their incidence neighborhoods. Existing cycles determine
where attachment is eligible; uniform rates, neighboring occupancy through q,
and reservoir fluctuations select which cycles actually fill. There is no
inserted dimension profile. The graph's supply of eligible cycles is nevertheless
a kinematic prior: this law cannot form a face where its boundary is absent.
Formation and destruction occur; translation of a persistent object and
stable finite-domain selection have not been established.

### 3. One tiny witness — exact energy relaxation and incidence diagnostic

Let G have vertices {0,1,2,3}, edges {01,12,20,03}, and only the possible
face sigma={0,1,2}. Edge 03 is a one-dimensional exterior appendage. There
are two states, empty (0) and filled (1), with

    E_0=0, E_1=epsilon=mu A+3 tau a.

Writing p=P_1, the two opposite rates sum to nu. Equation (3) reduces to

    dp/dt = nu(p_eq-p),  p_eq=1/[1+exp(beta epsilon)],
    p(t)=p_eq(1-exp(-nu t)),  <E>(t)=epsilon p(t),         (5)

for p(0)=0. Thus positive-energy space formation is thermally suppressed but
nonzero, and its mean reservoir energy loss is exactly epsilon p(t).
Negative epsilon instead releases energy on formation. The occupied state
has open disk-like points and the unoccupied tail has open interval-like
points simultaneously. This finite object is not an asymptotic vacuum.

For a coordinate-independent incidence diagnostic orient the cycle edges
as 01,12,20. Then B_2 sigma=(1,1,1,0)^T and B_1 B_2=0:
the three oriented vertex differences telescope. Since G is connected,
rank B_1=3 and dim ker B_1=4-3=1. Without the face rank B_2=0;
with it rank B_2=1. Consequently b_1=dim ker B_1-rank B_2 changes
from one to zero. This checks actual filling rather than proving dimension
from homology alone; the neighborhood argument supplies dimension.

## Approximations

No perturbation expansion or numerical approximation enters (1)-(5).
The finite complex and memoryless bath are defining idealizations, not a
controlled truncation of GR. For beta epsilon>>1, p_eq is approximately
exp(-beta epsilon), with absolute correction O(exp(-2 beta epsilon)); the
exact expression should otherwise be used. No continuum limit is taken.

## Validity regime

Finite G, finite couplings, a>0, T_R>0, and nu>0 suffice. Intrinsic charts
inside occupied triangles are auxiliary; permutation of graph names changes
no energy or dimension. There is no diffeomorphism constraint or gauge field.
Instantaneous moves are local in graph support but have no relativistic
speed bound. Relational observables may be used on this labeled substrate;
a quotient measure over unlabeled complexes is not constructed.

## Dimensional check

With energy unit E, [a]=E^-1, [A]=E^-2, [mu]=E^3,
[tau]=E^2, [beta]=E^-1, and [nu]=E. Thus (1) has energy
dimension, beta Delta E and nu t are dimensionless, and (3) has inverse-time
dimension. B_j and Betti numbers are dimensionless. Positive tau penalizes
exposed face boundary; shared edges reduce its contribution with the sign
shown in (1). Finite state count makes E bounded below even for negative mu.

## Limiting-case checks

At epsilon=0, both witness rates are nu/2 and p_eq=1/2, with zero heat
per jump despite geometry change. At beta->0 every face subset is equally
likely. At beta->infinity positive epsilon suppresses formation, whereas
negative epsilon favors filling; exactly zero-temperature dynamics can lose
irreducibility. At nu->0 the initial complex freezes. For q=0 versus q=2,
the boundary contribution in (1) is respectively +3 tau a versus -tau a,
as direct edge counting requires. A vertex-only graph Laplacian is identical
before and after the witness move: such a probe is blind. Universal coupling
to the physical interiors is therefore an additional, unresolved requirement.

## Unresolved concerns

The major blocker is macroscopic geometry: a pre-existing supply of small
cycles and independently allowed fills does not produce a smooth extended
two-dimensional phase, an isotropic continuum, or a particle. A face may
appear without matter-transfer conditions, because matter was not included.
The bath and absolute time prevent a closed relativistic interpretation.
Lower-dimensional open regions are literal here, but the target 3+1/4+1
vacuum, universal propagation, gravity, and quantum unitarity remain absent.

**Staged roadmap:** first challenge the two-state incidence/energy witness
with the bounded check above and internal critique. If retained, the next
scientific gate is whether one consistent matter coupling actually resolves
face interiors rather than only vertices. Only after that should many-cell
states be tested for extended geometric regions and finite interfaces.
Closed energy accounting and causal dynamics precede any continuum or
quantum-gravity interpretation. No further move family is proposed here.
