# D016: Regular core Palatini trace rows

## Question addressed

Does the adopted duplicated-trace presentation of the fixed-interface planar D005 Stueckelberg branch yield a faithful combined Palatini/interface primary system whose first preservation classifies the residual C016 rank-ten block, or does it expose a precise coupled-sector obstruction?

## Branch charter

In the same frozen duplicated-trace presentation, compute the five-dimensional Palatini Legendre derivative at the exact regular circle-collapse boundary, separating persistent `ab`, mixed `a4`, persistent coframe, collapsed-leg, and vertical-connection directions. Use only admissible D002/D004 equalizer tangents and explicitly distinguish pullback, circle pushforward, and independent interface-copy variations. Embed the surviving entries into the core bulk-plus-gluing primary matrix and determine whether any core row overlaps each of the five planar `dQ_C` directions; test the D010 shared-row assertion directly. Carry the first preservation of the relevant core Palatini defining primaries far enough to display all torsion/coframe or mixed-index equations that touch the gluing/interface sector, without an inverse coframe or an assumed bulk solution. Mixed `SO(1,4)` rows are diagnostic only: if preservation sources the excluded sector, record that as the stopping obstruction rather than silently widening scope. Stop once survival/overlap and the first coupled equation are decided.

## Distinction from sibling branches

This branch uses the director-frozen **duplicated-trace canonical
presentation**.  In particular, the interface coframe `epsilon_+` is not
silently identified with the bulk trace before the primary matrix is formed.
The calculation first differentiates the five-dimensional Legendre map on all
regular equalizer tangents, and only then introduces explicit gluing
constraints.  This is materially different from inferring a shared row from an
intrinsic/distributional trace momentum.  D015 and D017 were not inspected.
Distinct GPT sessions are procedural separation, not independent evidence.

## Derivation

### 1. Exact `4+1` Legendre split

Write

\[
 E^A=E_t^A dt+\boldsymbol E^A,
 \qquad \omega^{AB}=\omega_t^{AB}dt+\boldsymbol\omega^{AB}. \tag{6}
\]

The time-derivative part of the D004 potential is exactly

\[
 \Theta_{Y_+}=\int_{Y_+}\delta\boldsymbol\omega^{AB}
             \wedge P_{AB}(\boldsymbol E).             \tag{7}
\]

Therefore, with no approximation,

\[
 \pi_{\boldsymbol\omega}^{AB}=P^{AB}(\boldsymbol E),
 \qquad \pi_{\boldsymbol E,A}=0,\qquad
 \pi_{\omega_t}^{AB}=\pi_{E_t,A}=0.                   \tag{8}
\]

The temporal fields have no velocity.  Their preservation supplies the
spatial projections of the connection and coframe equations; they must not be
dropped as “lapse-like” data in a rank-changing problem.

The internal `4+1` split of (4), for independent pair labels `ab` and `4a`, is

\[
 \boxed{P_{ab}={1\over4\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}
 \boldsymbol E^c\wedge\boldsymbol E^d\wedge\boldsymbol E^4,}
                                                               \tag{9}
\]

\[
 \boxed{P_{4a}={1\over12\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}
 \boldsymbol E^b\wedge\boldsymbol E^c\wedge\boldsymbol E^d,
 \qquad P_{a4}=-P_{4a}.}                                \tag{10}
\]

The factors follow from three placements of the internal `4` in (9) and the
ordered-pair convention.  Equations (9)--(10) are bulk Legendre maps on all of
`Y_+`.  In a positive-radius collar, (9) is generally nonzero and (10) is
generally nonzero.  Nothing below says that the bulk Legendre map vanishes away
from the collapsed boundary.

Near `widetilde C`, split every one-form as

\[
 \boldsymbol E^a=E_h^a+E_v^a\eta,
 \qquad \delta\boldsymbol E^a=\delta E_h^a+
       \delta E_v^a\eta,
 \qquad \delta\boldsymbol E^4=\delta\rho\,\eta+
       \rho\,\delta\eta .                              \tag{11}
\]

Regularity gives, exactly at the cut,

\[
 E_v^a=\delta E_v^a=\rho=\delta\rho=0,
 \qquad j_C^*\boldsymbol E^4=j_C^*\delta\boldsymbol E^4=0. \tag{12}
\]

The same equalizer statement applies to allowed vertical connection
coefficients.  Horizontal and vertical components are therefore separated,
not discarded by a gauge choice.

Already at the level of the defining momentum, (9) has the zero factor
`E^4`, while (10) is a three-form made from horizontal persistent coframes on
the two-dimensional spatial base `C`.  Hence, on the spatial interface cut,

\[
 \boxed{j_C^*P_{ab}=j_C^*P_{4a}=0,
 \qquad p_!j_C^*P_{AB}=p_!^Hj_C^*P_{AB}=0.}            \tag{12a}
\]

Thus no five-dimensional connection-momentum defining-primary row has a
nonzero **tangential pullback to `C times S^1`**, and neither ordinary nor Haar
circle pushforward retains one.  This statement does not concern contraction
with the one-sided spatial normal before pullback.  The derivative test below
is still necessary: zero tangential pullback does not imply zero field
derivative or zero normal contraction.

Indeed, let an admissible adapted spatial coframe have
`E^1=dx^1`, `E^2=dx^2`, `E^3=ds`, let `n_s=partial_s`, and take the planar
tangential pullback `j_C^*E^3=0`.  Then the mixed row supplies the explicit
normal-contraction witness

\[
 j_C^*\iota_{n_s}P_{40}
 ={1\over2\kappa _5}dx^1\wedge dx^2\ne0,              \tag{12b}
\]

while `j_C^*P_{40}=0`.  For the admissible persistent variation
`delta E^1=chi dx^1`,

\[
 j_C^*\iota_{n_s}dP_{40}[\delta E]
 ={\chi\over2\kappa _5}dx^1\wedge dx^2\ne0,           \tag{12c}
\]

although `j_C^*dP_{40}=0`.  Normal contraction removes the `ds` leg before
restriction; circle pushforward removes a circle-vertical leg and cannot
replace this operation.  Equations (12b)--(12c) are compatible with the
equalizer because `ds` is the spatial normal, not the collapsed-circle
vertical one-form.

### 2. Boundary-pulled derivative of every Palatini row

Differentiating (9)--(10) gives the exact maps

\[
 dP_{ab}[\delta E]
 ={1\over2\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}
 \delta\boldsymbol E^c\wedge\boldsymbol E^d\wedge
 \boldsymbol E^4
 +{1\over4\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}
 \boldsymbol E^c\wedge\boldsymbol E^d\wedge
 \delta\boldsymbol E^4,                               \tag{13}
\]

\[
 dP_{4a}[\delta E]
 ={1\over4\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}
 \delta\boldsymbol E^b\wedge\boldsymbol E^c\wedge
 \boldsymbol E^d,
 \qquad dP_{a4}=-dP_{4a}.                             \tag{14}
\]

The admissible classes needed to test D010 are exhaustive:

| row | tangent class | regular cut result | reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ab` | persistent horizontal `delta E_h^a` | `0` | every term retains `E^4` |
| `ab` | persistent vertical `delta E_v^a eta` | `0` | `delta E_v^a|_C=0`, and an `E^4` remains |
| `ab` | collapsed leg `delta E^4` | `0` | `delta rho=rho=0` in (12) |
| `4a`/`a4` | persistent horizontal `delta E_h^a` | `0` after `j_C^*` | three horizontal one-forms on the two-dimensional `C` |
| `4a`/`a4` | persistent vertical `delta E_v^a eta` | `0` | its regular vertical coefficient vanishes |
| any | connection variation horizontal/vertical | not a column of `dP` | `P` depends only on `E`; vertical connection coefficients matter in the potential but vanish regularly at the cut |

Thus, specifically for tangential pullback,

\[
 \boxed{j_C^*dP_{AB}[\delta E]=0\quad
 \text{for every }AB\text{ and every admissible equalizer tangent}.} \tag{15}
\]

The tangential pullback in (15) is a three-form on `widetilde C`.  Ordinary and
normalized circle pushforward therefore also give

\[
 \boxed{p_!j_C^*dP_{AB}=p_!^Hj_C^*dP_{AB}=0.}          \tag{16}
\]

For the persistent rows this can also be checked one-sided before collapse:

\[
 p_!dP_{ab}={2\pi\over4\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}
 \left[2\rho\,\delta e^c\wedge e^d
       +\delta\rho\,e^c\wedge e^d\right],             \tag{17}
\]

up to terms whose vertical coefficient itself vanishes.  Both coefficients
are zero at `Sigma`.  Haar normalization removes only `2pi`; it cannot convert
this zero into a finite row.  At fixed `rho=R>0`, by contrast, (17) is nonzero
and reproduces the ordinary Kaluza--Klein Legendre derivative after
`kappa_4=kappa_5/(2pi R)`.  This explicitly separates zero tangential
pullback/pushed flux from a zero bulk Legendre map or zero normal contraction.

### 3. Comparison with the independent copy `dQ_C`

On the copy phase space, differentiation of (2) gives

\[
 dQ_{ab}[\delta\epsilon]
 ={b\over2}\epsilon_{abcd}
 \delta\epsilon_+^c\wedge\epsilon_+^d.                \tag{18}
\]

After pullback to the planar spatial cut, D006's basis argument gives

\[
 \operatorname{rank}dQ_C=5,
 \qquad \operatorname{im}dQ_C
 =\operatorname{span}\{dQ_{01},dQ_{02},dQ_{03},
 dQ_{13},dQ_{23}\},                                   \tag{19}
\]

with `dQ_12` absent in the D006 convention.  The precise displayed basis is
frame dependent, while rank five is invariant under an invertible persistent
Lorentz change of basis.

Before gluing, `delta E` and `delta epsilon_+` are coordinates of different
phase-space factors.  Combining (15) and (19),

\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{im}(p_!j_C^*dP)\cap
 \operatorname{im}(dQ_C)=\{0\}:\quad
 \text{the overlap is exactly zero in the duplicated phase space}.} \tag{20}
\]

It is not nonzero.  If one first identifies `epsilon_+=Tr E` without specifying
the induced Dirac bracket or gluing reduction, the phrase “same canonical row”
is instead undefined; it is not an alternative calculation of a nonzero
overlap.  Equation (20) is the well-defined pre-gluing answer.
More precisely, it is the answer in the **unreduced duplicated-trace phase
space**.  It does not determine the bracket after imposing (21) and performing
the associated Dirac trace reduction.

### 4. Canonical gluing matrix

On a spatial cut, (5) yields the constraints

\[
 G_e=\epsilon_{+,C}-\operatorname{Tr}_C E=0,
 \qquad G_\omega=\Omega_{+,C}-\operatorname{Tr}_C\omega^{ab}=0, \tag{21}
\]

together with zero momenta for all temporal and spatial multiplier
components.  The temporal equations are not omitted:

\[
 \epsilon_{+,t}-\operatorname{Tr}E_t=0,
 \qquad \Omega_{+,t}-\operatorname{Tr}\omega_t^{ab}=0. \tag{22}
\]

Keep the bulk and copy coframe momenta distinct:

\[
 C_E=\pi_E,
 \qquad C_\epsilon=p_\epsilon,
 \qquad C_\omega=\pi_\omega-P(E),
 \qquad \Phi_v=p_v-Q(\epsilon_+).                     \tag{23}
\]

Using the standard canonical convention `{q,p}=I`, the relevant ordered
primary/bracket submatrix is, suppressing form-delta kernels,

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 &C_\omega&\Phi_v&G_e\\ \hline
 C_E       &+dP&0&+\operatorname{Tr}_C\\
 C_\epsilon&0&+dQ&-I
\end{array}.                                           \tag{24}
\]

Antisymmetry fixes the transposed blocks.  For example
`{C_omega,C_E}=-dP`, matching D010's ordering.  The connection-copy analogue
contains `G_omega` with incidence blocks `(+Tr_C,-I)` between bulk and copy
connection momenta.  At the regular cut the tangentially pulled `dP` block in
(24) is zero, while `dQ` has rank five.  The two momentum rows are linked only
by the gluing incidence column; they are not a pre-existing shared row.

This also explains why neither an additive bulk/interface count nor immediate
elimination of `G_e` is legitimate.  Any canonical stabilization of (21) must
correlate the corresponding velocities, and eliminating it changes brackets
by a representation-sensitive Dirac reduction that is not computed here.

### 5. Weak covariant Euler/gluing equations: targets for canonical preservation

The following equations are obtained by covariant variation of the duplicated
action.  They show where gluing multipliers couple bulk and copy at the action
level and are necessary targets for a future Dirac preservation chain.  They
are **not** a completed canonical first-preservation calculation: this artifact
does not split every gluing multiplier into temporal and spatial components,
list all multiplier-momentum primaries, construct the total Hamiltonian, or
compute the post-gluing Dirac bracket.  Define the exact five-dimensional
Palatini Euler forms (D004 conventions)

\[
 {\cal C}^{(+)}_{AB}:={1\over4\kappa _5}
 \epsilon_{ABCDE}T^C\wedge E^D\wedge E^E,             \tag{25}
\]

\[
 {\cal G}^{(+)}_A:={1\over4\kappa _5}
 \epsilon_{ABCDE}R^{BC}\wedge E^D\wedge E^E
 -{\Lambda_5\over24\kappa _5}\epsilon_{ABCDE}
 E^B\wedge E^C\wedge E^D\wedge E^E.                 \tag{26}
\]

In the ordinary bulk Palatini `4+1` split, the zero momenta of `omega_t` and
`E_t` are expected to yield the all-spatial projections of (25) and (26), while
the spatial defining primaries yield complementary equations containing one
temporal leg and multiplier relations.  A faithful total Hamiltonian including
(5) must reproduce every such component.  Here none is set to zero
prematurely, but that component-by-component canonical derivation is left
undone.

Because a trace functional is present, the bulk equations with gluing are most
precisely written weakly.  For arbitrary regular bulk variations,

\[
 \int_{M_+}\delta\omega^{ab}\wedge{\cal C}^{(+)}_{ab}
 -\int_\Sigma s_{ab}\wedge\operatorname{Tr}\delta\omega^{ab}=0, \tag{27}
\]

\[
 \int_{M_+}\delta E^a\wedge{\cal G}^{(+)}_a
 -\int_\Sigma r_a\wedge\operatorname{Tr}\delta E^a=0. \tag{28}
\]

Covariant variation of the independent copy connection and coframe gives, with
the sign convention (5),

\[
 \boxed{Q+\lambda+s=0,}                                \tag{29}
\]

\[
 \boxed{H_a(K)+\mu_a+r_a=0.}                           \tag{30}
\]

Equations (27)--(30), together with (21)--(22), display the **first explicit
bulk/copy/interface coupling in the covariant duplicated action** and the
coupling that a canonical stabilization must recover.  In particular, the
first displayed weak Euler equation containing the five-direction interface
momentum is (29), not a nonzero tangential `dP` overlap.

The following separation is conditional.  Assume locally that the regular
persistent trace map is sufficiently surjective: after choosing an arbitrary
compactly supported regular bulk variation, its persistent boundary trace can
also be varied independently in the allowed horizontal trace class.  Assume
also that the bulk Euler forms are ordinary smooth forms, with no
distributional term supported on `Sigma`.  Under precisely these assumptions,
the bulk and boundary-supported functionals in (27)--(28) separate:

\[
 {\cal C}^{(+)}_{ab}=0,\quad s_{ab}=0,
 \qquad {\cal G}^{(+)}_a=0,\quad r_a=0,             \tag{31}
\]

so (29)--(30) reduce to the intrinsic-copy equations
`Q+lambda=0` and `H(K)+mu=0`.  This demonstrates equivalence of the gluing
Euler pairs to the D005 intrinsic-trace **covariant variation** under the local
trace-surjectivity/separation assumption.  It does not establish equivalence
of canonical preservation or of the eventual Dirac brackets.

### 6. Mixed rows and the stopping obstruction

The unchanged five-dimensional bulk also requires the unsourced mixed and
collapsed-leg equations

\[
 \boxed{{\cal C}^{(+)}_{a4}
 =-{1\over4\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}
 T^b\wedge E^c\wedge E^d=0,}                          \tag{32}
\]

\[
 \boxed{{\cal G}^{(+)}_4
 ={1\over4\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}R^{ab}\wedge E^c\wedge E^d
 -{\Lambda_5\over24\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}
 E^a\wedge E^b\wedge E^c\wedge E^d=0.}              \tag{33}
\]

The persistent connection equation itself contains the mixed torsion channel,

\[
 {\cal C}^{(+)}_{ab}={1\over4\kappa _5}\epsilon_{abcd}
 \left(2T^c\wedge E^d\wedge E^4
       +T^4\wedge E^c\wedge E^d\right)=0.             \tag{34}
\]

At collapse the first term vanishes, but

\[
 T^4|_\Sigma=(dE^4+\omega^4{}_a\wedge E^a)|_\Sigma
              =\omega^4{}_a\wedge e^a                \tag{35}
\]

need not vanish merely because the allowed **vertical** connection coefficient
vanishes.  Likewise (26) for `A=a` contains mixed-curvature components
`R^{b4}`.  Hence consistency of a truncation that omits horizontal
`omega^{a4}` is undecidable from the zero tangential Legendre trace alone; no
inconsistency is demonstrated here.

Finally, pullback of a four-form equation to the three-dimensional boundary of
the spatial cut is identically zero and loses its normal information.  The
full covariant equations, and any canonical preservation that reproduces them,
include the normal projections

\[
 \iota_{n_s}{\cal C}^{(+)}_{AB}|_\Sigma=0,
 \qquad \iota_{n_s}{\cal G}^{(+)}_A|_\Sigma=0,         \tag{36}
\]

which contain the regular one-sided normal jets of `E`, `omega`, `T`, and `R`.
Neither the planar tangential coframe (3) nor the D005 shell specifies these
jets or the horizontal mixed trace `omega^{a4}|_Sigma`.  Equations
(32)--(36) are therefore the exact missing core conditions, and they leave the
persistent-truncation consistency verdict undecidable.  In accordance
with the frozen scope, this derivation stops here rather than setting mixed
data to zero by fiat or enlarging the interface gauge group.

## Approximations

No perturbative expansion is used.  Equations (4)--(36) are exact in the
declared fixed-interface regular class.  Collapse means exact substitution of
the regular boundary values `rho=delta rho=0`, not retention through some
order in small `rho`.  No normal-jet, curvature, torsion, or mixed-connection
term is discarded.  The planar specialization is used only for the exact rank
of `dQ_C`.

## Validity regime

The result is local and pointwise on a fixed timelike planar interface, for
basic fields, a fixed marked-circle normalization, the smooth D002 equalizer,
the unchanged five-dimensional Palatini bulk, independent persistent trace
copies, and the fixed-seed D005 Stueckelberg branch.  It excludes moving
interfaces, inverse-coframe reductions, profile-divided/distributional traces,
nonbasic modes, large gauge transformations, unrestricted corner gauge, full
interface `SO(1,4)`, and a final constraint or degree count.  The rank-five
statement is valid while the induced planar triad remains rank three.

## Dimensional check

From `[E]=L` and `[kappa_5]=L^3`, `[P_AB]=1` as an integrated three-form and
`[dP/dE]=L^-1`.  Equations (13)--(14) have dimension `L^-1 times delta E`,
hence dimensionless, as required.  Since `[b]=L^-2`, both `Q` and `dQ` are
dimensionless integrated two-forms.  In (5), `epsilon-Tr E` has dimension
`L`, so `[r]=L^-1`; `Omega-Tr omega` is dimensionless, so `[s]=1`.  Therefore
(29) pairs dimensionless two-forms and (30) pairs `L^-1` two-forms.  Ordinary
and Haar pushforwards differ only by dimensionless `2pi`.  With
`n_s=partial_s`, normal contraction carries one inverse length, so both sides
of (12b)--(12c) have dimension `L^-1`.

Signs and factors are controlled by: (i) the three placements of the omitted
internal index in (9)--(10); (ii) the two ordered placements in (18); (iii) the
chosen plus signs in (5); and (iv) the standard `{q,p}=I` convention in (24).
Changing cut orientation reverses all pushed momenta together and leaves
zero, rank, and overlap unchanged.

## Limiting-case checks

1. **Fixed radius.** For `rho=R>0`, (17) is nonzero and with
   `kappa_4=kappa_5/(2pi R)` reproduces the four-dimensional Palatini
   derivative.  Thus (15) is specifically an exact-collapse result.
2. **Collapsed leg varied regularly.** `delta E^4=delta rho eta+rho delta eta`
   still vanishes at the cut; allowing `delta rho|_Sigma !=0` would leave the
