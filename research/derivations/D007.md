# D007: Direct canonical split of the complete local action

## Question addressed

Within the fixed-interface planar basic persistent `SO(1,3)` sector of
`D005`/`D006`, what is the minimum scientifically complete bulk-plus-interface
Lorentz generator and boundary polarization needed to decide whether the C016
Stueckelberg rank-ten block is first-class gauge, physical edge structure, or a
second-class remnant?

## Branch charter

Starting from the unmodified `D005` bulk actions plus interface action, perform
the smallest local `4+1`/`3+1` split needed to expose the core, exterior, and
interface Lorentz kinetic terms, primary constraints, differentiable Gauss
generators, and their boundary terms on a planar cut. Track the `D005`
algebraic shell but do not attempt diffeomorphism/Hamiltonian constraints or
hyperbolicity. Stop once the total Lorentz constraint matrix can be stated or
once a precise missing datum prevents it.

## Distinction from sibling branches

This branch uses a direct canonical split of the **unmodified complete local
action**, retaining the Palatini simplicity/coframe primaries long enough to
test whether the interface rank-ten matrix is actually a separate block. It is
not an interface-only rank computation, a covariant-phase-space charge
argument, or an inferred classification from covariance. In accordance with
the first-pass barrier, no sibling derivation from `D008`--`D014` was inspected.
Parallel GPT branches are not independent evidence.

## Target claim

The target is either:

1. the total persistent Lorentz constraint matrix, including its cut terms,
   sufficient to classify the Stueckelberg block; or
2. an exact localization of the first omitted equation or polarization datum
   without which that matrix is undefined.

The result below is exact, not perturbative. It derives the improved total
Lorentz generators and their cancellation on the `D005` shell. It also finds
that the rank-ten interface matrix is not a direct summand of the complete
primary matrix: it shares the boundary coframe variables with the bulk
Palatini simplicity/coframe primaries. Its final Dirac classification therefore
requires their torsion stabilization, together with a declared corner/seed
polarization. Those data are outside the permitted Lorentz-only split, so the
derivation stops there rather than manufacturing a Hamiltonian.

## Derivation

### Bulk and interface Lorentz potentials

#### 1. Exact core `4+1` split

Write

\[
\omega^{AB}=\omega_t^{AB}dt+\boldsymbol\omega^{AB},\qquad
R^{AB}=dt\wedge(\dot{\boldsymbol\omega}^{AB}
 -\mathbf D\omega_t^{AB})+\mathbf R^{AB}. \tag{7}
\]

Substitution in the curvature term of `D005` (21) gives the only connection
velocity term

\[
L_{5,\mathrm{kin}}=\int_{Y_+}
 \Pi^+_{AB}\wedge\dot{\boldsymbol\omega}^{AB}. \tag{8}
\]

This is an exact identity. Terms containing `E_t^A`, `\omega_t^{AB}`, spatial
curvature, or the cosmological term have no connection velocity and are not
needed to identify the Lorentz potential. Therefore

\[
\boxed{\Theta_+=\int_{Y_+}
 \delta\boldsymbol\omega^{AB}\wedge\Pi^+_{AB}.} \tag{9}
\]

The coefficient `1/12` in (4) is unchanged: the ordered antisymmetric-pair and
form conventions already account for the permutations.

#### 2. Exact exterior `3+1` split

Similarly,

\[
\mathcal R^{ab}=dt\wedge(\dot{\boldsymbol\varpi}^{ab}
 -\mathbf D\varpi_t^{ab})+\mathbf{\mathcal R}^{ab}, \tag{10}
\]

and the exterior kinetic term and potential are

\[
L_{4,\mathrm{kin}}=\int_{Y_-}
 \Pi^-_{ab}\wedge\dot{\boldsymbol\varpi}^{ab},
\qquad
\boxed{\Theta_-=\int_{Y_-}
 \delta\boldsymbol\varpi^{ab}\wedge\Pi^-_{ab}.} \tag{11}
\]

The sign in (11) is the bulk canonical sign. Its integration-by-parts boundary
term below has the opposite sign to the enriched exterior interface moment map;
this reproduces, rather than contradicts, the incident sign in `D005` (22).

#### 3. Exact interface `2+1` split

The `D006` split of the unchanged action (1), expressed in the locally
invertible variables `(g,v)`, is

\[
\boxed{\Theta_I=\int_C\left[
 \langle\lambda_C,\delta g\,g^{-1}\rangle
 +\langle Q_C,\delta v\,v^{-1}\rangle\right].} \tag{12}
\]

Neither `\mu` nor a connection coefficient receives an interface velocity.
Combining (9), (11), and (12) gives the minimum complete Lorentz polarization
on the cut:

\[
\boxed{\Theta_{\mathrm{tot}}=\Theta_++\Theta_-+\Theta_I.} \tag{13}
\]

On the shell (2), (12) is equivalently
`\int_C\langle Q_C,g\delta u\,u^{-1}g^{-1}\rangle`; its two-form has the C016
rank ten. Equation (13), rather than (12) alone, is required for a bulk gauge
test.

## Primary constraints

Introduce independent canonical momenta `\pi^+_{AB},\pi^-_{ab}` for the bulk
connections and `p^+_A,p^-_a` for the spatial coframes. The bulk primary
constraints include

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Phi^+_{AB}&:=\pi^+_{AB}-\Pi^+_{AB}(\mathbf E)\simeq0,
&p^+_A&\simeq0,\\
\Phi^-_{ab}&:=\pi^-_{ab}-\Pi^-_{ab}(\mathbf e_-)\simeq0,
&p^-_a&\simeq0. 
\end{aligned} \tag{14}
\]

Momenta of temporal connection/coframe components vanish. They are multiplier
primaries and are not expanded into Hamiltonian or diffeomorphism sectors here.
For the interface, right-trivialized momenta give

\[
\Phi_g:=p_g-\lambda_C\simeq0,
\qquad
\Phi_v:=p_v-Q_C(e_+)\simeq0, \tag{15}
\]

together with zero momenta for `e_+`, `\lambda`, `\mu`, and the connection
traces as interface variables. In `(g,u)` coordinates these are equivalent to
the `D006` constraints `p_g-(Q+\lambda)` and
`p_u-\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}Q`.

The important exact structural fact is

\[
\boxed{\{p_{e_+},\Phi_v\}=-{\delta Q_C\over\delta e_+}\ne0,
\qquad
\{p^+_E,\Phi^+\}=-{\delta\Pi^+\over\delta E}\ne0.} \tag{16}
\]

The interface `e_+` is the core boundary pullback, not a new unrelated
coframe. Consequently the first bracket in (16) occupies boundary rows of the
same coframe-primary system whose bulk rows enter the second bracket. One may
introduce duplicate trace variables plus gluing constraints, but that merely
enlarges the same matrix. Therefore the rank-ten `(v,Q(e_+))` block is **not an
independent direct summand** of the complete primary constraint matrix.

On the planar frame

\[
e_+^0=dt,
\quad e_+^1=dx^1,
\quad e_+^2=dx^2,
\quad e_+^3=0, \tag{17}

`Q_{03,C}=(b/2)dx^1\wedge dx^2`, and
`\operatorname{rank}(\delta Q_C/\delta e_{+A}^a)=5`. Hence (16) reproduces the
rank-ten local interface submatrix, but it does not determine the rank after
the bulk primary system is stabilized.

## Differentiability and boundary terms

For a spatial region `Y` with connection momentum `\pi`, the direct smeared
canonical Lorentz generator

\[
G_Y^{\mathrm{dir}}[\alpha]
=\int_Y\left[-\langle\pi,D\alpha\rangle
 +\langle p_E,\alpha E\rangle\right] \tag{18}
\]

is differentiable for fixed `\alpha` and generates
`\delta_\alpha\omega=-D\alpha`, `\delta_\alpha E=\alpha E`. Integrating the
first term by parts gives the exact improved form

\[
G_Y^{\mathrm{dir}}[\alpha]
=\int_Y\langle\alpha,\mathcal G_Y\rangle
 -\int_{\partial Y}\langle\alpha,\pi\rangle,
\qquad
\mathcal G_Y=D\pi+E\diamond p_E, \tag{19}
\]

with the obvious inclusion of every core field transforming under the
persistent subgroup. Equation (19) fixes the sign of the cut term without an
appeal to charge conventions.

The interface moment maps obtained by contracting (12) with independent core
and exterior transformations are exactly

\[
J_+^I=Q_C+\lambda_C,
\qquad
J_-^I=-\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\lambda_C. \tag{20}
\]

The core regular trace and exterior cut terms from (19) are

\[
B_+^{\mathrm{bulk}}=-p_*(\pi^+_{ab}|_{\widetilde C}),
\qquad
B_-^{\mathrm{bulk}}=-\pi^-_{ab}|_C. \tag{21}
\]

Thus the differentiable total persistent generators can be displayed either
in the direct form (18) plus canonical interface actions, or in the integrated
form

\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
G_+[\alpha_+]&=\int_{Y_+}\langle\alpha_+,\mathcal G_+\rangle
 +\int_C\langle\alpha_+,
 Q+\lambda-p_*\pi^+\rangle,\\
G_-[\alpha_-]&=\int_{Y_-}\langle\alpha_-,\mathcal G_-\rangle
 +\int_C\langle\alpha_-,
 -\pi^- -\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\lambda\rangle.
\end{aligned}} \tag{22}
\]

Here the interface canonical terms in the unintegrated generator generate the
actions on `g,v`; (22) displays their moment maps and the differentiability
improvements. Overall simultaneous reversal of the Hamiltonian-generator
convention changes both lines' signs but not their cancellation or algebra.

## Total Gauss generators and relevant brackets

On the primary Palatini surface, the shell (2), and the regular core descent
condition (5), the two cut coefficients in (22) vanish separately:

\[
Q+\lambda-p_*\pi^+=0,
\qquad
-P^- -\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\lambda
=-P^-+\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}Q=0. \tag{23}
\]

This is the decisive bulk-plus-interface cancellation absent from the
interface-only calculation. In particular, the generically nonzero
Stueckelberg temporal-cut term is canceled in the **total exterior Lorentz
generator** by the exterior Palatini flux. It is therefore not, by itself, a
physical Lorentz charge.

Before any non-Lorentz stabilization, covariance gives the exact weak bracket
structure

\[
\begin{aligned}
\{G_+[\alpha],G_+[\beta]\}&=G_+[[\alpha,\beta]],\\
\{G_-[\alpha],G_-[\beta]\}&=G_-[[\alpha,\beta]],\\
\{G_+[\alpha],G_-[\beta]\}&=0,\\
\{G_\pm[\alpha],\Phi_r\}&=(\rho_r(\alpha)\Phi)_r,
\end{aligned} \tag{24}
\]

for every covariant primary `\Phi_r` in (14)--(15), assuming the allowed
parameters preserve the stated boundary conditions and the fixed seed is
assigned the transformation law used in `D005`. The last line means that the
Gauss generators rotate the primary constraints into constraints; it does not
assert that the primary matrix itself has zero rank.

Equations (22)--(24) establish that the persistent Lorentz sector is a
first-class **candidate gauge system** with no residual cut moment map on (2).
They do not imply that the rank-ten two-form vanishes. Indeed, pulling (13) to
(2) leaves the rank-ten form found in C016, while Lorentz gauge vectors are
null directions of that pulled-back form. Quotienting null directions does not
turn a nonzero rank-ten form into zero; additional constraints are required if
all five pairs are to disappear.

The relevant unspecialized primary matrix has the schematic exact form

\[
\mathbb C_{\mathrm{prim}}=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-\delta\Pi^+/\delta E&0&0\\
(\delta\Pi^+/\delta E)^T&0&\mathcal T_C^T&0\\
0&-\mathcal T_C&0&-\delta Q/\delta e_+\\
0&0&+(\delta Q/\delta e_+)^T&A_Q
\end{pmatrix}
\oplus(\text{exterior Palatini and }g,\lambda\text{ defining blocks}), \tag{25}
\]

where `\mathcal T_C` denotes the pullback/gluing map identifying `e_+` with the
core trace, and
`(A_Q)_{IJ}=\langle Q,[T_I,T_J]\rangle`. Different choices between intrinsic
trace variables and duplicated boundary variables change the presentation of
`\mathcal T_C`, not the need for it. The lower-right sector alone is the C016
rank-ten block. Equation (25) is as far as the total matrix can be stated
without stabilizing (14).

## Exact classification blocker and stopping condition

Preservation in time of `p_E\simeq0` and
`\pi-\Pi(E)\simeq0` uses the coframe and connection equations. In first-order
Palatini gravity, the connection equation is the torsion equation (possibly
degenerate at the interface). Its boundary pullback determines:

1. which null directions of `\delta\Pi/\delta E` generate secondary
   constraints;
2. which combinations pair second-class with `p_E` and the simplicity
   constraints;
3. whether the five `\delta Q` directions in (25) are fixed, gauge-null, or
   survive as a reduced edge symplectic sector.

The temporal coframe components that multiply Hamiltonian/diffeomorphism-type
equations can also enter this stabilization. The charter forbids constructing
that Hamiltonian sector, and no inverse coframe is available to split the
degenerate torsion equation algebraically. Consequently deleting those rows,
or substituting `\pi=\Pi(e)` before computing the Dirac matrix, would change
the rank one is trying to classify.

There is a second, independent boundary datum: the action specifies a fixed
seed `a_0`, but a canonical phase space still must declare whether right
seed-frame transformations of `u` are gauge, global symmetry, or absent, and
whether Lorentz parameters vanish on temporal/lateral corners. If parameters
vanish at `C`, the cancellation (23) remains an identity but cannot be used to
quotient interface data. If they are allowed, (22) is the required improved
generator. A new corner polarization could alter which boundary data are held
fixed, although it cannot be chosen retrospectively to prove a desired rank.

Therefore the sharply localized blocker is:

\[
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
\text{the boundary pullback and stabilization of the Palatini}
\ (p_E,\pi-\Pi(E))\text{ system, including the torsion equation,}\\
\text{plus a declared seed/corner gauge polarization.}
\end{gathered}} \tag{26}
\]

This is mathematically narrower than a full Hamiltonian or hyperbolicity
analysis, but it necessarily touches equations excluded by the charter. The
derivation therefore stops at (26).

## Limiting-case checks

1. **No Stueckelberg field.** Removing `u` removes the `(v,Q)` term from (12).
   On `Q+\lambda=0`, the fixed-reference interface potential pulls back to
   zero, recovering the fixed-reference result of `D006`.
2. **Degenerate zero-area branch.** If `Q_C=0`, then
   `\delta Q_C` has lower rank and the rank-ten block vanishes. This is the
   excluded rank-at-most-one branch and confirms that the block is tied to
   nonzero triad area, not to `K`.
3. **`K=0` versus nonzero `H(K)=0`.** Neither choice changes (3), (12), or
   (22). This confirms that the six-dimensional kernel of `H` cannot decide
   the edge classification.
4. **Gauge parameters vanishing on `C`.** The boundary terms in (22) are then
   irrelevant to differentiability, but no boundary gauge quotient follows.
   This demonstrates why zero corner charge by support is not a proof of
   first-class edge gauge.
5. **Gauge parameters allowed on `C`.** The improved generators (22) are
   required, and (23) gives exact cancellation. This disproves the inference
   that the interface moment map alone is necessarily a physical charge.
6. **Interface action alone.** Dropping (11) removes `-P^-` from (23); the
   exterior connection equation then forces the contradiction `Q=0` already
   identified in `D006`. This checks that the bulk term is conclusion-critical.
7. **Regular core trace versus enriched circle current.** Setting the regular
   pushforward (5) to zero does not set `Q=0`; the first equation in (23) is
   instead satisfied by `\lambda=-Q`. This preserves the required distinction
   between the two objects.
