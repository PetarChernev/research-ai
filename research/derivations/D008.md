## Derivation

## Bulk covariant potentials/currents

### Exact Palatini relations

The bulk Lagrangians have the exact covariant potential

\[
 \boldsymbol\Theta_D(\delta)=
 \langle\delta\omega\wedge P^{(D)}\rangle .            \tag{8}
\]

For two commuting variations,

\[
 \boldsymbol\omega_D(\delta_1,\delta_2)
 =\langle\delta_2\omega\wedge\delta_1P\rangle
  -\langle\delta_1\omega\wedge\delta_2P\rangle .     \tag{9}
\]

On a bulk solution and for linearized solutions,
`d boldsymbol omega_D=0`. This is a within-stratum conservation law and does
not by itself impose interface matching.

For the two sides,

\[
 \Theta_{B_+}=\int_{B_+}\boldsymbol\Theta_5,
 \qquad \Theta_{B_-}=\int_{B_-}\boldsymbol\Theta_4.   \tag{10}
\]

The regular core trace obeys the exact restricted identity

\[
 p_!j_+^*\boldsymbol\Theta_5=0,
 \qquad p_!j_+^*\boldsymbol\omega_5=0,                 \tag{11}
\]

whereas the exterior trace is generally nonzero. Equation (11) is not a
statement about `Q`, which belongs to the enriched interface action.

## Lorentz contraction and Noether identities

### Exact local contraction

Using (2) in (8),

\[
 \boldsymbol\Theta_D(\delta_\alpha)
 =-\langle D\alpha\wedge P\rangle
 =-d\langle\alpha,P\rangle+\langle\alpha,DP\rangle .  \tag{12}
\]

Because (8) is invariant under simultaneous Lorentz transformation of its
arguments and `alpha` is field independent, field-space differentiation gives
the exact identity

\[
 \boxed{
 \boldsymbol\omega_D(\delta,\delta_\alpha)
 =-d\langle\alpha,\delta P\rangle
  +\langle\alpha,\delta(DP)\rangle .}                 \tag{13}
\]

This derivation retains the connection contribution hidden inside
`delta(DP)=D(delta P)+[delta omega,P]`; dropping it would spoil covariance.
Equation (13) is the covariant Lorentz Noether identity, not an on-shell
approximation.

### Sign-sensitive exterior bulk generator

Integrate (13) over `B_-`. Stokes' theorem with the outward orientation at its
interface boundary gives

\[
 \Omega_{B_-}(\delta,\delta_{\alpha_-})
 =\delta\left[
 \int_{B_-}\langle\alpha_-,D_\varpi P^-\rangle
 -\int_C\langle\alpha_-,P^-\rangle\right].             \tag{14}
\]

Thus the exterior bulk Lorentz generator and its interface moment map are

\[
 G_-^{\rm bulk}[\alpha_-]
 =\int_{B_-}\langle\alpha_-,D_\varpi P^-\rangle
  -\int_C\langle\alpha_-,P^-\rangle,
 \qquad J_-^{\rm bulk}=-P^- .                          \tag{15}
\]

This is the required independent sign reconstruction. It agrees with the
incident `-Theta_4` in D004: the same exterior face that contributes
`-delta varpi wedge P^-` to the action variation contributes `-P^-` to the
cut Lorentz moment map.

For the core,

\[
 G_+^{\rm bulk}[\alpha_+]
 =\int_{B_+}\langle\alpha_+,D_\omega P^+\rangle
  -\int_{\widetilde C}\langle\alpha_+,P^+\rangle .     \tag{16}
\]

The last term has zero persistent circle pushforward by (11), so the core bulk
supplies no finite persistent cut moment map in the frozen regular class.

## Interface contribution

The exact Stueckelberg interface cut potential inherited from the D005 action
is

\[
 \boxed{\Theta_C^I=\int_C
 \left[\langle\Pi,\xi\rangle+\langle Q,Z\rangle\right].}
 \tag{17}
\]

Equivalently, using `v=gu`,

\[
 \Theta_C^I=\int_C\left[
 \langle\lambda,\delta g g^{-1}\rangle
 +\langle Q,\delta v v^{-1}\rangle\right].             \tag{18}

Contracting (17) with (3) gives

\[
 \iota_{\delta_\alpha}\Theta_C^I

\]

Hence the interface moment maps are exactly

\[
 J_+^I=\Pi,
 \qquad J_-^I=-\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\lambda.      \tag{20}
\]

On shell, `J_+^I=0` and
`J_-^I=Ad_(g^{-1})Q`. This latter term is the apparent C016 charge.

## Total cut moment map on and off shell

Adding (15), (16), and (19), with the zero regular core trace understood, gives
the minimum differentiable total Lorentz generator in the unshifted D004/D005
polarization:

\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
 G_{\rm tot}[\alpha_+,\alpha_-]
 ={}&\int_{B_+}\langle\alpha_+,D_\omega P^+\rangle
 +\int_{B_-}\langle\alpha_-,D_\varpi P^-\rangle\\
 &+\int_C\langle\alpha_+,\Pi\rangle
 -\int_C\left\langle\alpha_-,
 P^-+\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\lambda\right\rangle .
\end{aligned}}                                                   \tag{21}
\]

The off-shell total cut moment maps are therefore

\[
 \boxed{J_+^{\rm tot}=\Pi,
 \qquad J_-^{\rm tot}=-(P^-+\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\lambda).}
 \tag{22}
\]

They are precisely the two connection boundary Euler forms of D005, expressed
in the two bundle frames. On the combined bulk-plus-interface shell (1),

\[
 \boxed{J_+^{\rm tot}=0,\qquad J_-^{\rm tot}=0.}        \tag{23}
\]

In particular,

\[
 \underbrace{-P^-}_{\text{exterior bulk}}
 +\underbrace{\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}Q}_{\text{interface}}
 =0                                                     \tag{24}
\]

uses `lambda=-Q` and `P^-=Ad_(g^{-1})Q`.

**Classification of the cancellation.** Equation (24) is **not** an off-shell
identity and is **not** produced by `H(K)=0`. It is the pullback of the total
moment map to the interface momentum-matching constraint surface. Its sign and

## Compact-support versus temporal/lateral corner cases

### Compact support

If `alpha_+`, `alpha_-`, and tangent variations vanish near every boundary of
the cut, the surface terms in (15)--(21) vanish. The bulk Lorentz contraction is
then proportional to `delta(DP)` and is null on the bulk Gauss shell. This is
the standard local first-class degeneracy. It says nothing about transformations
whose support reaches `C`.

If parameters may reach the internal cut `C` but vanish at the temporal and
lateral endpoints of the variational region, differentiability requires the
surface terms in (21). Equations (22)--(23) then show that the combined small
`SO(1,3)` transformation is a zero-moment-map direction on the full shell in
the unshifted polarization. Thus the `Ad_(g^{-1})Q` term alone is not a physical
Lorentz charge.

### Nonvanishing temporal corner

At a temporal cut, the Hamiltonian candidate is (21). In the D004/D005
polarization it vanishes on the complete shell, including the exterior bulk
piece. However, allowing a transformation at a temporal endpoint also requires
a declaration of which endpoint data are fixed and whether the transformation
is quotiented as gauge. The covariant computation establishes the zero total
moment map; it does not make that missing phase-space declaration.

### Lateral corner

When `partial C` is nonempty, (26) permits flux through
`I_t x partial C`. No one-dimensional lateral charge follows from (21) without
integrating a specified Gauss generator by parts under specified lateral
conditions. One must choose, for example, vanishing parameter/flux, fixed edge
frame, or a lateral action. None is declared in D005, so the derivation stops
before assigning a lateral charge.
