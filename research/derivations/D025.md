# D025: Lowest-derivative reduction-action classification

## Question addressed

In the exact C025/C027 finite-slope fixed-interface benchmark, does any element
of the **purely algebraic** local, inverse-coframe-free, `SO(1,4)`-covariant
polynomial operator space `A1`—built from a unit spacelike reduction field `V`,
`K=D_omega V`, and `E`, with exterior degree four and at most one `K`—both
cancel the free mixed `43` Palatini row and retain a freely selected `c>0`
rank-three polar background under complete independent `E,omega,V` variation?

The answer in the precisely frozen class `A1` is no. The invariant space is
two-dimensional: one zero-`K` volume control and D024's one-`K` primitive. The
coefficient required to cancel the mixed row is incompatible with the
coefficient required by a free rank-preserving triad rescaling.

## Branch charter

This is the sole theory worker in one bounded convergence cycle. It exhausts
the complete finite **algebraic** invariant class `A1`, varies its general
element, and stops after the exact rank-three polar coefficient test. There is
no delegation.

## Distinction from sibling branches

There is no sibling theory branch. Unlike D024, which tested one selected
primitive, this derivation classifies `A1` before varying it. Unlike D023, it
introduces no independent reference connection or orbit. Unlike a curvature or
transgression search, it excludes `R`, `DE`, and `DK` and therefore tests only
the lowest covariant-derivative momentum-matching class.

Prove exactly, at classical order and without perturbative expansion, that

## Derivation

### 1. Exhaustion of the invariant contraction space

#### 1.1 Reduction of arbitrary powers of `V`

Any invariant tensor polynomial generated by `eta` and `epsilon` is a sum of
metric pairings and at most one epsilon contraction; two epsilons reduce to a
determinant of metrics. Every pair of zero-form `V`s contracted by `eta` is
`V^2=1`. Contracting two `V`s into one epsilon vanishes by symmetry. Contracting
`V` into a one-form vector produces either `V.E` or `V.K=0`.

For a transparent exhaustive count, use `SO(1,4)` covariance to choose an
adapted internal frame `V=e_4` at one point. This is only a counting gauge. Set

\[
 q:=V_AE^A=E^4,\qquad e^a:=h^a{}_AE^A,\qquad k^a:=K^a,
 \quad a=0,1,2,3.                                               \tag{5}
\]

The residual invariant tensors are `eta_ab` and `epsilon_abcd`. A relation or
missing invariant in this frame would be covariant, so the stabilizer count is
equivalent to the original one.

#### 1.2 Zero-`K` sector

There are four one-form slots, all supplied by `E`.

- **Eta-only contractions.** Every metric pairing of two identical
  vector-valued one-forms is
  `eta_AB E^A wedge E^B=0`: the metric is symmetric and the wedge is
  antisymmetric. Pairing four `E`s with two metrics therefore vanishes.
- **Terms containing `V.E`.** In adapted form these contain `q`. Two `q`s
  wedge to zero. With one `q`, three Lorentz vectors `e^a` remain, but
  `SO(1,3)` has no invariant rank-three tensor. Thus all such candidates vanish.
- **Epsilon contractions.** Four `E`s need one `V` to fill the five-index
  epsilon. This gives exactly `I0`. Further `V`s either make powers of `V^2`
  or vanish in the epsilon.

Hence

\[
 \boxed{\dim A_1^{(0K)}=1,\qquad A_1^{(0K)}=\operatorname{span}\{I_0\}.} \tag{6}
\]

#### 1.3 One-`K` sector

Now the four one-form slots are one `K` and three `E`s.

- **Eta-only contractions.** A representative pairing is
  `(K_A wedge E^A) wedge (E_B wedge E^B)`, and the second factor is zero.
  Every other pairing is the same statement after relabeling.
- **Terms containing `V.E`.** One `q` leaves `k^a` and two `e^a`, an odd
  number of Lorentz-vector indices and therefore no invariant scalar. Two `q`s
  vanish. Explicit candidates such as
  `(K.E) wedge (V.E) wedge (V.E)` or
  `(K.E) wedge(E.E)` consequently vanish.
- **Epsilon contractions.** `K,E,E,E` supply four internal indices. Exactly one
  `V` fills the epsilon and gives `I1`; another `V` in the epsilon makes it
  zero, while contracted pairs again reduce to `V^2=1`.

Therefore

\[
 \boxed{\dim A_1^{(1K)}=1,\qquad A_1^{(1K)}=\operatorname{span}\{I_1\}.} \tag{7}
\]

The two basis elements are independent: at `K=0` with four independent
projected coframes, `I0` can be nonzero while `I1=0`; on the rank-three polar
trace below, `I0=0` while `I1` is nonzero for `c!=0`. Thus

\[
 \boxed{\dim A_1=2.}                                           \tag{8}
\]

#### 1.4 Algebraic independence and the differential-complex boundary

The dimension statements (6)--(8) use only the algebraic quotient declared in
Assumption 4. In that quotient there is no algebraic relation between `I0` and
`I1`: they have different `K` degree, and the two explicit evaluations following
(7) prove their linear independence.

No quotient by exact terms or integration by parts is part of this theorem.
Indeed, `A1` is not closed under `D`. For example, the admissible invariant
three-form `(K.E) wedge(V.E)` belongs to a larger jet algebra, but its covariant
derivative generates `DK`, `DE`, and `K wedge K` terms excluded from `A1`.
Moreover, on `Y` with retained `partial Y`, a total derivative changes the
corner action unless a corner equivalence is separately declared. Therefore a
comparison modulo exact forms would require (i) a larger explicitly specified
differential jet complex containing the relevant derivatives and (ii) an
explicit boundary/corner equivalence. Neither is defined or used here, and no
claim about the dimension of such a differential quotient is made.

This completes the purely algebraic class exhaustion; it is not a guess of
representatives and not a cohomology computation.

### 3. Complete independent variation before adapted gauge

The most general `A1` action, including the unit constraint, is

\[
 B_{A1}=\int_Y(\lambda I_0+\gamma I_1)
       +\int_Y\Lambda(V^2-1).                                 \tag{11}
\]

The exact zero-`K` variation is

\[
 \delta I_0=\delta V^A U_A+\delta E^C\wedge W_C,              \tag{12}
\]
\[
 U_A={1\over24}\epsilon_{ABCDE}E^B\wedge E^C\wedge E^D\wedge E^E,
 \qquad
 W_C={1\over6}\epsilon_{ACDEF}V^A E^D\wedge E^E\wedge E^F.  \tag{13}
\]

For the one-`K` term, use the exact identity

\[
 \delta K^A=D\delta V^A+\delta\omega^A{}_BV^B.                \tag{14}
\]

Direct Leibniz variation, followed only by covariant integration by parts of
`D delta V`, gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \delta I_1=\int_Y\big[&-\delta\omega^{AB}\wedge\Pi^\perp_{AB}
       +\delta E^C\wedge J_C+\delta V^A H_A\big]
       -2\int_{\partial Y}\delta V^AV^B\Pi_{AB},              \tag{15}\\
 \Pi^\parallel_{AB}:={}&h_A{}^Ch_B{}^D\Pi_{CD},\qquad
 \Pi^\perp:=\Pi-\Pi^\parallel,                              \tag{16}\\
 J_C={}&{1\over4}\epsilon_{ABCDE}C^{AB}\wedge E^D\wedge E^E
      =-{1\over2}\epsilon_{ABCDE}V^AK^B\wedge E^D\wedge E^E,\tag{17}\\
 H_A={}&2\left[V^BD\Pi_{AB}+2K^B\wedge\Pi_{AB}\right].       \tag{18}
\end{aligned}
\]

For sign audit, the primitive coefficient of `D delta V^A` is
`-2 V^B Pi_AB`; the identity
`D(delta V^A V^B Pi_AB)=D delta V^A wedge V^B Pi_AB
+delta V^A D(V^B Pi_AB)` produces the minus corner in (15). Moving
`delta E` past the one-form `C` produces the sign in (17). Equations
(15)--(18) are D024's dependent variation with `kappa_5` stripped, not an
independent-reference variation.

Combining (12)--(18) with the multiplier gives the complete interface
variation

\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
 \delta B_{A1}=\int_Y\big[&-\gamma\,\delta\omega^{AB}\wedge\Pi^\perp_{AB}
 +\delta E^C\wedge(\lambda W_C+\gamma J_C)\\
 &+\delta V^A(\lambda U_A+\gamma H_A+2\Lambda V_A)
 +(V^2-1)\delta\Lambda\big]\\
 &-2\gamma\int_{\partial Y}\delta V^AV^B\Pi_{AB}.
\end{aligned}}                                                \tag{19}
\]

No field was gauge-fixed in deriving (19). On the frozen complete D005 shell,
all projected-field chain-rule terms remain proportional to original D005
Euler coefficients and therefore vanish, exactly as in D024; this is an
on-shell background statement, not permission to omit them off shell.

The standalone invariant functional obeys the exact graded Noether identity

\[
 \boxed{D{\cal B}_\omega-{\cal B}_E\diamond E
              +{\cal B}_V\diamond V=0,}                       \tag{20}
\]

where the variation is ordered as
`delta omega wedge B_omega+delta E wedge B_E+delta V B_V`.
The minus sign is compulsory because `B_E` is a three-form. The multiplier
adds zero to the Lorentz identity by antisymmetry. This is the corrected D024
identity guarded by canonical O014, not the historical all-plus expression.

\[

## Limiting-case checks

1. **Rank three:** (23) is nonzero, so the mixed row is real. Common triad
   rescaling preserves rank for infinitesimal bounded `alpha`, making (28) a
   legitimate tangent rather than a degeneration test.
2. **Rank at most two:** every triple momentum vanishes and the mixed row and
   `I1` vanish. This is the known degenerate escape, not the target background.
3. **`c=0`:** (28) vanishes, but this lies outside the stipulated finite-slope
   branch and does not construct its flat positive-radius core.
4. **`K=0`:** `I1` vanishes as an action density, but under complete independent
   variation its connection derivative is still `-Pi^perp`; a function can
   vanish at a point without its derivative vanishing there. Only tangents

   Palatini tangent class. This is the correct complete-variation limit.
5. **Fixed coframe:** with `delta E=0`, `gamma=kappa_5^-1` cancels the mixed row.

6. **Zero-`K` control:** `I0` cannot affect momentum matching or common triad
   rescaling, but its transverse coframe equation enforces (31). This separates
   “zero background value” from “zero first variation.”
7. **Conservation:** no spacetime conservation law is added beyond gauge
   Noether identity (20). The frozen punctured bulks remain flat and satisfy
   their Bianchi identities; the interface result does not modify them.
