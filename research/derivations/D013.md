# D013: Reference-orbit and seed-frame gauge architecture

## Question addressed

Within the fixed-interface planar basic persistent `SO(1,3)` sector of
`D005`/`D006`, what group-action and boundary-polarization declarations are
minimally required before C016's Stueckelberg rank-ten block can be called
gauge-null, charged, or physical?

## Branch charter

Make

\[
 a=u a_0u^{-1}-du\,u^{-1}                                      \tag{1}
\]

coherent at the local canonical level by separating the exterior left action
from seed-frame right relabeling, finding their stabilizers and moment maps,

## Target claim

**Target.** Determine the extra declarations needed to classify the exact
rank-ten shell block

\[
 \Theta_C^{\rm St}=\int_C\langle Q_C,g\,\delta u\,u^{-1}g^{-1}\rangle,
 \qquad \operatorname{rank}\delta\Theta_C^{\rm St}=10,           \tag{2}
\]

found in `D006`, without assuming the missing total Gauss generator.

**Derived result preview.** A fixed seed permits the full exterior left action

equivalence relation explicitly transform `a0`. That right quotient removes

closure of its rank-five nonstabilizer part, and zero allowed corner charge.
None of those declarations follows from covariance of a family.

All results below are exact in the frozen regime. There is no perturbative
truncation beyond restriction to small local Lorentz transformations.

## Derivation

The exact derivation is organized by the following group-action, canonical,

## Bundle/group-action conventions

Let `P_0`, `P_-`, and `P_+` denote respectively a local seed bundle, exterior
persistent bundle, and core persistent bundle. Locally, `u:P_0->P_-` and
`g:P_-->P_+` are represented by `G`-valued maps. Thus

\[
 v:=gu:P_0\longrightarrow P_+ .                                  \tag{5}
\]

There are two commuting actions:

\[
 L_{h_-}:u\mapsto h_-u,
 \qquad
 R_r:(u,a_0)\mapsto (ur^{-1},r\cdot a_0).                         \tag{6}
\]

The first changes the exterior frame. The second relabels the seed frame. A
core transformation additionally sends `g->h_+g`; the exterior action sends
`g->g h_-^{-1}`. The left and right operations commute because
`(h_-u)r^{-1}=h_-(ur^{-1})`, while `h_-` does not act on `a0` and `r` does not
act on exterior fields.

## Transformations of `u,a0,a,g,v` and composition checks

Define the dressing map `F(u,a0)=u·a0`. Direct substitution gives the exact
identities

\[
 F(h_-u,a_0)=h_-\cdot F(u,a_0),                                  \tag{7}
\]

\[
 F(ur^{-1},r\cdot a_0)=F(u,a_0),                                 \tag{8}
\]

and

\[
 a\mapsto h_-\cdot a,
 \quad g\mapsto h_+g h_-^{-1},
 \quad v\mapsto h_+v r^{-1}.                                    \tag{9}
\]

Equation (8), not covariance, is the candidate *right parametrization
redundancy*. It is available only when both entries in (6) lie in the admitted
field space. Composition is exact:

\[
 R_{r_1}R_{r_2}=R_{r_1r_2},\qquad
 L_{h_1}L_{h_2}=L_{h_1h_2},\qquad [L_h,R_r]=0.                    \tag{10}
\]

For a fixed seed representative `a0`, the admissible right subgroup is only

\[
 \operatorname{Stab}(a_0)
 :=\{r\mid r\cdot a_0=a_0\}.                                     \tag{11}
\]

Infinitesimally it is `D_(a0) beta=0`, supplemented by the declared boundary
condition on `beta`. Generic local `r` is therefore not a symmetry of the
fixed-seed problem.

## Left/right Maurer--Cartan variables

Use right-trivialized field-space forms

\[
 \xi:=\delta g\,g^{-1},\qquad
 \zeta:=\delta u\,u^{-1},\qquad
 \chi:=\delta v\,v^{-1}=\xi+g\zeta g^{-1}.                       \tag{12}
\]

They satisfy `delta xi=(1/2)[xi wedge xi]`, and similarly for `zeta,chi`.
For independent variations of both factors in (1), exact differentiation
gives

\[
 \boxed{\delta a=u\,\delta a_0u^{-1}-D_a\zeta.}                  \tag{13}
\]

For infinitesimal left parameter `alpha_-` and right seed parameter `beta`,

\[
 \zeta=\alpha_- -\operatorname{Ad}_u\beta,
 \qquad \delta a_0=-D_{a_0}\beta .                              \tag{14}
\]

The identity

\[
 D_a(\operatorname{Ad}_u\beta)=\operatorname{Ad}_uD_{a_0}\beta \tag{15}
\]

then proves from (13) that the right part of (14) has `delta a=0`, whereas the
left part gives `delta a=-D_a alpha_-`. This checks both signs.

## Candidate seed-frame symmetries and stabilizers

Four notions must not be conflated.

1. **Fixed seed, no right declaration.** `a0` is fixed coefficientwise.
   `u->h_-u` realizes exterior covariance. Only (11) can act on the right.
   A general `u->ur^-1` changes `a` and is a physical/reference-orbit
   displacement unless another declaration says otherwise.
2. **Fixed seed modulo its stabilizer.** Quotienting by (11) removes only
   nonunique representatives of the same `a`. For a generic irreducible
   connection this local stabilizer can be discrete/trivial after boundary
   conditions; for `a0=0` on a contractible chart it contains constant Lorentz
   transformations. No universal stabilizer dimension is asserted.
3. **Seed gauge orbit.** Admit `a0=s·a_*` and quotient
   `(u,s)->(ur^-1,rs)`. Then
   \[
   a=(us)\cdot a_*                                                \tag{16}
   \]
   depends on `U=us`, while the right action removes only the redundant
   factorization. The orbit variable `U` remains.
4. **Unrestricted dynamical seed.** If every component of `a0` is varied
   independently, (13) inserts `u delta a0 u^-1` into the D005 variation and
   yields `Ad_(u^-1 g^-1)Q=0`. Since adjoint maps are invertible, this is
   `Q=0`, incompatible with the frozen rank-three shell. This is not a gauge
   completion.

## Canonical potentials and moment maps

### 1. Fixed geometrically transforming reference

For fixed `a`, `D006` gives

\[
 \Theta_C^{\rm fix}=\int_C\langle\Pi_C,\xi\rangle,
 \qquad \Pi=Q+\lambda .                                          \tag{17}
\]

Its interface moment maps are

\[
 J_+^{\rm fix}=\Pi_C,
 \qquad J_-^{\rm fix}=-\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\Pi_C.         \tag{18}
\]

Both vanish on (3). Nevertheless, a transformation with
`delta a=-D_a alpha_- !=0` changes fixed external data. In one fixed-`a` phase
space only `D_a alpha_-=0` is tangent, unless an equivalence between different
background-data sectors is separately declared.

### 2. Fixed seed with Stueckelberg `u`

For `delta a0=0`, (13) and the D005 integration by parts yield exactly

\[
 \Theta_C^{\rm seed}
 =\int_C[\langle\Pi_C,\xi\rangle
          +\langle Q_C,g\zeta g^{-1}\rangle]
 =\int_C[\langle\lambda_C,\xi\rangle+\langle Q_C,\chi\rangle].  \tag{19}
\]

Contraction with the admitted left core/exterior action gives

\[
 J_+^{I}=\Pi_C,
 \qquad J_-^{I}=-\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}\lambda_C.            \tag{20}
\]

On (3),

\[
 J_+^I=0,
 \qquad J_-^I=\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}Q_C=P^-_C\ne0.           \tag{21}
\]

The last equality uses the shell matching and is exact. The fixed-seed right
stabilizer has candidate contraction

\[
 J_{0}^{I}[\beta]
 =-\int_C\langle\operatorname{Ad}_{v^{-1}}Q_C,\beta\rangle,
 \qquad D_{a_0}\beta=0,                                         \tag{22}
\]

up to the common sign convention for Hamiltonians. Thus even a stabilizer
transformation is not automatically gauge-null when allowed at the temporal
corner. It is null only after its total generator and corner condition are
specified.

### 3. Seed-orbit right action

When `a0` is varied only as `delta a0=-D_(a0) beta` simultaneously with
`zeta=-Ad_u beta`, equations (13)--(15) give `delta a=0` before integration by
parts. Hence the *unintegrated* reference action is exactly invariant and the
right direction is a kernel of the map `(u,a0)->a`. If one integrates the two
terms separately, the apparent endpoint term (22) is cancelled by the endpoint
term generated when `-<Ad_(gu) delta a0,Q>` is integrated with
`delta a0=-D_(a0)beta`; the bulk pieces cancel by (15). Dropping either member
would manufacture a charge.

This cancellation establishes only a redundancy of the factorization. In the
explicit orbit parametrization (16), the reduced potential is again the
Stueckelberg potential for `U=us`. Therefore the rank-ten block belongs to the
remaining orbit variable `U`, not to the redundant split between `u` and `s`.

### 4. Fully dynamical reference

For unrestricted `delta a0`, the reference contribution to the variation is

\[
 -\int_\Sigma
 \langle\operatorname{Ad}_{(gu)^{-1}}Q,\delta a_0\rangle .       \tag{23}
\]

Its Euler equation is `Q=0`. Because the planar rank-three shell has
`Q_(03,C)=(b/2)dx^1 wedge dx^2 !=0`, this theory has no frozen target branch.

## Constraint relations on the D005 shell

The algebraic shell (3) reduces (19) to

\[
 \Theta_{C,\rm shell}^{\rm seed}
 =\int_C\langle Q_C,g\zeta g^{-1}\rangle,                        \tag{24}
\]

whose two-form has rank ten by the exact D006 calculation. The orbit equation

\[
 D_{\bar a}Q=0,                                                   \tag{25}
\]

an evolution equation, not an algebraic cut constraint annihilating (24).
`H(K)=0` constrains a connection difference with no interface kinetic term and
does not change (24).

The minimum total exterior generator must have the form

\[
 {\cal G}_-[\alpha_-]
 =G_-^{\rm bulk}[\alpha_-]
  +\int_C\langle J_-^I,\alpha_-\rangle
  +{\cal Q}_{\rm corner}[\alpha_-],                              \tag{26}
\]

where `G_-^bulk` includes its differentiability boundary term. With the D005
incident sign, cancellation on (3) requires its boundary moment map to be

\[
 J_-^{\rm bulk}|_C=-P^-_C,
 \qquad J_-^I=P^-_C.                                             \tag{27}
\]

Equation (27) is the necessary matching of known boundary coefficients, not a
claim that the omitted bulk generator has been derived or closes. The core
generator analogously includes `J_+^I=Pi_C=0` on shell, but its bulk Gauss
density and any outer-boundary term must still be retained.

## Explicit countermodel: identical `a`, different canonical quotients

Take a contractible chart, `a_*=0`, and any small nonconstant `r(x)`. Consider

\[
 (u_1,a_{01})=(1,0),\qquad
 (u_2,a_{02})=(r^{-1},r\cdot0)
              =(r^{-1},-dr\,r^{-1}).                             \tag{28}
\]

By (8), both give the identical connection `a=0`.

* In phase space `P_R`, declare the full right relation (6). The two pairs are
  one point of the canonical configuration quotient.
* In phase space `P_N`, admit the same seed-orbit pairs but do **not** declare
  (6) to be gauge. They are distinct points (or unresolved null-labelled
  sectors) even though every functional of `a` agrees.

Thus identical `a` does not determine the quotient. `P_R` and `P_N` differ by
an explicit equivalence declaration, not by an equation of motion. Conversely,
quotienting (28) still leaves `U=us` in (16), so this countermodel cannot be
used to erase the rank-ten `U` block.

## Exact conditions under which `u` is gauge-null versus charged

Let `X_alpha` be the exterior Lorentz vector field acting on all bulk,
interface, and reference variables. It is gauge-null only if **all** of the
following hold:

1. **Admissibility:** `X_alpha` is tangent to one declared phase space; it
   preserves fixed data, or changing those data is explicitly quotiented.
2. **Complete polarization:**
   `Theta_tot=Theta_+^bulk+Theta_-^bulk+Theta_I+Theta_corner` is fixed, including
   seed terms. No endpoint term is selectively dropped.
3. **Differentiability and moment-map cancellation:** the total generator
   (26) exists and its shell moment map vanishes, requiring at least (27).
4. **First-class regularity:** the relevant total Gauss constraints close and
   have constant rank. On the planar block, the nonstabilizer action must have
   rank five to pair with the five independent `dQ` directions; dimension
   counting alone does not prove this.
5. **Corner condition:** either `alpha|_(partial C)=0` and its temporal-corner
   value is excluded, or an allowed counterterm makes the total corner charge
   vanish. A nonzero allowed value of
   \[
   {\cal Q}_C[\alpha_-]
   =\int_C\langle\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}Q_C,\alpha_-\rangle   \tag{29}
   \]
   before bulk cancellation is a candidate charge, not proof of a surviving
   physical charge.
6. **Quotient:** solutions are explicitly identified along the resulting null
   orbits.

If conditions 1--3 fail, `u` is not established as gauge. If the differentiable
total generator has a nonzero boundary value for an admitted parameter, that
transformation is charged rather than gauge-null under that polarization. If
the total two-form remains nondegenerate on the rank-ten block after all
first-class constraints and boundary conditions, it is physical. If constraints
pair nondegenerately without generating a symmetry, it is second class. The
present sources do not select among these last outcomes.

## Global caveats without overclaim

The local seed stabilizer is the covariantly constant automorphism group of
`a0`; globally it depends on holonomy, bundle topology, boundary conditions,
and reducibility. A global `u` or `r` need not exist. Large transformations can
split sectors or change charge quantization. None of those effects is inferred
from this local chart. The right quotient may also have fixed points, so its
global reduced space can be stratified. No statement here covers those strata.

\[
 u\mapsto h_-u r^{-1},\qquad a_0\mapsto r\cdot a_0,
 \qquad g\mapsto h_+g h_-^{-1},                                  \tag{31}
